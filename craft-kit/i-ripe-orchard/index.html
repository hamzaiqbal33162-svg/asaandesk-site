Fresh cut

Cheek, seed, cheek. That is the whole secret.

A ripe one gives a little under your thumb and smells sweet at the stem. Cut either side of the flat seed and you get two golden cheeks.

  1. Stand the mango on its end, stem up.
  2. Slice down each side, just missing the seed.
  3. Score a grid, push the skin up and enjoy the cubes.
