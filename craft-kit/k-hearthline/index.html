Inside the workshop

Open the Wren Cabinet.

Keep scrolling and the doors swing open. Four slow steps go into every piece we make.

  1. 01

    Kiln-dried sheesham

    Boards rest for six months, then dry slowly so they never warp in a humid monsoon.

  2. 02

    Hand-cut joints

    Dovetails and mortises, no metal brackets, glued and pinned by hand.

  3. 03

    Seven coats of oil

    Rubbed in over a week, so the grain glows and spilt chai beads off.

  4. 04

    Solid brass hinges

    Cast and polished by hand, made to swing for decades.
