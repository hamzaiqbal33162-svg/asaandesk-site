For artists

How selling works.

Four steps, one flat fee, no exclusivity. Keep selling at fairs and from your studio too.

i.

Open your studio page

Add a short bio and up to twelve works. It takes about twenty minutes.

ii.

We visit and photograph

True-colour photos and exact sizes, so collectors know what arrives.

iii.

Collectors make offers

Accept, counter or decline from your phone. You set the lowest price you take.

iv.

We crate, ship and pay

Our couriers collect from your studio. You are paid within seven days.
