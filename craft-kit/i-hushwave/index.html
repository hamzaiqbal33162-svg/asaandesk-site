Features

Made for long, quiet days.

Everything you need for a commute, a flight or a deep-work afternoon, and nothing that gets in the way.

50 hours, honestly

Measured with noise cancelling off at a sensible volume. With it on, still 40.

Six listening mics

Three per cup track the room and your voice, so calls stay clear on a windy street.

Folds flat

Cups turn and lie flat, so it slides into a laptop sleeve or a slim case.

Sound that surrounds

Spatial mode keeps the music in front of you when you turn your head.

Two devices at once

Stay paired to your phone and laptop, and switch when a call comes in.
