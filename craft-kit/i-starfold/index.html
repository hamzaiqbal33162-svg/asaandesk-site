    About the gallery

    A dark room on purpose.

    Starfold began as a rooftop club of six friends with one second-hand telescope. Today we show artists who paint, print and build with light, and we keep the rooms dim so your eyes adjust the way they do outside at night.

    Every Friday the roof opens for an hour of real sky. Bring a shawl.
