Seven days, one old jeep, far too much chai and the most beautiful road we have ever driven.

Up the Karakoram Highway

Day 1 · Islamabad to Chilas

470 km · 12 hours · tea stops: 4

We left before the call to morning prayer with the jeep packed to the roof. By noon the green hills of Hazara had turned into steep grey walls, and the Indus was a loud brown ribbon far below the road.

The Indus gorge near Dasu, late afternoon.

Day 3 · Fairy Meadows

Jeep track, then a 3 hour walk · 3,300 m

The jeep track to Tato is not for the nervous. After it, a slow walk up through pines brought us to a meadow where Nanga Parbat filled half the sky, pink at sunset and silver by moonlight.

Nanga Parbat from the meadow, 6 am.

Day 5 · Hunza and Attabad Lake

Karimabad · apricot season

Karimabad smelled of apricots drying on every roof. The next morning we rowed out on Attabad Lake, a startling turquoise that looks painted on, with the Passu cones standing like a row of teeth behind it.
