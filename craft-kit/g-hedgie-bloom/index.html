How the card works

1. Open it

Press the card. It lifts a little, then the cover swings open on a springy hinge and the hedgehog pops up, a bit shy.

2. Take the tulip

It brings a tulip out from behind its back. Drag the flower into the jar, or press Take the tulip.

3. Watch it bloom

The bouquet grows in the jar, the hedgehog hops for joy, petals fall and the birthday wish writes itself.
