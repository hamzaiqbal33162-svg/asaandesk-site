    from pod to bar

    Three slow steps, done by hand

    We buy beans from two small farming co-operatives, roast them in our Lahore kitchen and temper every batch on a marble slab.

    i.

    Bean

    Ripe pods are opened by hand, the beans ferment under banana leaves for six days, then dry in the sun.

    ii.

    Roast

    Small batches turn in a drum roaster for 22 minutes. We taste every batch before it moves on.

    iii.

    Temper

    Melted chocolate is cooled and folded on cold marble until it shines and snaps. Then it rests for two days.
