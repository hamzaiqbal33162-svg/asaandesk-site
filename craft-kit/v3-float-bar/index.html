A night on the food street

Draft by Mahnoor Sheikh · Lahore · 4 min read

By nine the lane is loud and golden. Vendors wake their coals and the smell of roasting corn drifts over everything, sweet and a little smoky.

Halfway down, a man selling kites has strung them above his stall like a second sky: paper diamonds in pink, green and gold, each one tied with a careful knot.

Past the fruit carts, a small shop glows with brass lamps of every size. The owner polishes one while he talks, and the light moves across the ceiling like water.

I buy a cup of tea, find a step to sit on and try to write it all down before the night moves on without me.
