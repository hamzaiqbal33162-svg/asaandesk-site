From furnace to filament

Four steps, two days, one glow

Our glass travels from a 1,140°C furnace to your ceiling in four slow steps. Scroll to follow the heat.

  2. 01

    Gather

    A glowing gather of sand, soda and lime is lifted from the furnace on a long steel pipe.

  3. 02

    Breathe

    One steady breath opens the bubble. The maker turns the pipe so the wall stays even.

  4. 03

    Shape

    Wet wooden paddles and a pear-wood mould give each lamp its globe, cone, bell or drop.

  5. 04

    Rest

    Forty-eight hours in a cooling kiln make the glass strong. Then we fit brass, cord and a warm filament.
