Story Cube

Tap a ring and the story bursts out of it. Every friend sits on a side of a cube you can turn with your finger.

  • Tap right for next, left for back
  • Hold to pause and hide the bars
  • Swipe sideways to turn the cube
  • Swipe down to close into the ring
  • Keys arrows, Space and Esc work too
