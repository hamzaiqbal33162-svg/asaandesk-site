Pull Shade

A notification shade that slides down with your finger. The room blurs behind it, tiles and cards tumble in one by one, and every card can be flicked away.

  • Pull down from the top edge
  • Tap the message stack to fan it out
  • Swipe a card sideways to dismiss
  • Push up or drag the handle to close
  • Delete on a card, Esc to close
