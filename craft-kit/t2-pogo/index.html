When is the best month to visit Hunza?

Asked by Omer F. · 214 answers

AKAreeba KhanGuide in Gilgit · 9 years

Late April for apricot blossom, or the second half of October for golden poplars. The roads are open, the days are crisp and most guest houses drop their prices.
