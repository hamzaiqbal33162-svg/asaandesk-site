01 · Studio

We are a small studio in Lahore that designs homes, clinics and reading rooms around one stubborn idea: a building should know *where *the *sun *is. We study the light before the walls, so mornings are bright, afternoons stay cool and every evening ends with a glow.

Sun before walls

We model the sun path for your exact plot, then place bedrooms, kitchens and verandas where the light suits them.

Honest materials

Local brick, lime plaster and timber. Screens and courtyards do the cooling, so machines only help.

Evenings by design

Lighting plans are part of the first sketch, not the last week. Every room gets a warm, low glow for the night.

04 · Process

From first sunrise to handover

Four clear stages, one team from start to finish, and a fixed fee agreed before we draw a line.

  2. 01

    Site and sun

    We walk your plot at dawn and at dusk, measure the trees and neighbours, and map the sun for all twelve months.

    2 weeks
  3. 02

    Sketch and model

    Hand sketches and a light model you can hold up to a lamp. We test three layouts before choosing one together.

    4 to 6 weeks
  4. 03

    Drawings and approvals

    Full working drawings, structure and services, plus the paperwork for your development authority.

    6 to 8 weeks
  5. 04

    Build and hand over

    Weekly site visits, a shared photo diary and a final walk at sunset, with the keys and a manual for the house.

    10 to 16 months
