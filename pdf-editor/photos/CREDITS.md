# Photo library credits

Every photo in this folder comes from Wikimedia Commons and is either dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) or is in the public domain (the Commons licence template is listed for each photo).
No attribution is legally required, but we credit the photographers below as a courtesy. AsaanDesk does not claim ownership of these photos.

The files were resized to 1600 px on the long side (plus a 360 px thumbnail), re-encoded as JPEG and stripped of metadata; some detailed images were very lightly softened to keep the files small.
Photos were picked to avoid identifiable people, logos, brand names and watermarks. Licences were checked against the Commons file pages on 2026-11-10 (photos 1-44) and 2026-11-12 (photos 45-129).
