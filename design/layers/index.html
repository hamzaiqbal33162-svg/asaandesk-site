Magic Layers

Turn a flat picture or PDF into layers you can edit

Text becomes real text, the main subject is cut out, pictures come apart and the background is cleaned up. It all happens in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

What to split out

The first time, the text reader and the picture tools download once (up to about 50 MB) and then work offline. Big pictures are worked on at up to 3000 px on the long side; PDFs: the first 30 pages. Flat designs (posters, flyers, slides) split cleanly; busy photos may separate less cleanly, so check the layers and fix them by hand.

    Layers

      Untick a layer to leave it out. Everything that is ticked opens in Studio as a design you can edit.
