Good to know

What are the limits?

Animation and transparency are lost in JPG.

Input up to 15 MB · 20 megapixels is supported.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.

How do I open a WebP image on my PC or phone?

Convert it to JPG here. A JPG opens in every photo viewer on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone, and in older software.

Can I keep the quality high?

Yes: set the quality slider, up to 100%. Animation is lost in a JPG, and a transparent background becomes white or black.
