How to use AI Image Watermark Remover

  1. Drop Gemini pictures here, paste one with Ctrl+V, or choose them (up to 30).
  2. Press Remove the mark. Each picture is checked; only a clear match is changed.
  3. Compare before and after with the slider and download the PNG files or one ZIP.

What does this tool do?

The size of the picture tells where Gemini put its sparkle (a 48 or 96 pixel mark at a set distance from the corner). The corner is checked against the calibrated shape of the mark, and only when it clearly matches is the mark taken out by reverse alpha blending: the exact opposite of how it was blended in. If the evidence is weak, the picture is left exactly as it was.

Good to know

What are the limits?

It removes only the visible Gemini sparkle drawn in the corner. It does not remove SynthID (the invisible watermark Google adds to the pixels), Content Credentials (C2PA), or anyone else's logo or ownership mark, and the result is still AI-made. Pictures that were resized, cropped or re-compressed after Gemini made them may not match and are then left unchanged.

Input up to 30 pictures · 300 MB in total is supported.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.

Why was my picture not changed?

The tool only changes a picture when the corner clearly holds the Gemini sparkle at a place Gemini uses for that picture size. Pictures that were resized, cropped, screenshotted or sent through a chat app often no longer match, and then nothing is changed. The guide "When the mark is not removed" on the AI Watermark Remover page lists the usual causes.

Does it remove SynthID or the "Made with AI" information?

No. SynthID is hidden in all the pixels and is not touched, and Content Credentials or other metadata stay too. The picture is still AI-made, and the rules for labelling AI content still apply; read Responsible use on the AI Watermark Remover page.

Does it lower the quality?

Only the corner with the mark is changed; every other pixel is copied unchanged. With PNG output (the default) nothing is compressed again. Choose "Same type as the picture" to keep a JPG or WebP as JPG or WebP (quality 95).

Can it remove other watermarks or logos?

No. It knows only the shape of the Gemini sparkle. For your own pictures with another mark, the Remove Watermark from Photo tool fills a marked area instead.
