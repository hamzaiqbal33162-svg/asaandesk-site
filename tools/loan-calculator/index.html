How to use Loan / EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the tenure.
  2. Choose years or months and press Calculate.
  3. See the EMI, total interest and total payment; open the schedule or download it as CSV.

What does this tool do?

Enter the amount, the yearly interest rate and the tenure in months or years. EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months; at 0% it is the amount ÷ n. A month-by-month schedule shows the principal, interest and balance.

ExamplePKR 1,000,000 at 12% for 5 years → EMI 22,244.45

Good to know

What are the limits?

The result is an estimate. Banks may add fees or insurance and round differently, so the real installment can differ.

Input up to 50 years (600 months) is supported.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.

Can I use it for a car, bike or house loan?

Yes, for any loan with a fixed rate and equal monthly installments: enter the amount after your down payment, the yearly rate the bank quotes and the tenure. Banks may add insurance or fees, and many rates are floating (KIBOR plus a margin), so the bank's final installment can differ.

What is the EMI formula?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r the monthly rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the number of months.
