How to make an animation

  1. 1

    Open the Magic Animate editor

    Select "Animate a design". The editor opens full screen in your browser, with no sign-up.

  2. 2

    Pick a template or start blank

    Choose one of our animation templates, or begin with an empty canvas.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    Choose fade, rise, pop, slide or typewriter for each element.

  4. 4

    Download or share

    Save it as WebM video and GIF. Your work also stays saved on this device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download the animation?

Yes, as a WebM video or an animated GIF.

Is it free to use?

Yes. It is free to use on AsaanDesk and needs no account or sign-up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser and your work stays on this device.

Which formats can I download?

You can download WebM video and GIF.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; bigger projects are easier on a tablet or computer.

Where is my work saved?

In this browser on this device. Download a copy to keep it safe or to move it to another device.
