How to use Conclusion Writer

  1. Paste your text in the box.
  2. Choose the options.
  3. Press the button, then copy or download the result.

What does this tool do?

AI restates the main argument, sums up the key points and ends with a closing thought, without adding new facts.

ExampleYour essay → a closing paragraph

Good to know

What are the limits?

Needs Google Chrome 138 or newer on a computer, which has built-in AI; your text stays on your device. Phones and other browsers: coming soon. AI can make mistakes.

Input up to Up to 8,000 characters is supported.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Chrome's own AI writes the answer on your device; your text is not sent anywhere. In browsers without that AI the tool says so and does nothing.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.
