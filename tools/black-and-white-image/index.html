How to use Black and White Image

  1. Choose one or more pictures (several give a ZIP).
  2. Check the options.
  3. Press the button and download the result.

What does this tool do?

Grey uses the eye's brightness weights, so skin and sky look natural. Pure black and white (with a threshold) suits signatures and documents; dots (dithering) suit thermal and laser printers.

Good to know

What are the limits?

Input up to 50 MB in total · 20 pictures is supported.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.
