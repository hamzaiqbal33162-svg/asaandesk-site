Frequently asked questions

What size should my post be?

Instagram post 1080 × 1080 px (portrait 1080 × 1350), Instagram story and WhatsApp status 1080 × 1920, Facebook post 1200 × 630, LinkedIn post 1200 × 627, YouTube thumbnail 1280 × 720. Pick the size when you start, or change it later with Resize.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload it, or save it with your colours and fonts in the Brand kit so it is one click away in every post.

How do I keep the text easy to read?

Use a short, large headline, strong contrast with the background, and keep words away from the edges that apps crop.

Can I turn a post into a story?

Yes. Resize makes a 1080 × 1920 copy of your post (your original stays), then move things into place.

Is it free to use?

Yes. It is free to use on AsaanDesk and needs no account or sign-up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser and your work stays on this device.

Which formats can I download?

You can download PNG and JPG.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; bigger projects are easier on a tablet or computer.

Where is my work saved?

In this browser on this device. Download a copy to keep it safe or to move it to another device.
