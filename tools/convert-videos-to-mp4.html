Often, yes. Choose "Small" for the smallest file; the result depends on the video.

Yes. It is free to use on AsaanDesk and needs no account or sign-up.

No. It runs in your browser and your work stays on this device. MP4 export downloads the video engine the first time you use it; after that it runs from your browser cache.

No. It works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; bigger projects are easier on a tablet or computer.

There is no fixed limit; very large files depend on the memory and speed of your device.
