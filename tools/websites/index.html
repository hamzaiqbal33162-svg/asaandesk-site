Frequently asked questions

What is a website builder?

A tool that turns ready blocks into a website without writing code. Ours makes sites of one or more pages that you download as a ZIP.

How long does it take to build a website?

A simple one-page site takes a few minutes: pick a template, change the words and photos, download.

Do I get a domain or hosting?

No. You download index.html or a ZIP and upload it to any hosting you choose, with your own domain.

How should I plan my website?

Start with a home page that says what you offer, then pages or sections for services, prices, gallery and contact. Our templates follow that order.

Is my site secure (SSL)?

That depends on your host: most static hosts give you a free HTTPS certificate. The site is plain HTML with no server code.

Can I add my social media links?

Yes. Add links to your profiles as buttons or text links, for example in the contact block or footer.

Is it free to use?

Yes. It is free to use on AsaanDesk and needs no account or sign-up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser and your work stays on this device.

Which formats can I download?

You can download a ZIP with every page, ready for any web host.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; bigger projects are easier on a tablet or computer.

Where is my work saved?

In this browser on this device. Download a copy to keep it safe or to move it to another device.

Ready to make your website?

It opens in a second, right in your browser.
