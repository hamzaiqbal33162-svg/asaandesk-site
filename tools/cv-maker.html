Why a structured CV helps

Recruiters usually skim a CV in less than a minute, so a clear layout with name, job title, contact details, experience and education in predictable places matters more than decoration. Building the CV from fields rather than a free document also keeps spacing even and stops sections from drifting onto a second page.

How the fields are read

A few boxes use simple text patterns so the preview can arrange them for you:

SectionWhat to type
ExperienceFirst line: Role | Company | Years. Duties on the following lines. Leave one blank line between jobs.
EducationOne entry per line: Degree | Institution | Year
SkillsComma separated. Add a level such as "Excel 90%" or "Excel: 90"; without a level a gently falling default is used for the skill bar
AdditionalOne item per line, for example a driving licence

Templates, colour and photo

Three templates are available: Modern with a coloured sidebar, Classic with a centred heading, and Minimal with clean lines. One colour picker sets the accent. A photo is optional; a sample photo is shown at first, which you can replace with your own (under 3 MB) or remove. Many employers in some countries expect no photo, so follow local custom.

Saving as PDF

Press Print / Save PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in the print window. The page is set to A4 portrait with no browser margins, and only the CV paper is printed. Your entries are kept as a draft in this browser, so you can come back and update the CV later. Clear all removes the draft and restores the sample.

Common mistakes

Put the most recent job first. Start duty lines with action words and add numbers where you can, such as "Cut expenses by 12%". Use a professional email address. Keep skill levels honest, since an interviewer may test them. Read the preview once more for spelling, then save a fresh PDF for each application.

Good to know

What are the limits?

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.

Is the CV maker free with PDF download?

Yes. Fill in your details, pick a template, check the live preview and save it as a PDF, with no sign-up or payment. Your details stay in your browser.
