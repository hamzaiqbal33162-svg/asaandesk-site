How to use Speech to Text (Audio to Text)

  1. Choose a file from your device, drop it here or paste a file link.
  2. Check the options and press Convert.
  3. Download the finished file.

What does this tool do?

OpenAI's Whisper speech model runs inside your browser, so the recording never leaves your device. Choose the language, the accuracy and plain text or subtitles.

Examplevoice-note.ogg (2 min, Urdu) → voice-note.txt

Good to know

What are the limits?

The first run downloads the speech model (40–250 MB), later runs start at once. A computer is faster than a phone; a 10-minute recording takes a few minutes.

Input up to 200 MB · 30 minutes is supported.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The speech model is downloaded to your browser (from Hugging Face, once) and the recording is turned into text on your device. It is never uploaded.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.
