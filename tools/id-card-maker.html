How to use ID Card & Badge Maker

  1. Pick a template from a category or start with Blank Canvas.
  2. Change text, photo, barcode and shapes in the Card Designer, then Save.
  3. Get PNG/PDF from Download, or print everyone's cards from My Members.

What does this tool do?

75 ready designs in 12 categories (including US Cards) or a blank card. Add text, photos, signatures, logos, barcodes, QR codes and shapes; design the front and back. Add members (or upload a CSV) and print everyone's cards in one PDF. Everything happens in your browser.

ExampleEmployee badge → front + back PDF

Good to know

What are the limits?

Card size: CR80 (85.6 × 54 mm, the size of an ATM card) or Nametag (4 × 3 in). Designs and members are saved in this browser only. For real cards, use a PVC card printer or a print shop.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.
