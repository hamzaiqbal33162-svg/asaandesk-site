Frequently asked questions

Is there a presenter view with notes?

Yes. Present mode has a presenter view; write your notes per slide in the Notes panel at the bottom.

Can I record my presentation?

Open the Online video recorder with your slides on screen and record the screen with your camera bubble.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. Arrow keys and Space move through slides when presenting; the Help (?) button lists the editor's keyboard shortcuts.

Can I download PowerPoint (PPTX)?

Yes. Download a .pptx where text and simple shapes stay editable, or a PDF or images.

Can AI make a deck for me?

Not yet. A deck from a prompt is coming with AsaanDesk AI; start from one of our templates today.

Can I work offline?

Editing runs in your browser and decks stay on this device, so a weak connection is fine once the editor is open. Opening the editor needs a connection.

Is the presentation maker free?

Yes. Presentations is free to use with no sign-up.

Can I present directly from the browser?

Yes. Present mode shows your slides full screen; use arrow keys, a clicker or a tap to move on.

Which slide sizes are available?

Widescreen 16:9 and standard 4:3.

How do I download my slides?

Download the deck as a PDF or each slide as an image from the File or Share menu.

Is my presentation uploaded?

No. The editor runs in your browser and keeps your deck on this device.

Can I use my own brand colours?

Yes. Save colours, fonts and a logo in the brand kit and apply them to any slide.
