How to use the online whiteboard

  1. 1

    Open a board

    Select "Start a whiteboard". An endless canvas opens full screen.

  2. 2

    Pick a template

    Start from a brainstorm, mind map, kanban or flowchart, or begin blank.

  3. 3

    Add your ideas

    Drop sticky notes, shapes, arrows and text, or draw freely with the pen.

  4. 4

    Save and share

    Download the board as PNG, SVG or PDF, or keep it saved on this device.

Frequently asked questions

Is the online whiteboard free?

Yes. The whiteboard is free to use with no sign-up.

What can I add to a board?

Sticky notes, shapes, arrows, text and freehand drawing with the pen.

Can I export my board?

Yes. Download it as PNG, SVG or PDF.

Is my board uploaded?

No. The whiteboard runs in your browser and keeps your board on this device.

Does it work with a touch screen or pen?

Yes. Draw and move notes with touch or a stylus on tablets and phones.

Can I start from a template?

Yes. Open the Templates panel in the editor or pick one below.
