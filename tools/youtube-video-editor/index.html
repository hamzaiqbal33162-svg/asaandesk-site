Frequently asked questions

Can I make the thumbnail here too?

Yes. Design a 1280 × 720 thumbnail next to your video and download it as an image.

How do I add chapters?

Mark each chapter on the timeline, then copy the list with times into your YouTube description.

Is it free to use?

Yes. It is free to use on AsaanDesk and needs no account or sign-up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser and your work stays on this device. MP4 export downloads the video engine the first time you use it; after that it runs from your browser cache.

Which formats can I download?

You can download MP4 and WebM.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; bigger projects are easier on a tablet or computer.

Where is my work saved?

In this browser on this device. Download a copy to keep it safe or to move it to another device.
