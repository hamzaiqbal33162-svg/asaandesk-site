Frequently asked questions

Are the files ready for a print shop?

Yes. Downloads are 300 dpi with bleed and crop marks where the format needs them.

What is bleed?

A little extra background beyond the trim line (usually 3 mm or 1/8 in), so no white edge shows after cutting. It is added for you.

RGB or CMYK colour mode?

Files are saved in RGB. Most print shops convert to CMYK for you; very bright blues and greens may print a little duller.

Can I print at home?

Yes. Download the PDF and print on a home printer at 100% size; use thicker paper for cards.

Is it free to use?

Yes. It is free to use on AsaanDesk and needs no account or sign-up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser and your work stays on this device.

Which formats can I download?

You can download 300 dpi PDF and PNG.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; bigger projects are easier on a tablet or computer.

Where is my work saved?

In this browser on this device. Download a copy to keep it safe or to move it to another device.

Ready to make your print design?

It opens in a second, right in your browser.
