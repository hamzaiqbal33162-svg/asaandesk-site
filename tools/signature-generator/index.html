How to use Signature Generator

  1. Choose draw, name or photo.
  2. Set the colour and style.
  3. Download PNG / SVG or copy it.

What does this tool do?

Three ways: draw with a mouse or finger, type your name and pick one of four styles, or remove the white background from a photo of a signature on paper. Choose colour and thickness; PNG, SVG or copy.

ExampleAli Khan → signature.png (transparent)

Good to know

What are the limits?

This is an image signature, not a certificate-based digital signature.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.
