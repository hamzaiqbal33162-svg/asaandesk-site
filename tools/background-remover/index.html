Frequently asked questions

Which photos work best?

Photos with a clear subject, such as a person, a product or a pet, in good light.

Is it free to use?

Yes. It is free to use on AsaanDesk and needs no account or sign-up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser and your work stays on this device.

Which formats can I download?

You can download transparent PNG, or a new colour or picture behind it.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. A small model (about 5 MB) downloads the first time; after that it works offline.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; bigger projects are easier on a tablet or computer.

Is there a size limit?

There is no fixed limit; very large files depend on the memory and speed of your device.
