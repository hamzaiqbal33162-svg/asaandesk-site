Frequently asked questions

How is AI art made?

A model turns your description into a picture in the style you pick. Ours works once AsaanDesk AI is switched on.

Is it free to use?

Yes. It is free to use on AsaanDesk and needs no account or sign-up.

Does the AI work today?

AI generation needs AsaanDesk AI, which is not switched on yet. Until then the tool says "coming soon" and never shows a fake result.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The editor runs in your browser and keeps your work on this device. Once AsaanDesk AI is on, only the words of your AI request are sent.

Which formats can I download?

You can download PNG.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; bigger projects are easier on a tablet or computer.
