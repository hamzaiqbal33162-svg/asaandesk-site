How to use Sign PDF

  1. Open Sign PDF and choose your PDF.
  2. Draw your signature and place it on the page.
  3. Press Download PDF.

What does this tool do?

Draw your signature with a mouse or finger, then place it anywhere on the page and resize it. The file never leaves your device.

Exampleagreement.pdf → signed agreement.pdf

Good to know

What are the limits?

This is a drawn signature, not a certificate-based digital signature.

Input up to Large PDFs too, up to your device memory is supported.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.

Can I sign a PDF without Adobe or an account?

Yes. Draw your signature with a mouse or your finger, place it on the page, resize it and download the signed PDF. No Adobe, account or printing is needed.

Is this a digital signature?

It is a drawn signature, like signing on paper. It is not a certificate-based digital signature (digital ID). To have someone else sign, use Request a Signature.

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. Open this page in your phone's browser and draw the signature with your finger.
