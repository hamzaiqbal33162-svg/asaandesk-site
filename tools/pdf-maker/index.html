How to use PDF Maker

  1. Open PDF Maker; a blank A4 page is ready.
  2. Click to type text, add images, shapes or a signature; add pages with "+ Blank page".
  3. Press Download PDF.

What does this tool do?

Start with an A4 page, click anywhere to type, add images, shapes, links and a signature, add pages and download the PDF. Everything runs in your browser.

ExampleBlank page → notice.pdf

When to start a PDF from scratch

Sometimes you need a simple one-page document fast: a notice for a building, a price list, a cover page for a report, a short letter with a signature, or a flyer with a picture. Opening a full word processor and exporting can feel heavy for that. This tool starts you on a blank A4 page and lets you place items exactly where you want them, then saves a PDF that looks the same on every device.

Worked example: a shop notice

Click near the top and type a large heading such as "Closed on Friday". Click lower down to add the reopening time and a phone number. Add your shop logo as an image in a corner, draw a box around the times so they stand out, then press Download PDF and print it or share it in a chat.

Why PDF for documents like this

PDF fixes the position of every element, so the layout does not shift when someone opens the file on another phone or computer. Text in the file uses open fonts that look similar to Arial, Times and Calibri, which keeps the look familiar. Everything is put together in your browser, and the page size is A4 (210 by 297 mm), the standard paper size in most countries.

Tips

Leave a margin of at least 10 mm around the edges, since many printers cannot print right to the edge. Use one size for headings and one for body text to keep the page tidy. Keep images reasonably small in file size so the final PDF is easy to email. Check spelling and phone numbers before sharing.

PDF creator for notices, forms and one-page documents

A PDF creator is useful when you have no Word file to convert and simply need to make a PDF from scratch: a shop notice, a price list, a cover page or a short letter with a signature. Because you start on a blank A4 page and place each item where you click, it works more like a simple design tool than a converter.

To make a PDF, type your headings and text, drop in a logo or photo, draw a line or box to separate sections and sign at the bottom. Add another page with "+ Blank page" whenever one is full. The finished file is a normal PDF that opens on any phone or computer.

If you want to change a PDF you already have instead of starting a new one, open it in the [PDF Editor](/tools/pdf-editor).

Good to know

What are the limits?

Text uses open fonts (similar to Arial, Times and Calibri).

Input up to Up to your device memory is supported.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The tool's code is downloaded; your file or input is not sent to a server for conversion. Closing the browser stops the work.

How long does it take?

Text and small inputs are usually processed quickly. PDFs, images and audio take longer depending on their size, codec and the speed of your device.

How do I create a PDF online for free?

Open PDF Maker and a blank A4 page is ready. Click anywhere to type, add images, shapes, links or a signature, then press Download PDF. There is no sign-up.

Can I make a PDF with more than one page?

Yes. Press "+ Blank page" to add as many pages as you need; the only limit is your device's memory.

Which fonts can I use?

Text uses open fonts that look similar to Arial, Times and Calibri.

Are my files uploaded when I make a PDF?

No. The PDF is built in your browser, so the images and text you add stay on your device.

I already have the text or photos. Is there a faster way to make a PDF?

Yes. Paste or upload your text in Text to PDF, or put photos straight into one PDF with JPG to PDF.
