What each city page shows

Every city page has the sky, temperature, chance of rain, wind and humidity for each of the next 24 hours, in the city’s local time, and a 7-day table with each day’s high, low and total rain. The pages work on phones and load fast, and there is nothing to install or sign up for.

Where the forecast comes from

The city pages use the weather model of the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) for the centre of each city, refreshed several times a day; the quick widget above uses Open-Meteo. A model forecast is a good guide for planning the day, but local showers and dust storms can still arrive earlier or later than shown, so check again before you travel.
