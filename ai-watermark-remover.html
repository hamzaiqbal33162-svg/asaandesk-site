Questions

What exactly does the AI watermark remover remove?

Only the small visible sparkle that Gemini, Nano Banana, Veo and Flow blend into the bottom-right corner. Nothing else in the picture or video is changed.

Does it remove SynthID?

No. SynthID is an invisible watermark spread through the whole picture, and these tools do not touch it. A picture without the sparkle can still be recognised as made with Google AI by tools that check for SynthID.

Does it remove Content Credentials (C2PA)?

The tools do not look for them or change them in your original. Because a cleaned picture is saved again from its pixels, the new PNG carries no metadata at all, including any Content Credentials. That is a side effect, not a feature, and it does not change what the picture is.

Why was my picture not changed?

Because the tool could not prove the sparkle is there. It only changes a picture when the corner matches the exact shape of the mark at a place Gemini uses for that size, and when removing it leaves no trace. Resized, cropped or re-sent pictures often fail this test. See the guide on when the mark is not removed.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. The pictures and videos are read and cleaned in your browser. The page and its code are downloaded from AsaanDesk; for videos, the free video engine (ffmpeg.wasm) is downloaded once from unpkg.com. Your files never leave your device.

Does it lower the quality?

Only the pixels under the mark change; every other pixel is copied exactly. Pictures are saved as PNG by default, which keeps every value. Videos are encoded again as H.264 at high quality, which is close to the original but not bit for bit the same.
