About this forecast

This is a weather model forecast for the centre of Rawalpindi from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), refreshed several times a day. Temperatures are in degrees Celsius, wind speed in km/h and rain in millimetres. The hourly table covers the next 24 hours; the 7-day table shows each day's expected high, low and total rain.
