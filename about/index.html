About AsaanDesk

AsaanDesk (“asaan” means “easy” in Urdu) is a free website of everyday online tools: PDF tools, image and video converters, text and data tools, design makers and more than 200 calculators. Our aim is simple: the tool you need, in one place, with no sign-up and no install.

What makes AsaanDesk different

  • Your files stay with you. Most tools run inside your own browser, so your PDF, photo or video is not uploaded to our server. The few tools that need a server say so clearly, and our Privacy Policy explains each one.
  • Free, with no account. Every tool works without signing up, and there are no hidden watermarks or file-count traps.
  • Explained, not just calculated. Calculators and tools come with guides, formulas, examples and answers to common questions, so you can check the result and understand it.
  • Made for real use. Alongside international tools we build things people around us ask for, such as the Pakistan salary tax calculator, local units and Urdu text recognition.

Who runs it

AsaanDesk is an independent website run by a small team. We write, build and test the tools ourselves and keep improving them from the feedback we receive.

How the site stays free

AsaanDesk may show advertising (for example through Google AdSense) to pay for hosting and development. Ads never change how a tool works or what result it gives. See the Privacy Policy for how advertising cookies work and how to turn off personalised ads.

Accuracy

We test every tool, but results are for general information. Please read our Disclaimer before relying on a calculator or AI result for an important decision.

Get in touch

Found a bug, want a new tool or have a question? Visit the Contact page. We read every message.
