How to remove the Gemini watermark from an image

The cleanest result comes from the file Gemini gives you, not from a screenshot. Here is the whole path from the Gemini app to a picture without the sparkle, and how to check the result.

Start from the file Gemini gives you

The tool finds the sparkle from the picture's size, so the picture must still have the size Gemini made. In the Gemini app or on gemini.google.com, open the picture and use the download button; on a phone, use the share or download option rather than a screenshot. The file usually arrives as a PNG named something like Gemini_Generated_Image_….png.

Avoid screenshots, pictures forwarded through WhatsApp, Telegram or social apps, and pictures you already cropped or resized. Those steps change the size or compress the corner, and then the tool cannot be sure where the sparkle is. If you only have such a copy, try anyway: the tool will tell you if it cannot find the mark, and it will not change the picture then.

Remove the sparkle in four steps

  • Open the AI Image Watermark Remover (or the box at the top of the AI Watermark Remover page).
  • Drop your Gemini pictures on the box, paste one with Ctrl+V (⌘+V on a Mac), or press Choose pictures. Up to 30 pictures at a time work.
  • Leave "Save as" on PNG unless you need a JPG or WebP, and press Remove the mark.
  • Check the result in the slider: "Zoom on the mark" shows the corner up close. Then download the PNG, or one ZIP when you cleaned several pictures.

How to check the result

Drag the slider over the corner. On the left is the original with the sparkle, on the right the cleaned picture. In most pictures the corner looks exactly like its surroundings. On very bright or very dark corners, and on pictures with fine texture, a careful look can show tiny rounding differences, but no star.

The report line under each picture says which mark was found, its size and position, and which output size of Gemini it matches. The TXT report lists the same for a batch, including the pictures that were left unchanged and why.

Batches, phones and big pictures

All pictures of a batch are checked one by one on your device. A 4K picture takes a moment longer than a small one, but the work happens only in the corner, so even large pictures are quick. On a phone, use the same steps in your browser; paste works there too when your keyboard offers it.

Pictures where no mark is found are not included in the ZIP, so you never get an unchanged copy that looks like a cleaned one.

What does not change

Every pixel outside the mark is copied as it was. SynthID stays in the picture, so it can still be recognised as made with Google AI, and the picture is still AI-made: label it where that is required. The new PNG has no metadata; if you also want to see or clean the metadata of your originals, the Metadata & Privacy tools do that.

Use it only on pictures and videos you made or may edit. AI-disclosure rules and platform rules still apply after the mark is gone, and it must never be used to pass AI media off as real.
