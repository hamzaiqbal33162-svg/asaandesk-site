AI WATERMARK REMOVER · RULES

Responsible use

Removing the visible sparkle is useful for your own work: a clean slide, a mock-up, a print. It does not make AI media real, and it does not lift any duty to say that something is AI-made. Please read this before you use the tools.

Use it only on media you may edit

Use these tools on pictures and videos that you made yourself, or that you have the right to edit. The visible sparkle is Google's label for AI-made media; removing it is an edit to that media, like cropping or colour correction, and the same rights apply.

Never use it on someone else's picture or video to take their work, and never use it to remove another person's logo, signature, stock-photo mark or other ownership mark. The tool cannot remove those marks anyway: it knows only the Gemini sparkle.

It does not hide that the media is AI-made

SynthID, the invisible watermark Google adds to its AI pictures, video and audio, stays in the pixels after the sparkle is gone. Content Credentials (C2PA) stay in your original file. The result is exactly as AI-made as before, and it can still be recognised as such.

These tools are not made to get past AI detectors or platform checks, and they must not be presented that way. AsaanDesk will not add features for that purpose.

Disclosure rules still apply

Many places ask you to say when realistic media is made or changed with AI. Laws such as the EU AI Act require that deepfakes, and AI-written text published to inform the public, are disclosed as AI-made, and video and social platforms ask creators to label realistic AI content. Removing the visible mark does not change any of these duties.

The service you used to make the media has its own rules too. Google's generative AI policy, for example, forbids misleading people about where generated content came from, such as claiming it was made by a person to deceive. Check the terms of the service you used.

  • Label AI-made media wherever a law, a platform or common sense calls for it.
  • Do not use the result to pass AI media off as a real photo or a real recording.
  • Do not use it for fraud, false news, harassment, or to impersonate a real person.
  • Do not remove other people's ownership marks, logos or signatures.

Good reasons to use it

  • A clean picture for a slide, a mock-up, a moodboard or your own website, where you say elsewhere that it is AI-made.
  • A print or a wallpaper of your own AI picture, where the corner mark would be in the way.
  • Test images for your own software, design or teaching work.
  • A video clip for your own edit, where the AI origin is disclosed in the credits or the description.
