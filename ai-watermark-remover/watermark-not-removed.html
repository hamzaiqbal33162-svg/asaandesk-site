When the mark is not removed, and why

"Your image was not changed" is not an error: it means the tool could not prove that the Gemini sparkle is where it expects it. These are the usual reasons, and what you can do about each.

Why the tool says "not changed"

The remover is built to fail closed: it would rather leave your picture alone than change it on a guess. It changes a picture only when three things are true: the picture has a size Gemini makes (or a clean scaled copy of one), the corner matches the exact brightness pattern and edges of the sparkle at a place used for that size, and taking the mark out leaves no visible star-shaped rest and does not darken the corner. When any of these fails, you see "No visible Gemini mark found. Your image was not changed." or a similar line, and no file is offered.

Weak, unsafe or not found

"No visible Gemini mark found" means nothing in the corner looks like the sparkle. "Only looks a little like the Gemini mark" means something there resembles it, but not enough to be sure. "Removing it would leave a trace or damage the picture" means something shaped a bit like it is there, but the reverse blending does not take it away cleanly: often another logo, a white shape, or a mark whose strength was changed by editing.

In all three cases nothing was changed, and running the same file again gives the same answer. The tool has no setting to force a removal, on purpose.

When it worked but you still see something

Zoom on the corner with the slider. Very bright or very dark corners can show tiny rounding steps of one or two levels, invisible at normal size. In videos, compression can leave a faint outline where the edge of the star was. If you see a clear star after the tool said it removed it, please tell us through the contact details on the site, with the size of the picture or video.

What the tool will never do

It will not remove SynthID, Content Credentials or any other mark than the Gemini sparkle, and it will not guess. If you need to remove something else from your own picture, use an editing tool where you choose the area yourself.
