How to remove the Veo watermark from a video

Videos made with Veo show the same kind of sparkle as Gemini pictures, near the bottom-right corner. This guide explains how the video tool finds it, cleans every frame and keeps your sound.

What the Veo mark looks like

Videos made with Veo in the Gemini app and in Google Flow usually show a small sparkle near the bottom-right corner, much like the one on Gemini pictures. On a 1080p video it is about 72 pixels wide and sits about 108 pixels from the right and bottom edges (some videos have it a little further in); on a 720p video it is about 48 pixels wide. Portrait videos have their own measured places.

The sparkle is blended into every frame at the same place and strength, which is what makes it possible to remove it cleanly: once its place and strength are known, every frame can be undone the same way.

Remove it in three steps

  • Download the video from Gemini, Flow or wherever you made it, without editing or re-recording it.
  • Open the AI Video Watermark Remover, drop the MP4, MOV or WebM file on the box (up to 200 MB) and press Remove the mark. The first time, the free video engine is downloaded (about 30 MB).
  • Wait while the frames are checked and cleaned; the bar shows the progress. Compare the frame from the middle of the video, play the result, and download the MP4.

Try it: AI Video Watermark Remover

What happens to your video

Eight frames spread over the video are read first, only from the corner. The tool checks the known places for the video's size and measures the mark's strength on each frame. It continues only when the mark is clearly there on most samples and on every sample that has detail in the corner, so a video where the mark comes and goes is left unchanged rather than damaged.

Then every frame is cleaned by reverse alpha blending inside the video engine and saved as H.264 MP4, which plays everywhere. The picture is encoded again at high quality. AAC and MP3 sound is copied without change; other sound, such as Opus in a WebM file, is converted to AAC. Finally three frames of the new file are checked: if the mark still shows, you get no file.

Limits

Videos are already compressed, and compression softens the edge of the mark a little differently on each frame. After cleaning, a very faint outline can remain in places where you look for it closely; the star itself is gone. Videos that were cropped, resized, re-recorded or edited with effects over the corner usually no longer match and are left unchanged.

Everything happens in your browser, so the size of the video is limited by your device's memory: 200 MB is the upper limit, and long 1080p videos are faster on a computer than on a phone.

Still an AI video

SynthID is also embedded in Veo videos and is not touched by this tool. The video stays AI-made, and the rules of the platform where you publish it, and the law where you live, may require you to say so.

Use it only on pictures and videos you made or may edit. AI-disclosure rules and platform rules still apply after the mark is gone, and it must never be used to pass AI media off as real.
