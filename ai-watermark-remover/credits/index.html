Credits and licence

The method and the calibrated masks of the mark come from open-source work. We are grateful to its authors; their licence notice follows in full.

Based on

  • Gemini Watermark Remover by GargantuaX (github.com/GargantuaX/gemini-watermark-remover), commit c596ae7 of 24 November 2026.
  • Gemini Watermark Tool by Allen Kuo (AllenK, Kwyshell), the original reverse alpha blending method and calibrated masks.

What we use from it

  • The calibrated alpha masks of the 48 px, 96 px, 96 px wide-margin and 36 px marks, unchanged (lib/aiwm/alpha-maps.ts).
  • The reverse alpha blending, the correlation and edge scores, the corner search, the catalog of Gemini output sizes and the evidence thresholds, rewritten in TypeScript (lib/aiwm/core.ts).
  • The place catalog of Veo, Gemini and Flow videos, the video mask and the strength estimate, rewritten in TypeScript (lib/aiwm/video.ts). The video encoding uses ffmpeg.wasm instead of the original WebCodecs path.

The rest (the pages, the workspaces, the texts, the sample pictures and the video encoding with ffmpeg.wasm) is AsaanDesk's own work. The original project is not affiliated with AsaanDesk.
