Metadata & Privacy

Photos, AI pictures, videos, documents and songs carry hidden details: where a photo was taken, which phone took it, who wrote a document, the prompt behind an AI picture. These free tools show you what a file gives away and remove it, on your device. Nothing is uploaded and you do not need an account.

Remove EXIF data, GPS, XMP, IPTC, Content Credentials and AI prompts from photos and AI pictures. Up to 50 at once.

Video Metadata Cleaner

Remove GPS location, camera model, dates, titles and tags from MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM and MKV videos without re-encoding.

Photo Privacy Check

See what a photo gives away before you share it: GPS location, your name, device serial numbers, dates and AI prompts.

Check a picture or video for Content Credentials (C2PA): the app that made it, its actions, AI source type and signer.

Remove ID3 tags, cover pictures, comments and encoder notes from MP3, M4A and FLAC files. The audio stays the same.

Why it is safe to use

  • Nothing is uploaded. Your files are read and cleaned in your browser. Nothing read from them leaves your device, with one optional exception: the map in Photo Privacy Check sends the photo's position to OpenStreetMap when you press its button.
  • Quality stays. Pictures cleaned with "Keep quality" keep every pixel, videos and audio are never re-encoded, and documents keep their content.
  • You see what changes. Every tool shows what it found and what it removed, and Image Compare proves that the pixels are the same.
Privacy cleanup, not disguise. This is privacy cleanup. Visible labels such as "AI info" or "Made with AI" and watermarks drawn into the pixels stay, and platforms may still recognise AI-made pictures by other means. Removing metadata does not remove a duty to label AI-made content where a law or a platform rule asks for it.
