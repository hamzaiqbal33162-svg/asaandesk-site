Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

AsaanDesk (asaandesk.com) is a website of free online tools. We use as little data as possible. This page explains what happens.

1. Most tools: your file stays on your device

The PDF, image, audio, video, text and calculator tools run inside your browser. Your file is not uploaded to the AsaanDesk server, and we cannot see or save it.

2. Tools that use a server

  • Request Signature: the PDF you send for signing is kept on the AsaanDesk server (Cloudflare) for at most 7 days so the signer can open it, and is then deleted automatically. Once it is signed, the original file is erased. You can delete it at any time from the status page. The secret parts of links are stored only as hashes.
  • ID Card Maker Members API (only if you create an API workspace): members sent by you or your software (names, details and photos) are stored on the AsaanDesk server (Cloudflare) until you delete them or the whole workspace. Only your API key can see them, and we keep that key only as a hash. Without the API, designs and members stay in your browser only.
  • Document Scanner cloud sync (only if you turn it on): My Scans pages (images), names, folders, tags and recognised text are stored on the AsaanDesk server (Cloudflare) so you can see them on another device with your sync key. We keep the sync key only as a hash; you can use “Delete all from cloud” at any time. With sync off, scans stay in your browser only.
  • Webpage to PDF: our server opens the link you give and sends the page text back to your browser. The link and page are not saved.
  • AI tools (when Online AI is on): the text extracted from the PDF (not the file) is sent to OpenAI for the answer and is not saved. When Online AI is off, the AI tools use Chrome's own on-device AI and the text never leaves your device.

To enforce daily limits we keep a hash of your IP address (which changes every day) and that day's count. Your actual IP address is not saved.

3. Google Drive

If you click “Choose from Google Drive”, Google's own window opens. AsaanDesk asks only for the Google Drive drive.file permission, which means only the files you choose yourself. That file is downloaded straight into your browser so the tool can work on it. We do not look at other files in your Drive, we do not save your file on our server, and we do not share or sell Google user data or use it for advertising. AsaanDesk's use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can remove Google's permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Weather

With your permission, the weather widget sends your location (or the city you chose) to Open-Meteo to show the weather. AsaanDesk does not save it.

5. Browser storage and cookies

Some tools (such as the CV or invoice maker) keep what you typed in your own browser's local storage so your work is not lost when the page closes. It does not go to our server. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Children

AsaanDesk is not specifically meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.
