What this day counter does

This day counter works out how many days lie between two dates, then splits that span into weeks, months and days, weekend days, holidays and business days. It also tells you how far the end date is from today, so the same box works as a countdown to an event or a count of days since something happened.

The second tab works the other way round: start from a date, add or subtract a number of days, and see the date you land on. Tick one box and it counts business days only, skipping weekends and the holidays you choose. Everything is worked out on whole calendar days, so time zones and clock changes never shift the answer.

How to use the day counter

On the "Count days" tab, pick the Start date and the End date. Both open on today and 30 days from today, so you only change what you need. Tick "Count the end date too (adds 1 day)" when both the first and the last day should be counted.

  • "Add or subtract days" tab: set the Start date, choose Add or Subtract, and type the number of Days.
  • Tick "Count business days only" to move through working days instead of calendar days, for deadlines such as "within 10 business days".
  • Open "Weekends and holidays" to change the Weekend (Saturday and Sunday, Friday and Saturday, or Sunday only).
  • "Business days skip" chooses between "Weekends and holidays" and "Weekends only".
  • "A holiday on a weekend" decides whether a Saturday or Sunday holiday gives a day off on the nearest working day (the US federal rule) or gives no extra day off.
  • Untick any of the listed US holidays you do not get, tick the three optional company days off (the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve), and type your own in "Your own holidays", such as "Mar 23 Pakistan Day; 2027-01-04 Office closed". A date without a year counts every year.

Should you count the end date?

Most mistakes with day counts come from this one choice. By default the start date is counted and the end date is not, which measures the time that passes, like nights in a hotel. Counting both dates measures the days you are present or active, so the answer is one day more.

From Monday, Nov 23, 2026 to Friday, Nov 27, 2026 the counter gives 4 days. With the end date ticked it gives 5, the five days of a Monday-to-Friday course.

Worked examples

  • Countdown: from Sunday, Nov 22, 2026 to Thursday, Dec 31, 2026 there are 39 days, which is 5 weeks 4 days or 1 month 9 days. Eleven of them fall on a weekend and two are holidays (Thanksgiving and Christmas), leaving 26 business days.
  • Days until a date next year: from Nov 22, 2026 to Saturday, Aug 14, 2027 is 265 days, or 37 weeks 6 days.
  • Adding days: 90 days after Nov 22, 2026 is Saturday, Feb 20, 2027, and 100 days after it is Tuesday, Mar 2, 2027. Subtracting 40 days from Nov 22, 2026 lands on Tuesday, Oct 13, 2026.
  • Business days: 10 business days after Monday, Nov 23, 2026 is Tuesday, Dec 8, 2026. The span holds 15 calendar days, because 4 weekend days and Thanksgiving are skipped.
  • Another weekend: December 2026 with a Friday and Saturday weekend and no holidays has 31 days counting both ends, 8 weekend days and 23 business days.

Business days, holidays and working days in Pakistan

Business days are the days left after the weekend days and the holidays that fall on working days. The built-in list holds the US federal holidays, such as Memorial Day (last Monday of May) and Thanksgiving (fourth Thursday of November). Under the US federal rule, a holiday on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before and one on a Sunday on the Monday after. The counter never subtracts the same day twice.

For Pakistan, untick the US holidays and type the fixed national holidays into "Your own holidays": Feb 5 Kashmir Solidarity Day; Mar 23 Pakistan Day; May 1 Labour Day; May 28 Youm-e-Takbeer; Aug 14 Independence Day; Nov 9 Iqbal Day; Dec 25 Quaid-e-Azam Day. With a Saturday and Sunday weekend and "Gives no extra day off" for weekend holidays, Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2027 (both dates counted) has 365 days, 104 weekend days, 4 holidays on working days and 257 working days. Three of the seven fall on a Saturday in 2027.

Eid and other religious holidays follow the Islamic calendar, so their Gregorian dates change every year. Add them with the year, such as 2027-03-10, once the dates are announced, and set the weekend your office actually keeps.

  • Moved days off: with "Gives a day off on the nearest working day", the same 2027 list moves Labour Day (Saturday, May 1) to Friday, Apr 30 and Independence Day (Saturday, Aug 14) to Friday, Aug 13. The holiday table under the results shows each holiday, its date and the day it is taken off.
  • Weekends only: pick it under "Business days skip" for a plain count of weekdays, such as a delivery estimate that ignores holidays.
  • A day off outside the span: when a weekend holiday moves to a Friday before your start date or a Monday after your end date, the table marks it "outside these dates" and it is not subtracted.
  • Very long spans: the holiday table lists the first holidays and says how many more were counted, so the business-day total is still complete.

Day counter for visas, milestones and sobriety

Visa and residence rules are often written in days. The Schengen short-stay rule allows a stay of up to 90 days in any 180-day period, checked by looking back 180 days from each day of the stay, and the Schengen Borders Code counts the date of entry as the first day of stay and the date of exit as the last. So tick "Count the end date too": a trip from Thursday, Jun 10, 2027 to Saturday, Jul 24, 2027 is 45 days of stay. For several trips, the European Commission's own short-stay calculator checks the rolling window.

Citizenship and permanent residence rules also count days spent in or out of a country, but each country sets its own limits and counting method, so read the official rules for your application and use the counter to add up each trip.

For milestones, count from the first day to today. Someone sober since Saturday, Mar 1, 2025 has 631 days on Nov 22, 2026, which is 90 weeks 1 day or 1 year 8 months 21 days. Couples can do the same with an anniversary, and the second tab finds a future milestone: 1,000 days after Mar 1, 2025 is Friday, Nov 26, 2027.

Calendar rules: leap years and the day of the week

Month lengths and leap years are why counting days by hand goes wrong. In the Gregorian calendar a year is a leap year when it divides by 4, except century years, which must divide by 400 (2000 was a leap year, 1900 was not). The counter gives 28 days from Feb 1 to Mar 1, 2027 but 29 days for the same dates in 2028, and 366 days in the whole of 2028.

Every date in the results also shows its weekday. To find a weekday in your head, John Conway's Doomsday rule uses dates that share a weekday within each year. Those dates are 4/4, 6/6, 8/8, 10/10 and 12/12; 5/9, 9/5, 7/11 and 11/7; and 3/14 (Pi Day). In January it is Jan 3 (Jan 4 in a leap year), and in February the last day of the month.

  • Start from the century's anchor day: Tuesday for the 2000s.
  • Take the last two digits of the year. For 2027 that is 27: 27 ÷ 12 = 2 with 3 left over, and 3 ÷ 4 = 0 (ignore remainders).
  • Add the anchor and the three numbers, counting Sunday as 0: Tuesday (2) + 2 + 3 + 0 = 7, which is 0, so the doomsday of 2027 is a Sunday.
  • Count from the nearest doomsday: Aug 8, 2027 is a Sunday, so Aug 14 (six days later) is a Saturday, and Mar 14 is a Sunday, so Mar 23 is a Tuesday. The counter shows the same weekdays.

How to calculate days in Excel and Google Sheets

Spreadsheets store dates as day numbers, so the simplest day count is the end cell minus the start cell. The functions below do the same jobs as this page; Google Sheets has the same names.

Spreadsheet formulas for day counts (start date in A1, end date in B1)
TaskFormulaNotes
Days between two dates=B1-A1 or =DAYS(B1,A1)End date not counted; add 1 to count both
Business days, Saturday and Sunday weekend=NETWORKDAYS(A1,B1,holidays)Counts the start and the end date when they are working days
Business days, other weekends=NETWORKDAYS.INTL(A1,B1,7,holidays)Weekend code 7 is Friday and Saturday, 11 is Sunday only
Date N business days later=WORKDAY(A1,10,holidays)Negative days go back in time
Date N days later=A1+90Format the cell as a date
Day of the month=DAY(A1)1 to 31; the weekday name is =TEXT(A1,"dddd")

Because NETWORKDAYS counts both ends, it matches this page with "Count the end date too" ticked. WORKDAY and this page agree when the start date is a working day, but can differ by one day when you start on a weekend or holiday, because this page counts the next working day as day 1.

How it works

Pick a start and an end date to see how many days lie between them, the same span in weeks and months, and how far the end date is from today, which makes a handy countdown. The weekend days, holidays and business days inside the span are counted too. The second tab adds or subtracts a number of days, or of business days that skip weekends and holidays. The weekend, the holiday list and your own holidays are set under Weekends and holidays.

The formula

  • Days = end date − start date; + 1 when the end date is counted too
  • Weeks = days ÷ 7 (rounded down), with the days left over
  • Business days = days − weekend days − holidays that fall on working days
  • A holiday on a weekend gives a day off on the nearest working day (US rule) or none, and is never subtracted twice
  • N business days later: count the start as day 1 when it is a business day; the answer is the next business day after day N

A worked example

From Saturday, Nov 21, 2026 to Friday, Dec 25, 2026 there are 34 days (4 weeks 6 days). Ten of them are Saturdays or Sundays, so without holidays there are 24 business days; with Thanksgiving on Thursday, Nov 26 there are 23.

Questions

How do I calculate the days between two dates?

Enter both dates on the "Count days" tab. The counter subtracts the start date from the end date, so Sep 7 to Dec 18, 2026 is 102 days, and shows the same span in weeks, months and business days. Tick "Count the end date too" to include the last day.

Should I count the end date?

Leave it off to count the nights or the full days that pass, for example from a check-in date to a check-out date. Tick it when every listed day counts, like the days of a course from Monday to Friday, which is then 5 days instead of 4.

How do I count down to a date?

Keep the start date on today and pick the date you are waiting for as the end date. The result shows the days left and "End date from today", which also tells you how long ago a past date was.

How do I calculate days from a date?

Use the "Add or subtract days" tab: pick the start date, choose Add or Subtract and type the number of days. For example, 90 days after Nov 22, 2026 is Saturday, Feb 20, 2027. Tick "Count business days only" to skip weekends and holidays.

How many business days are between two dates?

The counter shows it under Business days: the total days minus the weekend days and the holidays on working days. Choose your weekend and holidays under Weekends and holidays, or pick "Weekends only" to ignore holidays.

Why does a holiday on a Saturday not lower the business days twice?

A Saturday is not a business day anyway, so subtracting the holiday again would be a mistake. Here its day off moves to the Friday (the US federal rule), or, if you choose, it gives no extra day off. Both ways each day is counted once.

Can I use a Friday and Saturday weekend or my own holidays?

Yes. Under Weekends and holidays pick Saturday and Sunday, Friday and Saturday, or Sunday only, untick holidays that do not apply and type your own, separated by semicolons, such as "Mar 23 Pakistan Day; 2027-03-10 Eid holiday".

How do I count days for a Schengen visa?

Tick "Count the end date too", because the Schengen rules count both the day you enter and the day you leave. Add up the days of every trip in the 180 days before the date you check; the total must not go over 90. The European Commission's short-stay calculator does the rolling check for several trips.

How do I calculate days in Excel?

Subtract the cells (=B1-A1) or use =DAYS(B1,A1) for calendar days, =NETWORKDAYS(A1,B1) for working days and =WORKDAY(A1,10) for the date 10 working days later. NETWORKDAYS counts both the start and the end date, so add the end date here to compare.

What does the DAY formula in Excel do?

DAY returns only the day of the month, from 1 to 31, so =DAY(A1) gives 23 when A1 holds Mar 23, 2027. For the weekday name use =TEXT(A1,"dddd"), and for a day count subtract the two dates instead.

How do I count the days of a pregnancy?

Pregnancy is usually counted from the first day of the last period, so put that date as the start and today as the end; the In weeks line then gives the weeks and days. Our [Due Date Calculator](/calculators/due-date-calculator) and [Pregnancy Calculator](/calculators/pregnancy-calculator) show the weeks and the key dates directly.

Does the day counter handle leap years?

Yes. It uses the Gregorian calendar, so February has 29 days in leap years such as 2028 and 28 in other years. A whole leap year counts as 366 days.
