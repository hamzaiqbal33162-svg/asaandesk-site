What is a body type calculator?

A body type calculator sorts your figure into a named shape from a few tape measurements. This one is a body shape calculator for women: it uses your bust, waist, high hip and hip sizes and the seven shapes of the Female Figure Identification Technique (FFIT), a system from clothing research that a 2007 study used to compare the body shapes of women in the USA and Korea.

The shape is about proportions, not size or health. Two women with the same shape can wear very different dress sizes, and every shape is normal. Its main use is practical: knowing which parts of you are widest helps you choose cuts and patterns that fit.

The one number in the results that does say something about health is the waist-to-hip ratio. It shows how much of your weight sits around the middle, which the World Health Organization links with heart and metabolic risk.

How to measure your body type

  • Stand straight with your arms relaxed at your sides and breathe out normally. Wear light clothing or measure on skin, and for the bust wear a bra that fits well.
  • Bust: around the fullest part of the chest, with the tape level across your back.
  • Waist: around the narrowest part of your natural waist, usually a little above the navel. Do not pull the stomach in.
  • High hip: about 7 inches (18 cm) below the waist, across the top of the hip bones.
  • Hips: around the widest part of the seat. Look in a mirror to check the tape is level all the way round.
  • Keep the tape snug but not digging in, measure each place twice and use the average. A second person makes this much easier.

How to use the calculator

Choose US units (inches, the default) or Metric (cm) at the top; one switch sets all four tapes. Type the bust and waist in the first row and the high hip and hips in the second, then press Calculate. You can also type a unit with a number, such as 90cm, and it is converted.

The results show your body shape with a one-line description, the waist-to-hip ratio to two decimals, and a health note that says Higher risk at 0.85 or more and Not raised below it. The steps list the differences the rules use (bust minus hips, bust minus waist, hips minus waist) and the high hip to waist ratio, so you can see why a shape was chosen. If no shape fits, the calculator says No clear match and names the closest shape and how many of its conditions you meet.

Apple, pear and somatotypes: other body type systems

Fashion writing often uses fruit and letter names. Roughly, an apple or inverted triangle carries more width at the top, a pear or triangle more at the hips, a banana or rectangle has a straight line, and an hourglass has a narrow waist between similar bust and hips. FFIT splits these into seven shapes with fixed rules so that two people measuring the same body get the same answer.

Somatotypes are a different idea. They describe build rather than outline, with three components: endomorphy (relative fatness), mesomorphy (relative muscle and bone) and ectomorphy (relative slimness). The widely used Heath and Carter method (1967) rates each one from careful body measurements such as skinfolds, bone widths and limb girths, which a tape at home cannot give, so this calculator does not estimate your somatotype.

Waist-to-hip ratio and your health

Fat stored around the waist is linked with a higher risk of heart disease and type 2 diabetes than fat stored on the hips and thighs. The WHO expert consultation on waist circumference and waist-hip ratio (meeting 2008, report 2011) treats a waist-to-hip ratio of 0.85 or more in women, and 0.90 or more in men, as a substantially increased risk of metabolic complications.

In the examples above, the rectangle (0.89) and the inverted triangle (0.94) are over the women's line, while the spoon (0.68) is well under it. Shape and risk are related but not the same: a rectangle with a small waist can have a low ratio.

Another simple check is the waist-to-height ratio. NICE advises adults to keep their waist to less than half their height. This matters in Pakistan and the rest of South Asia, because a WHO expert consultation found that many Asian populations reach a high risk of type 2 diabetes and heart disease at a lower BMI than Europeans, so a waist check adds a lot when BMI looks fine.

Body type for men, with shoulders, or from height and weight

  • Men: the seven shapes were built from women's measurements for clothing fit, so treat the shape as a rough description. The useful number for men is the waist-to-hip ratio, with the WHO line at 0.90. A man with a 44 in chest, 40 in waist, 41 in high hip and 42 in hips gets rectangle and a ratio of 0.95, above the men's line, although the page's note uses the women's 0.85.
  • Shoulders: FFIT uses the bust as the top measurement, and this calculator has no shoulder box. If your shoulders are much wider than your hips, an inverted triangle or top hourglass result usually matches what you see.
  • Height and weight: these give your size, not your shape, so they cannot tell an hourglass from a rectangle. For size and health, use the BMI Calculator or the Body Fat Calculator.

Tips for accurate results

  • Measure in the morning before a large meal, and always at the same spots, so results are comparable over time.
  • Keep all four measurements in one unit. A single cm value typed while the switch is on inches can change the shape completely.
  • If you get No clear match, check the tape was level, especially at the hips, and measure again. Some bodies simply sit between two shapes.
  • Your shape can change after weight loss or gain, pregnancy or menopause, so measure again before buying clothes after a big change.
  • For weight loss, watch the waist and the waist-to-hip ratio rather than the shape name; a smaller waist is the change that matters for health.

How it works

Measure four places with a soft tape, keeping it level and snug: the fullest part of the bust, the narrowest part of the waist, the high hip about 7 inches (18 cm) below the waist, and the widest part of the hips. The calculator compares the differences between them with the FFIT rules, a body shape system from clothing research, and checks the seven shapes in a fixed order; the first one that fits is your shape. It also divides your waist by your hips: the World Health Organization uses this waist-to-hip ratio as a simple sign of fat stored around the middle.

The formula

  • All four measurements in inches (cm ÷ 2.54); B = bust, W = waist, HH = high hip, H = hips
  • Hourglass: B − H ≤ 1 and H − B < 3.6 and (B − W ≥ 9 or H − W ≥ 10)
  • Bottom hourglass: 3.6 ≤ H − B < 10 and H − W ≥ 9 and HH ÷ W < 1.193
  • Top hourglass: 1 < B − H < 10 and B − W ≥ 9
  • Spoon: H − B > 2 and H − W ≥ 7 and HH ÷ W ≥ 1.193
  • Triangle: H − B ≥ 3.6 and H − W < 9; Inverted triangle: B − H ≥ 3.6 and B − W < 9
  • Rectangle: H − B < 3.6 and B − H < 3.6 and B − W < 9 and H − W < 10
  • Waist-to-hip ratio = W ÷ H; for women 0.85 or more means a substantially higher health risk (WHO)

A worked example

Bust 94, waist 70, high hip 88 and hips 96 cm: in inches the bust is 0.8 inches smaller than the hips (so bust − hips is under 1 and hips − bust under 3.6), and the hips are 26 cm, about 10.2 inches, bigger than the waist, so the shape is hourglass. The waist-to-hip ratio is 70 ÷ 96 = 0.73, below the 0.85 cut-off.

Questions

How do I calculate my body shape?

Measure your bust, waist, high hip and hips with a soft tape, enter them here and press Calculate. The calculator compares the differences between the four numbers with the FFIT rules and gives the first of seven shapes that fits, plus your waist-to-hip ratio.

What are the 7 female body shapes?

Hourglass, bottom hourglass, top hourglass, spoon, triangle, inverted triangle and rectangle. They differ in whether the bust or hips are bigger, how much smaller the waist is, and, for the spoon and bottom hourglass, how full the upper hip is.

Why is my shape shown as no clear match?

Some measurements fall between the seven shapes, for example when the hips are much larger than the bust but the waist is not much smaller. The calculator then names the closest shape. Measuring again with the tape level often helps.

Does this work for men?

The seven shapes were built from women's measurements for clothing fit, so for men they are only a rough guide. Men can use the waist-to-hip ratio, where the WHO cut-off is 0.90 instead of the 0.85 the page notes for women.

Can I find my body type with height and weight?

Not your shape. Height and weight tell you how big you are, but two people with the same height and weight can be an hourglass and a rectangle. You need tape measurements for shape, and the BMI or body fat calculators for size and health.

Is one body shape healthier than another?

Shape itself is not a health measure. The waist-to-hip ratio is the useful part: more fat around the waist is linked with a higher risk of heart disease and diabetes.

What waist-to-hip ratio is healthy?

The WHO links a ratio of 0.85 or more in women, and 0.90 or more in men, with a substantially higher risk of metabolic problems, so lower than that is better. A woman with a 70 cm waist and 96 cm hips has a ratio of 0.73.

Where exactly is the high hip?

Roughly 18 cm (7 inches) down from your natural waist, across the top of the hip bones. It shows whether the hips curve out early (a full high hip) or later.

What is an endomorph body type?

Endomorph comes from the somatotype system, where endomorphy means relative fatness, mesomorphy relative muscle and bone, and ectomorphy relative slimness. Somatotypes need skinfold and bone measurements, so this calculator, which uses four tape measurements, does not give one.

Can my body type change with weight loss?

The measurements can, and so can the shape, because the waist, bust and hips do not shrink by the same amount. Measure again after a big change in weight, and use the waist-to-hip ratio to track the change that matters for health.
