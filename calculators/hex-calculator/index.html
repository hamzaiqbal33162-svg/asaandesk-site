What hexadecimal is

Hexadecimal, or hex, is the base 16 number system. Ordinary decimal numbers use ten digits and each place is worth ten times the one to its right. Hex needs sixteen digits, so after 0 to 9 it borrows the letters A to F for the values ten to fifteen, and each place is worth sixteen times the one to its right.

This hex calculator adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides hex numbers, combines them with AND, OR and XOR, and converts hex to decimal and decimal to hex, with the working shown. Every answer is also given in binary and octal, because hex is mostly used as a short way to write binary.

In computers, hex is everywhere a long string of bits has to be read by a person: memory addresses, error codes, color codes on web pages, network hardware addresses and file data viewed byte by byte. One hex digit is exactly four bits, so one byte (eight bits) is always two hex digits, from 00 to FF.

How to use the hex calculator

  • Pick a tab: Calculate, Hex to decimal or Decimal to hex.
  • Calculate: type the First hex number, choose the Operation (+ Add, − Subtract, × Multiply, ÷ Divide (with remainder), AND, OR or XOR) and type the Second hex number. The page starts with 3E8 + FF.
  • Hex to decimal: type the Hex number. A point is allowed for a fraction, such as A.8. The example is 7E4.
  • Decimal to hex: type the Decimal number. It can be negative or have a decimal part, such as 10.5. The example is 2026.
  • Upper and lower case both work (ff = FF), and a leading 0x is ignored. Numbers can be as long as you need.

Windows Calculator has a Programmer mode for hexadecimal, decimal, octal and binary; open its menu and choose Programmer. It works with whole numbers only. This page also takes fractions such as A.8, shows each step, and has no limit on the length of the numbers.

Because 16 = 2⁴, every hex digit stands for exactly four bits, and the conversion is just a lookup. To go from hex to binary, replace each digit by its four-bit group: 3E8 becomes 0011 1110 1000, or 1111101000 once the leading zeros are dropped.

To go from binary to hex, split the bits into groups of four from the right, add zeros on the left of the last group if needed, and replace each group by its digit: 1011 0110 becomes B6. The same trick with groups of three bits gives octal.

Converting hex to decimal and decimal to hex, step by step

Write the place value above each digit, starting with 1 on the right and multiplying by 16 each step to the left: 1, 16, 256, 4,096 and so on. Turn each letter into its value, multiply each digit by its place value and add the results.

Example: 7E4. The digits are 7, E (14) and 4, in the 256s, 16s and 1s places. 7 × 256 = 1,792, 14 × 16 = 224 and 4 × 1 = 4, so 7E4 = 1,792 + 224 + 4 = 2,020 in decimal.

Digits after a point work the same way with 1/16, 1/256 and so on. A.8 is 10 + 8 ÷ 16 = 10.5.

Divide the number by 16 and write down the remainder. Divide the whole-number answer by 16 again, and keep going until the answer is 0. The remainders, read from the last one back to the first, are the hex digits; remainders 10 to 15 become A to F.

Example: 2026. 2026 ÷ 16 = 126 remainder 10 (A). 126 ÷ 16 = 7 remainder 14 (E). 7 ÷ 16 = 0 remainder 7. Reading upwards gives 7EA. Check: 7 × 256 + 14 × 16 + 10 = 1,792 + 224 + 10 = 2,026.

For a decimal fraction, multiply the part after the point by 16 again and again; each whole part you get is the next hex digit. 0.5 × 16 = 8, so 10.5 = A.8. Some fractions never end: 0.1 × 16 = 1.6 (digit 1), then 0.6 × 16 = 9.6 (digit 9) for ever, so 0.1 in decimal is 0.1999… in hex. That is also why many computer programs store 0.1 only approximately.

Hex addition, subtraction, multiplication and division by hand

Addition works like decimal addition, but you carry when a column reaches 16 instead of 10. For 3E8 + FF: in the right column 8 + F = 8 + 15 = 23, which is 16 + 7, so write 7 and carry 1. In the middle, E + F + 1 = 14 + 15 + 1 = 30, which is 16 + 14, so write E and carry 1. On the left, 3 + 1 = 4. The answer is 4E7.

Subtraction borrows 16 instead of 10. For 3E8 − FF: 8 is less than F, so borrow from E, which leaves D, and work out 8 + 16 − 15 = 9. D is less than F, so borrow from 3, which leaves 2, and work out 13 + 16 − 15 = 14, written E. The answer is 2E9, which is 1,000 − 255 = 745.

For multiplication and division, most people convert to decimal, work there and convert back, which is exactly the Worked in decimal line the calculator shows. 1F × 1F is 31 × 31 = 961, which is 3C1 in hex. 3E8 ÷ FF is 1,000 ÷ 255 = 3 remainder 235, so the hex answer is 3 remainder EB.

AND, OR, XOR and two's complement

AND, OR and XOR compare two numbers one bit at a time. AND gives 1 only where both bits are 1, OR gives 1 where either is 1, and XOR gives 1 where the bits differ. Programmers use them to keep, set or flip chosen bits: 3E8 AND FF = E8 keeps only the lowest byte, 3E8 OR FF = 3FF switches the lowest eight bits on, and 3E8 XOR FF = 317 flips them. XOR the result with FF again and you get 3E8 back, which is why XOR turns up in checksums and simple ciphers.

Computers store negative whole numbers in two's complement: the value plus 2 to the power of the bit width. In 8 bits, −1 is FF; in 16 bits, −717 is 65,536 − 717 = 64,819, written FD33. When a result here is negative, the calculator keeps the minus sign (−2CD) and also shows the two's complement in the smallest of 8, 16, 32, 64 or 128 bits that fits.

Hex color codes, ASCII and other everyday uses

Web colors. A color such as #FF8800 is three hex bytes for red, green and blue, each from 00 (none) to FF (full). Here red is FF = 255, green is 88 = 136 and blue is 00 = 0, a bright orange. #000000 is black, #FFFFFF is white and #808080 is a middle grey. With 256 levels for each of the three channels there are 256³ = 16,777,216 possible colors. Use Hex to decimal on each pair to get the RGB numbers.

  • Text: in ASCII and Unicode, the capital letter A is 41 in hex (65 in decimal), the small letter a is 61 and the digit 0 is 30. Unicode writes code points the same way, as U+0041 for A.
  • Network cards: a MAC address is 48 bits written as six pairs of hex digits, such as 00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5E.
  • IPv6: an internet address of 128 bits is written as eight groups of four hex digits separated by colons.
  • Error and memory codes: values like 0x80070005 are 32-bit numbers, eight hex digits long.

Tips and common mistakes

  • 10 in hex is sixteen, not ten. Write 0x10 or add hex after it when there could be confusion.
  • Do not mix up the letter O with the digit 0, or the letter I with 1; only A to F are hex letters.
  • Carry or borrow 16, not 10, when working by hand.
  • Read the remainders from the bottom up when converting decimal to hex.
  • Looking for a hexagon? A regular hexagon with side s has area (3√3 ÷ 2) × s², about 2.598 × s²; that is geometry, not hexadecimal.

How it works

Hexadecimal (base 16) uses the digits 0-9 and A-F, where A = 10, B = 11, C = 12, D = 13, E = 14 and F = 15; each place is worth 16 times the place to its right (1, 16, 256, 4,096 …). Choose Calculate to add, subtract, multiply or divide two hex numbers (division gives a whole-number quotient and a remainder) or to combine them bit by bit with AND, OR and XOR; the answer is also shown in decimal, binary and octal. Hex to decimal adds up the place values, and Decimal to hex divides by 16 again and again. Upper or lower case and a 0x in front are all fine.

The formula

  • Hex to decimal: d = hₙ×16ⁿ + … + h₁×16¹ + h₀×16⁰ (+ h₋₁×16⁻¹ + … after the point)
  • Decimal to hex: divide by 16 and keep the remainders (10-15 written A-F); read them from the last to the first
  • Division: a = q × b + r with 0 ≤ |r| < |b| (q rounded toward zero)
  • Each hex digit is exactly 4 binary digits: F = 1111, A = 1010

A worked example

8AB + B78: in decimal 2,219 + 2,936 = 5,155. 5,155 ÷ 16 = 322 r 3, 322 ÷ 16 = 20 r 2, 20 ÷ 16 = 1 r 4, 1 ÷ 16 = 0 r 1, so the answer is 1423 in hex. DAD = 13×256 + 10×16 + 13 = 3,501.

Questions

How do I convert hex to decimal?

Multiply each digit by its place value (1, 16, 256, 4,096 … from the right), turning A-F into 10-15, and add. 7E4 = 7 × 256 + 14 × 16 + 4 = 2,020. The Hex to decimal tab does this and shows the steps.

How do I convert decimal to hex?

Divide by 16 again and again and write down each remainder, using A-F for 10-15; then read the remainders from last to first. 2026 gives remainders 10, 14, 7, so it is 7EA in hex.

How do I convert hex to binary?

Replace each hex digit with its four-bit group: 0 = 0000, 9 = 1001, A = 1010, F = 1111. So 3E8 = 0011 1110 1000. Every result on this page also shows the binary form.

What is FF in decimal?

FF is 15 × 16 + 15 = 255, the largest value one byte can hold. FFFF is 65,535 and FFFFFFFF is 4,294,967,295.

How do I XOR two hex numbers?

Choose XOR under Calculate and type both numbers. The answer has a 1 bit wherever the two numbers differ; the Bit by bit table lines them up so you can check each bit. 3E8 XOR FF = 317.

How do hex color codes work?

#RRGGBB holds three bytes for red, green and blue, each 00 to FF (0 to 255). #FF8800 is red 255, green 136, blue 0. Convert each pair on the Hex to decimal tab.

Why do computers use hex?

One hex digit stands for exactly four binary digits, so long binary numbers become short and easy to read: 11111111 is FF, and a 32-bit value is just 8 hex digits. Colors (#FF8800), memory addresses and byte values are usually written in hex.

Does upper or lower case matter?

No. ff, FF and 0xFF all mean 255. Answers are written in upper case.

How are negative hex numbers shown?

The answer keeps a minus sign, such as −2CD for −717. Computers store negative whole numbers in two's complement, so the result also shows that form in 8, 16, 32 or 64 bits (−1 is FF in 8 bits).

What does the remainder mean when I divide?

Hex division here works with whole numbers, like long division at school: B78 ÷ 8AB = 1 with 2CD left over, because 1 × 8AB + 2CD = B78. The exact quotient with hex digits after the point is shown as well.

Can I convert hex to ASCII text here?

Not as text, but you can convert each byte: 48 65 6C 6C 6F are 72, 101, 108, 108, 111 in decimal, the ASCII codes of Hello. Common codes are 41 for A, 61 for a and 30 for the digit 0.

Is this a hexagon calculator?

No, hex here means hexadecimal numbers. For a regular hexagon with side s, the area is (3√3 ÷ 2) × s² and the perimeter is 6s; a 10 cm side gives about 259.81 cm².
