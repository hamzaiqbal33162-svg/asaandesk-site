How it works

Type an amount and pick the unit it is in and the unit you want. The amount is turned into kilograms with the exact size of the first unit, then divided by the size of the second. The table under the result shows the same amount in every unit at once. Results keep up to 14 significant digits, so small differences are not rounded away.

The formula

  • Result = amount × (kilograms in 1 'from' unit) ÷ (kilograms in 1 'to' unit)
  • 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg exactly; 1 ounce = 1/16 pound; 1 grain = 64.79891 mg; 1 carat = 200 mg
  • 1 stone (UK) = 14 lb; 1 stone (US) = 12.5 lb; 1 quarter (UK) = 28 lb; 1 quarter (US) = 25 lb; long ton = 2,240 lb; short ton = 2,000 lb; tonne = 1,000 kg
  • 1 tola = 180 grains = 11.6638038 g = 12 masha = 96 ratti
  • Market ser = 1 kg and maund = 40 kg; old ser = 80 tola (933.1 g) and old maund = 40 old ser (37.32 kg)
  • 1 atomic mass unit (dalton) = 1.66053906892 × 10^-27 kg (CODATA 2022)

A worked example

70 kg in pounds: 70 × 1 ÷ 0.45359237 = 154.32358352941 lb. And 10 tola of gold in grams: 10 × 11.6638038 = 116.638038 g.

Questions

How many grams are in a tola?

One tola is 11.6638 grams (180 grains), so 10 grams is about 0.857 tola. A tola is 12 masha, so a masha is about 0.972 g, and a masha is 8 ratti of about 0.1215 g each. Gold is still priced per tola in Pakistan and India.

How many kilograms is one maund?

In markets today a maund is 40 kg, made of 40 ser of 1 kg each. The older maund was 40 ser of 80 tola each, which is 37.32 kg. Both are in the lists, so check which one your bill or market uses.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

1 kg is 2.2046226218488 pounds, because a pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kg. For a quick estimate, double the kilograms and add 10% (70 kg is about 154 lb).

What is a stone?

A UK stone is 14 pounds (6.35 kg), still used in Britain and Ireland for body weight: 11 stone is 154 lb or about 70 kg. The less common US stone is 12.5 pounds.

Is a ton the same as a tonne?

No. A tonne (metric ton) is 1,000 kg. A US short ton is 2,000 lb (907.2 kg) and a UK long ton is 2,240 lb (1,016 kg). Choose the one you mean from the list.
