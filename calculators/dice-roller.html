How it works

Choose how many dice to roll and what kind: standard six-sided dice (shown as dice pictures for up to 12), the d4, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100 used in board and role-playing games, or any number of sides from 2 to 1,000,000. Press Roll for a new throw; changing a box rolls again too. With two or more dice you also get the total, the product of all the faces, the average, the highest and lowest roll, and a count of how often each face came up, with a chart for 20 or more dice. Every face is picked with your browser's secure random number source, and any random value that would favour some faces is thrown away and drawn again, so each face has exactly the same chance, just like a perfectly fair die. Nothing you roll leaves your device.

The formula

  • One die with s sides: face = 1 + r, where r is a fair random whole number from 0 to s − 1
  • Fair r: take just enough random bits for s − 1 and draw again whenever they give s or more (no remainder trick, so no bias)
  • Total = sum of the faces; average = total ÷ number of dice; a fair die averages (s + 1) ÷ 2
  • Product = all faces multiplied; shown exactly up to 15 digits, then in powers of ten

A worked example

Rolling two six-sided dice might give 5 and 3: a total of 8, a product of 15 and an average of 4. Over many rolls a total of 7 comes up most often (6 of the 36 ways), and 2 or 12 least often (1 way each).

Questions

Is this dice roller fair?

Yes. Each roll uses the secure random number source built into your browser, and random values that would make some faces more likely are thrown away and drawn again. Every face of every die has exactly the same chance.

How do I roll a d20 or another kind of die?

Pick it from the Kind of dice menu: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 20 or 100 sides. For anything else choose Other number of sides and type any number from 2 to 1,000,000.

How many dice can I roll at once?

Up to 10,000. The first rolls are shown at the top, the full list is in a table with a CSV download, and the count of each face shows how evenly they came up.

Why does the same face sometimes come up several times in a row?

Every roll is independent, so repeats happen naturally: with one six-sided die the chance of the same face twice in a row is 1 in 6. Over thousands of rolls each face still comes up about equally often.
