How it works

Paste your numbers into the box (commas, spaces and new lines all work as separators), or switch to "One number per box" and type them in one at a time, the way you would on a handheld statistics calculator. Every result updates as you type: the averages first (mean, median and mode), then the size of the list (count, sum, smallest, largest, range), the geometric mean, and the spread as both the whole-population and the sample standard deviation and variance. The sums of squares are there too, for anyone checking a formula by hand.

The formula

  • Mean = Σx ÷ N
  • Median = the middle number of the sorted list, or the average of the two middle numbers when N is even
  • Mode = the number or numbers that appear most often (when one appears more than once)
  • Range = largest − smallest
  • Geometric mean = (x₁ × x₂ × ... × x_N)^(1/N), for numbers that are 0 or more
  • Population variance σ² = Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ N; sample variance s² = Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ (N − 1); standard deviation = √variance

A worked example

For 3, 7, 7, 19 the sum is 36, so the mean is 36 ÷ 4 = 9. Sorted, the middle two numbers are 7 and 7, so the median is 7, and 7 is also the mode because it appears twice. The range is 19 − 3 = 16. The squared distances from 9 are 36, 4, 4 and 100, which add up to 144: the population variance is 144 ÷ 4 = 36 (standard deviation 6) and the sample variance is 144 ÷ 3 = 48 (standard deviation 6.9282032302755).

Questions

Which standard deviation should I use?

Use the sample column when your numbers are some of a bigger group (most surveys, tests and measurements). Use the whole-population column only when your list holds every member of the group you are describing.

Why does the geometric mean say "Not defined"?

The geometric mean multiplies the numbers together and takes a root, which only works for numbers that are 0 or more. If your list has a negative number there is no geometric mean, so we say so instead of showing a made-up value.

What if no number repeats?

Then the list has no mode. When two or more numbers tie for the most appearances, all of them are listed as modes, smallest first.

How do I use the one-number-per-box mode?

Type each number in its own box; empty boxes are skipped, and clearing a box removes that number. Twelve boxes are shown, and twelve more are under "More boxes". For longer lists, paste them into "Paste a list" instead.
