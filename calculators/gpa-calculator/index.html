How it works

Pick how your grades are written: letters on the Pakistan 4.0 scale (A 4.00, A- 3.67, B+ 3.33 ...), letters on the US 4.0 scale (A+ 4.3, A- 3.7, B+ 3.3 ...), grade points you type from your result card, or percentage marks. Then enter the credit hours and grade of each course; empty rows are skipped. Each course gives quality points (credit hours × grade points), and your GPA is all quality points divided by all credit hours, so a 4-credit course counts more than a 2-credit one. Tick the earlier CGPA box and enter your CGPA so far and the credit hours it covers to get your new CGPA. Pass/fail courses (P, NP) are listed but not counted. Under More options you can split the courses into semesters to see each semester's GPA and the CGPA after it, cap A+ at 4.0, or choose the decimal places. The Target CGPA tab works out the GPA you need in your remaining credit hours to reach the CGPA you want, and says plainly when it is out of reach.

The formula

  • Quality points of a course = credit hours × grade points
  • GPA = sum of quality points ÷ sum of credit hours (P and NP courses left out)
  • New CGPA = (earlier CGPA × earlier credit hours + quality points now) ÷ (earlier credit hours + credit hours now)
  • GPA needed = (target CGPA × all credit hours − CGPA now × credit hours done) ÷ credit hours still to take

A worked example

On the Pakistan scale, three 3-credit courses graded A, B+ and B give 3 × 4.00 + 3 × 3.33 + 3 × 3.00 = 30.99 quality points over 9 credit hours, a GPA of 3.44. With an earlier CGPA of 3.20 over 30 credit hours, the new CGPA is (96 + 30.99) ÷ 39 = 3.26.

Questions

How is CGPA calculated from my earlier semesters?

Multiply your earlier CGPA by the credit hours it covers to get your earlier quality points, add this semester's quality points, and divide by all the credit hours together. Tick the earlier CGPA box and the calculator does it for you.

Which scale should I choose, Pakistan or US?

Many Pakistani universities give A 4.00, A- 3.67, B+ 3.33 and so on, with no D- (the Pakistan option); some use 3.66 and 2.66 instead. Many US schools give A+ 4.3, A- 3.7, B+ 3.3 down to D- 0.7. If your university uses other points, choose grade points and type them from your transcript.

Does a course with more credit hours count more?

Yes. Each grade is weighted by its credit hours, so an A in a 4-credit course lifts your GPA more than an A in a 2-credit course.

What GPA do I need to raise my CGPA?

Open the Target CGPA tab, enter your CGPA now, the credit hours done, the CGPA you want and the credit hours you still have. You get the average GPA you need; if it is above the top of your scale, the target cannot be reached in those credit hours.

Why can my result differ slightly from my transcript?

Your earlier CGPA is already rounded, so a new CGPA built on it can be off by about 0.01. Universities also differ on repeated courses and failed grades, which this calculator does not apply.
