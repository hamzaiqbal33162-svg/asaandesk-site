How it works

A simple commission is the sale price times the commission rate, so any two of the three give the third. Many sales jobs pay more for bigger sales with tiers: for example 3% on the first 20,000 and 5% on everything above it. Each tier's rate is paid only on the part of the sale that falls inside that tier, the same way tax brackets work, so moving into a higher tier never lowers your pay. Some plans also pay a base commission on every sale, a fixed amount or a small percent of the whole sale, on top of the rest.

The formula

  • Commission = sale price × commission rate ÷ 100
  • Sale price = commission ÷ (commission rate ÷ 100); rate = commission ÷ sale price × 100
  • Each tier: part of the sale inside the tier = smaller of (sale, tier top) − tier start; tier commission = that part × tier rate
  • A tier paid as a fixed amount pays it once the sale reaches into that tier
  • Total = base commission + all tier commissions; overall rate = total ÷ sale price × 100

A worked example

A $200,000 sale with 3% up to $20,000 and 5% above it: the first tier pays 20,000 × 3% = $600 and the second pays (200,000 − 20,000) × 5% = $9,000. The total is $9,600, an overall rate of 4.8%, not 5% of the whole sale.

Questions

Does the top tier rate apply to the whole sale?

No. Each rate applies only to the part of the sale inside its own tier. With 3% up to 20,000 and 5% above, a sale of 200,000 earns 3% on the first 20,000 and 5% on the other 180,000. The breakdown table shows every part.

How do I find the rate I was paid?

In the Simple tab, type the sale price and the commission you received, and leave the rate empty. The rate is the commission divided by the sale price.

What is a base commission?

An amount paid on every sale on top of the rest, either a fixed sum or a percent of the whole sale. Turn it on in the Tiers and base pay tab and pick the money symbol or % to choose which.

Can a tier pay a fixed amount instead of a percent?

Yes. Switch that tier to the money symbol. The fixed amount is paid once the sale reaches into that tier, which suits bonus-style plans such as an extra 500 for any sale above 100,000.
