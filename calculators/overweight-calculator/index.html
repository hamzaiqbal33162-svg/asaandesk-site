How it works

Your weight is compared with your height through the body mass index (weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared). For adults 20 and over, the World Health Organization calls a BMI under 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 25 normal, 25 to 30 overweight and 30 or more obese, in three classes. The normal range for your height is 18.5 and 25 times your height squared, and the result shows how far your weight is from it. Children and teens from 2 to 19 are compared with others of the same age and sex on the CDC growth charts instead, and their status comes from the BMI-for-age percentile.

The formula

  • BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²
  • Adults: under 18.5 underweight; 18.5 to 25 normal; 25 to 30 overweight; 30 to 35 obese class I; 35 to 40 obese class II; 40 and over obese class III
  • Normal weight range = 18.5 × height² to 25 × height²
  • Children 2 to 19: under the 5th percentile underweight; 5th to under the 85th healthy weight; 85th to under the 95th overweight; 95th and over obesity (120% of the 95th percentile or more: severe obesity)

A worked example

A man 5 ft 10 in (1.778 m) tall weighing 190 lb (86.2 kg) has a BMI of 86.2 ÷ 1.778² ≈ 27.3, which is overweight. His normal range is 128.9 to 174.2 lb, so he is about 15.8 lb above it.

Questions

What counts as overweight?

For adults, a BMI of 25 up to 30 is overweight and 30 or more is obese. For children and teens, a BMI at or above the 85th percentile for their age and sex is overweight, and at or above the 95th percentile is obesity.

Why is my child called overweight and not at risk of overweight?

Older charts used the words at risk of overweight for the 85th to 95th percentile. Since 2007 the CDC and child health experts use overweight for that band and obesity for the 95th percentile and up, and this calculator uses the current terms.

Can a fit person show as overweight?

Yes. BMI counts muscle the same as fat, so people with a lot of muscle can read as overweight while having little body fat. A waist measurement or the body fat calculator gives a better picture for them.

How much should I lose?

The result shows how far your weight is above the top of the normal range. Even losing 5 to 10% of your weight can improve blood pressure and blood sugar, so a small, steady goal is a good start. Talk to a doctor before a big change.
