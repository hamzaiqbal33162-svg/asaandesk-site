How it works

This page has two parts. The snapshot at the top takes a home price, a down payment, a rate and a term, and adds the loan payment, property tax, insurance, mortgage insurance under 20% down and any HOA fee into one monthly cost of owning. It also shows the cash you need on the day (down payment plus closing costs) and the yearly income at which that monthly cost is 28% of income before tax, a common lender guide. Below it are all our property calculators, grouped by what you want to do next.

The formula

  • Loan L = price − down payment; loan payment M = L × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), r = rate ÷ 1200, n = years × 12
  • Monthly cost = M + price × tax % ÷ 12 + insurance ÷ 12 + PMI (L × PMI % ÷ 12, under 20% down) + HOA fee
  • Cash needed = down payment + price × closing cost %
  • Income that fits = monthly cost × 12 ÷ 0.28

A worked example

A $400,000 home with 20% down is a $320,000 loan; at 6.5% over 30 years the loan payment is $2,022.62. Tax at 1.1% adds $366.67 a month and $1,500 of insurance adds $125, so owning costs $2,514.28 a month. With 3% closing costs you need $80,000 + $12,000 = $92,000 to buy, and the cost fits an income of about $107,755 a year.

Questions

What does the monthly cost of owning include?

The loan payment (principal and interest), property tax, home insurance, mortgage insurance when you put down less than 20%, and any HOA or condo fee. Repairs, utilities and upkeep come on top; many owners set aside about 1% of the price a year for them.

Why 28% of income?

Many lenders like housing costs to stay at or under about 28% of your income before tax, and all your debts together under about 36%. It is a guide, not a rule: some loans allow more, and a lower share leaves more room in your budget.

Which calculator should I use next?

To see the full payment schedule with extra payments, open the mortgage calculator. To find a price from your income and debts, use the house affordability calculator. To compare buying with renting, or to check a rental property, use the renting and investing calculators below.
