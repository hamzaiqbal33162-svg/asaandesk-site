What a boat loan calculator tells you

A boat loan calculator turns a boat price, a down payment, a loan term and an interest rate into the fixed monthly payment you would make, and shows how much of the money you hand over is interest. It can also run the other way: start from the monthly payment you can live with and find the most expensive boat that payment covers.

A boat loan (also called a marine loan) is an installment loan used to buy a boat, and the boat itself usually secures it, much like a car secures an auto loan. Banks, credit unions, specialist marine lenders and dealers all offer them. Each payment covers that month's interest plus a slice of the balance, so the loan is fully repaid with the last payment.

Because boats are often financed for longer than cars, small changes in the rate or the term move the total interest by thousands. Seeing the payment, the interest and the full cost side by side is the quickest way to judge whether an offer fits your budget.

How to use the boat loan calculator

  • Pick a tab: From the boat price if you know what the boat costs, or From a monthly budget if you know what you can pay each month.
  • Choose your currency. Dollars are the default; rupees, pounds, euros, Canadian and Australian dollars and others are in the list.
  • Enter the Boat price (default 35,000) or the Monthly payment you can afford (default 400).
  • Enter the Down payment and switch its unit between an amount and %. A % is taken from the price, and on the budget tab from the price the calculator finds.
  • Set the Loan term in years or months (default 10 years) and the Interest rate (APR), default 7% a year.
  • Open Trade-in, tax and fees for the rest: Trade-in value, Sales tax rate (default 6%), the box The trade-in lowers the taxed price, Title, registration and other fees (default 500) and the box Add the sales tax and fees to the loan.

The result updates as you type. Below it you get a pie chart of the total cost, a balance chart and the amortization schedule by year and by month, so you can see the balance fall payment by payment.

Worked examples: price, budget, trade-in and a Canadian boat

Price first. With the defaults the payment is 325.10 a month. Put 10% down instead of 7,000 and the calculator shows a 3,500 down payment, a 31,500 loan and a payment of 365.74, with 12,389.01 of interest. A bigger deposit is the simplest lever you control.

Budget first. On the From a monthly budget tab, 400 a month for 10 years at 7% supports a 34,450.54 loan. Add the 7,000 down payment and the calculator finds a boat price of 41,450.54. Sales tax on that price is 2,487.03, so you would need 9,987.03 in cash at the start.

A trade-in in Texas. Say a 60,000 boat with a 10,000 trade-in, 6,000 down, 6.25% tax, 300 of fees, 15 years at 7.5%. The loan is 44,000 and the payment 407.89 either way. Ticking the trade-in credit taxes only 50,000, so tax falls from 3,750 to 3,125 and the total cost from 93,469.38 to 92,844.38.

A boat in Ontario, in Canadian dollars. A C$45,000 boat, C$9,000 down, 13% HST, C$400 of fees, 12 years at 8%: the payment is C$389.68 and you pay C$15,250 upfront. Roll the tax and fees into the loan and the payment rises to C$457.34, while total interest grows from C$20,114.34 to C$23,606.42.

Sales tax, trade-ins and state rules

Tax on a boat is set by the state, province or country where you register or use it, and the rules differ more than for cars. Some places tax the full price, some tax the price after the trade-in, and some cap the tax on expensive boats. The calculator taxes the full price unless you tick the trade-in credit.

Texas charges a 6.25% boat and boat motor tax with no local tax added, and the Comptroller says a trade-in reduces the taxable value. For vessels and outboard motors bought in Texas on or after September 1, 2019, Texas Parks and Wildlife says the tax is capped at 18,750. Florida limits the tax on a boat to 18,000 per sale, a cap in force since July 1, 2010 that includes the county surtax.

Outside the US, use your country's rate. In Canada the combined GST/HST is 5% to 15% depending on the province (rates in force since April 1, 2025). Australia's GST is 10%, and the UK standard VAT rate is 20%. Check with your tax office before you buy, because rates and boat rules change.

The full cost of owning a boat

The loan payment is only part of the bill. Before you sign, list the one-off costs and the yearly costs, and test whether the payment still fits once they are added.

  • At purchase: sales tax, title and registration, dealer documentation charges, any lender origination fee, a marine survey (an inspection many lenders ask for on used boats) and a trailer if the boat is small enough to tow.
  • Every year: insurance, which some lenders require while the loan is open, registration renewal, routine engine service, hull cleaning and winter storage or haul-out where waters freeze.
  • Every trip: fuel, launch or marina fees and wear on safety gear such as life jackets and fire extinguishers.
  • Larger boats: a slip or mooring, bottom paint and bigger repair bills, which can make a cheap boat expensive to keep.

Enter the one-off costs you will pay at purchase in the fees box, and decide whether to finance them. Keep the yearly costs in your own budget, or use a budget calculator to see the full monthly picture.

Tips before you sign a boat loan

  • Compare the total interest, not just the payment. A lower payment from a longer term is not a cheaper loan.
  • Pay tax and fees upfront if you can. With the defaults, financing them adds 1,022.58 of interest over 10 years.
  • Ask whether the loan has a prepayment penalty. Without one, paying extra each month shortens the loan; this calculator uses a fixed schedule, so model extra payments with the amortization calculator.
  • Ask how a balloon payment works before you accept one. A low payment that ends in a large lump sum is a different loan from the level payments shown here.
  • If the boat has sleeping, cooking and toilet facilities, the IRS can treat it as a home, so the interest may count as mortgage interest for a second home. Read IRS Publication 936 or ask a tax adviser before relying on that.
  • Get a survey on a used boat before you agree a price. Repairs found later are paid with your own cash.

How it works

On From the boat price, enter the price, the down payment (as an amount or a % of the price), the loan term in years or months and the APR to get the monthly payment. On From a monthly budget, enter the payment you can afford and the calculator works back to the boat price it pays for. Open Trade-in, tax and fees to add a trade-in, the sales tax rate and the title, registration and other fees, and choose whether tax and fees are paid upfront or added to the loan. Sales tax is charged on the full price unless you tick the trade-in credit, which some states give. Results come with a yearly and a monthly amortization schedule.

The formula

  • Down payment = the amount, or price × down % ÷ 100
  • Sales tax = (price − trade-in, if you tick the trade-in credit) × tax rate
  • Loan = price − down payment − trade-in + (tax + fees, if added to the loan)
  • Monthly rate r = APR ÷ 100 ÷ 12; monthly payment M = loan × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n); at 0% M = loan ÷ n
  • From a budget: loan = M × (1 − (1 + r)^−n) ÷ r, then the price whose loan is exactly that
  • Total cost = price + sales tax + fees + total interest

A worked example

A 35,000 boat with 7,000 down over 10 years at 7% APR: the loan is 28,000, r = 0.07 ÷ 12 and n = 120, so M = 28,000 × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−120) = 325.10 a month. With 6% sales tax (2,100) and 500 of fees paid upfront, you pay 9,600 at the start, the 120 payments add up to 39,012.45 (11,012.45 of it interest), and the boat costs 48,612.45 in all.

Questions

How do I calculate a boat loan payment?

Divide the APR by 1,200 to get the monthly rate r, count the payments n, and use M = loan × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n). A 28,000 loan over 120 months at 7% works out to 325.10 a month. The calculator does this for you and adds tax, fees and the full schedule.

What is a typical boat loan term?

Boat loans usually run longer than car loans, and terms vary by lender; larger loans on newer boats tend to get the longest terms. Try a few terms in the calculator: on a 28,000 loan at 7%, 10 years costs 11,012.45 of interest and 20 years costs 24,100.09.

What is the longest boat loan term?

It depends on the lender, the loan amount and the boat. Large loans on new boats get the longest terms, while small loans and older boats get shorter ones. This calculator accepts terms up to 50 years (600 months), but always check the total interest before choosing a long one.

What is a good boat loan interest rate?

A good rate is the lowest one you are offered for the same term and deposit, and it depends mainly on your credit score, the term and the boat's age. Rates change with the market, so collect quotes from a bank, a credit union and a marine lender and compare the total interest each gives.

How much is the monthly payment on a 50,000 boat loan?

At 7% APR, a 50,000 loan costs 580.54 a month over 10 years, with 19,665.09 of interest, or 449.41 a month over 15 years, with 30,894.54 of interest. Enter your own price, down payment, term and APR for an exact figure.

Does my trade-in lower the sales tax on a boat?

It depends on where you register the boat. Texas lets a trade-in reduce the taxable value, while other places tax the full price. The calculator taxes the full price unless you tick The trade-in lowers the taxed price.

Can I add sales tax and fees to a boat loan?

Some lenders allow it, and the calculator has a box for it. It means less cash at the start, but you pay interest on the tax and fees for the whole term. With the defaults that costs 1,022.58 more over 10 years.

Can I enter the down payment as a percentage?

Yes. Switch the down payment unit to % and it is taken as a share of the price. On the budget tab the % applies to the price the calculator finds, and the result shows the down payment as an amount.

Does this boat loan calculator show an amortization table?

Yes. Under the results there is a yearly and a monthly schedule with the interest, the principal and the balance left after each payment, plus a chart of the balance over time.

Is boat loan interest tax deductible?

In the US it can be, if the boat has sleeping, cooking and toilet facilities and qualifies as your main or second home under IRS Publication 936. Other boat loans are personal loans with no deduction. Check with a tax adviser for your situation.
