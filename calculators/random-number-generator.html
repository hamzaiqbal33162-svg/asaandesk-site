What this random number generator does

A random number generator picks numbers that you cannot predict, where every allowed number has the same chance. This one works between any two limits you choose: a single number between 1 and 100, ten numbers between 1 and 10, or thousands of decimals with up to 999 decimal places.

It suits lucky draws, raffles, classroom picks, board games, choosing a random sample for a survey, and testing software. The numbers are drawn on your own device, so no list of names or ticket numbers is sent anywhere.

How to use it, step by step

  • From (lowest) and To (highest): type the two limits. Both can come up. If you type them the wrong way round, they are swapped and a note says so.
  • How many numbers: from 1 to 10,000 in one go.
  • Decimal places: 0 gives whole numbers; 2 gives numbers such as 4.07. From 0 To 1 with 2 decimal places draws one of 101 values, 0.00 to 1.00, each equally likely.
  • More options, "The same number may come up more than once": ticked by default. Untick it for draws where nobody can win twice.
  • More options, Order: As drawn, Smallest first or Largest first. Sorting helps when you read winners off a list.
  • Press Generate, or change any box, for a fresh set.

Worked examples: lucky draws and random picks

Office lucky draw in Pakistan: a company holds an Eid draw for 250 staff with 3 prizes of PKR 10,000 each. Number the staff list from 1 to 250 (for example in alphabetical order) and share it before the draw. Set From 1, To 250, How many 3 and untick repeats. A result such as 17, 203, 88 means the people at those positions win. Each person has a 3 in 250 chance, which is 1.2%.

Classroom pick: to choose 5 of 40 students to present, set From 1, To 40, How many 5, untick repeats and choose Smallest first, then read the names from the register.

Lottery quick pick: Powerball asks for five different numbers from 1 to 69 and one number from 1 to 26, drawn from a separate pool. Run the generator twice with those settings. The chance that one ticket matches all six is 1 in 292,201,338, and no generator, this one included, can change that.

How random are these numbers?

Computers usually make random numbers with an algorithm called a pseudo-random number generator (PRNG). Ordinary ones, such as JavaScript's Math.random or Python's random module, are fine for games but are not meant for security, as their own documentation warns.

This page uses the browser's Web Crypto source, getRandomValues. Browsers implement it as a cryptographically strong generator seeded with entropy, usually from the operating system, the same kind of source used for passwords and encryption keys. Truly physical randomness, such as electronic noise or radioactive decay, needs special hardware; for draws, games and sampling a cryptographic generator is more than good enough.

Fairness also depends on how raw random bits become a number in your range. The common shortcut of taking a remainder is biased: random whole numbers from 0 to 7 divided by 6 leave remainders 0 and 1 twice as often as the others. This page throws such values away and draws again, so in 1 to 6 each face has exactly a 1 in 6 chance.

Repeats, streaks and predictions

With repeats allowed, a repeat is more likely than most people expect. Drawing 10 numbers from 1 to 100 gives at least one number twice about 37% of the time, and 20 numbers about 87% of the time. Three dice rolls show a repeated face 44% of the time. This is the same maths as the well-known birthday problem.

Each draw is independent, so a number that has not come up for a while is not "due", and a number that just came up is not less likely next time. For the same reason, tools that claim to predict lottery numbers or online crash games such as Aviator cannot work when the game is fair; treat any such app as a scam.

Random numbers in Excel, Google Sheets, Python and JavaScript

  • Excel: =RANDBETWEEN(1, 100) gives a whole number from 1 to 100, both included. It changes every time the sheet recalculates, so copy and paste the values to keep them.
  • Google Sheets: =RANDBETWEEN(1, 100) works the same way, with both limits included.
  • Python: random.randint(1, 100) includes both limits. For anything secret, such as tokens or codes, use the secrets module instead.
  • JavaScript: Math.floor(Math.random() * (max − min + 1)) + min gives a whole number from min to max, but Math.random is not secure; use crypto.getRandomValues for security, as this page does.
  • The general formula is lowest + a random whole number from 0 to (highest − lowest), which this page computes exactly, even for limits with hundreds of digits.

Random samples and the random number table method

Researchers pick random samples so that every member of a group has the same chance of being chosen. Number every member, then draw as many different numbers as your sample size. To survey 50 of 1,200 households, set From 1, To 1200, How many 50 and untick repeats.

Before computers, this was done with printed random number tables. The best known is the RAND Corporation's A Million Random Digits with 100,000 Normal Deviates, first published in 1955. The generator replaces the table: it gives the numbers directly, already inside your range and without repeats. The Sample Size Calculator tells you how many to draw.

Tips for a fair public draw and related tools

  • Publish the numbered list before you draw, so nobody can change who is number 17 afterwards.
  • Draw once, on a shared screen or a recorded call, and use the first result.
  • Untick repeats so one person cannot win two prizes, unless your rules allow it.

How it works

Type the lowest and the highest number you want, and how many numbers to pick. Both limits can come up, so 1 to 6 works like a dice. Set decimal places to get numbers such as 12.47; under More options you can stop a number from coming up twice (for raffles and lucky draws) and sort the result. Each number is picked with your browser's secure random number source (Web Crypto), and any value that would make some numbers more likely is thrown away and picked again, so every number in the range has exactly the same chance. Limits can have hundreds of digits, and nothing you type leaves your device.

The formula

  • Whole numbers from L to U: L + r, where r is a fair random whole number from 0 to U − L
  • Fair r below n: take random bits as long as n − 1, and pick again whenever the bits are n or more (no remainder trick, so no bias)
  • With p decimal places: pick a whole number from L × 10^p to U × 10^p, then divide by 10^p (exact, with exactly p decimals)
  • No repeats: Floyd's sampling picks k different numbers with k draws, then a Fisher–Yates shuffle puts them in random order

A worked example

For a lucky draw among tickets 1 to 500 with 3 prizes, type From 1, To 500, How many 3, and turn repeats off under More options. You might get 417, 38, 251: three different tickets, each of the 500 equally likely to win.

Questions

How do I generate a random number between 1 and 100?

Type 1 in From and 100 in To, keep How many at 1 and press Generate. Every whole number from 1 to 100, including 1 and 100, has the same 1 in 100 chance.

How do I pick random numbers with no repeats?

Open More options and untick "The same number may come up more than once". You cannot ask for more different numbers than the range holds: 1 to 10 has only 10.

Can both the lowest and the highest number come up?

Yes. The limits are included, so From 1 To 6 gives 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6, just like a dice.

How do I generate multiple random numbers at once?

Set How many numbers to the amount you need, up to 10,000. The first numbers show at the top, the full list is in the table below, and Copy takes them all.

How do I pick a random name from a list?

Number the names from 1 to the length of the list, then draw one number in that range, or several with repeats off. The name at that position is your pick.

What is the formula for a random number between two numbers?

Random number = lowest + a random whole number from 0 to (highest − lowest). In spreadsheets the same idea is =RANDBETWEEN(lowest, highest).

How do I generate random numbers in Excel within a range?

Use =RANDBETWEEN(bottom, top), which includes both limits, and fill it down for more numbers. The values change on every recalculation, so paste them as values once you are happy.

Are these numbers really random and fair?

They come from the secure random source built into your browser, the same kind used for passwords and encryption keys. Values that would favour some numbers are rejected and picked again, so in 1 to 100 each number has exactly a 1 in 100 chance.

Can a random number generator predict the lottery or Aviator?

No. In a fair game every draw is independent of the ones before it, so nothing can predict the next result. Apps that promise predictions are best avoided.

How do I get new numbers?

Press Generate, or change any box. Each time you get a fresh, independent set. A shared link keeps your settings, not the numbers.

How large can the numbers be?

Limits can have up to 1,000 digits and up to 999 decimal places, and you can pick up to 10,000 numbers at once. Everything is worked out exactly with whole-number maths, not rounded.
