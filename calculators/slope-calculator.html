Steps

  1. Run (change in x): ΔX = x₂ − x₁ = 6 − 2 = 4
  2. Rise (change in y): ΔY = y₂ − y₁ = 4 − 1 = 3
  3. Slope: m = ΔYΔX = 34 = 0.75
  4. Angle: arctan(0.75) = 36.869897645844°, so θ = 36.869897645844°
  5. Line y = mx + b through point 1: b = y₁ − m × x₁ = 1 − 0.75 × 2 = -0.5, so y = 0.75x − 0.5
  6. Where it crosses the x-axis, y = 0: x = −bm = 0.50.75 = 0.66666666666667

How it works

With two points, type x and y for each one. The slope is the rise (how far y changes) divided by the run (how far x changes), so it says how many steps up the line goes for each step to the right. You also get the angle of the direction from the first point to the second, measured counter-clockwise from the positive x direction (0° to 360°), the straight distance between the points, and the equation y = mx + b with both intercepts. The other tab works backwards: from one point, a distance and a slope (or an angle) it finds where the second point is. A slope fits two directions, so you get both answers; an angle fixes one direction, so you get one.

The formula

  • Slope m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁) = rise ÷ run
  • Angle θ = arctan(ΔY ÷ ΔX), turned into a direction between 0° and 360° (add 180° when the run is negative, 360° when only the rise is negative)
  • Distance d = √(ΔX² + ΔY²) (the Pythagorean theorem)
  • Line: y = mx + b with b = y₁ − m × x₁; it meets the x-axis at x = −b ÷ m
  • From a distance and a slope: ΔX = ± d ÷ √(1 + m²), ΔY = m × ΔX
  • From a distance and an angle: ΔX = d × cos θ, ΔY = d × sin θ, m = tan θ

A worked example

From (−3, 4) to (5, −2): the run is 5 − (−3) = 8 and the rise is −2 − 4 = −6, so m = −6 ÷ 8 = −3/4 = −0.75. The distance is √(8² + 6²) = √100 = 10. Then b = 4 − (−0.75 × −3) = 1.75, so the line is y = −0.75x + 1.75.

Questions

What does a negative slope mean?

The line goes down as you move to the right. A slope of −2 means y falls by 2 for every 1 step to the right. A slope of 0 is a flat line, and a bigger number (positive or negative) means a steeper line.

Why is the slope of a vertical line not defined?

On a vertical line the run is 0, and the slope would divide the rise by 0, which has no value. Its equation is x = a number instead of y = mx + b, and its angle is 90° going up or 270° going down.

Why is the angle sometimes more than 180°?

The angle shows the direction from the first point to the second, going counter-clockwise from the positive x direction. Going down and to the right gives an angle between 270° and 360°, which points the same way as a small negative angle.

Why do I get two answers for the second point?

A slope gives a line but not which way along it you travel. Moving the same distance to the right or to the left both land on the line, so both points are shown. With an angle the direction is fixed, so there is one answer.

Can I use decimals or negative numbers?

Yes. Decimal points are turned into exact fractions first, so a slope such as 0.6/0.8 comes out exactly as 3/4 = 0.75, and negative coordinates are fine anywhere.
