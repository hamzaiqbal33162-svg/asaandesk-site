How it works

A car lease payment has three parts. Depreciation is the value the car loses while you drive it: the amount being leased (price minus down payment and trade-in) minus the residual value, spread over the months. The finance charge is the lender's interest, worked out with a money factor on the leased amount plus the residual. Sales tax is then added to both. The second tab runs the same sum backwards to find the car price a monthly budget allows. Below the result, the same car bought with a loan at the same rate over the same months shows which way costs less to own.

The formula

  • Amount leased C = car price − down payment − trade-in; R = residual value
  • Money factor MF = APR ÷ 2,400 (and APR = MF × 2,400)
  • Depreciation D = (C − R) ÷ months; finance charge F = (C + R) × MF
  • Tax T = (D + F) × sales tax; monthly payment = D + F + T
  • Up-front tax = down payment × sales tax (the trade-in is not taxed)
  • Cost to own after the lease = all payments + down payment + up-front tax + trade-in + R × (1 + sales tax)
  • Loan instead: payment = C × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−months) with r = APR ÷ 12; sales tax = (price − trade-in) × tax, paid up front

A worked example

A $50,000 car with $10,000 down, a $24,000 residual, money factor 0.00208 and 7% tax over 36 months: D = (40,000 − 24,000) ÷ 36 = $444.44, F = (40,000 + 24,000) × 0.00208 = $133.12, T = 7% × 577.56 = $40.43, so the payment is $617.99 a month.

Questions

What is a money factor?

It is how lease deals quote the interest rate. Multiply it by 2,400 to get the yearly rate: a money factor of 0.00208 is about 5%. You can type either one here, and the result shows the other.

What residual value should I use?

The leasing company sets it, usually as a share of the car's list price: often around 50% to 60% after three years. A higher residual means the car loses less value on paper, so the payment is lower.

Is a big down payment a good idea on a lease?

It lowers the monthly payment, but if the car is written off early, insurance usually pays the leasing company, not you, and the down payment can be lost. Many people put down only what they must.

Is leasing cheaper than buying?

Leasing usually means a lower monthly payment, because you only pay for the value the car loses. If you want to keep the car, buying with a loan often costs less in total. The comparison table shows both totals for your numbers.

How is tax handled?

Here sales tax is added to each monthly payment and the down payment is taxed at signing, while the trade-in is not taxed. A few US states tax leases differently, for example on the full price up front, so check your state's rule.
