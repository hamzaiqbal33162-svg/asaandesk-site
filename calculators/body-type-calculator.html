Steps

  1. Bust − hips = -2 in; hips − bust = 2 in
  2. Bust − waist = 8 in; hips − waist = 10 in
  3. High hip ÷ waist = 1.214 (the split between full and flat high hips is 1.193)
  • The shapes follow the FFIT system used in clothing research and are meant for women’s measurements. Every shape is normal; it only helps with choosing clothes that fit.

How it works

Measure four places with a soft tape, keeping it level and snug: the fullest part of the bust, the narrowest part of the waist, the high hip about 7 inches (18 cm) below the waist, and the widest part of the hips. The calculator compares the differences between them with the FFIT rules, a body shape system from clothing research, and checks the seven shapes in a fixed order; the first one that fits is your shape. It also divides your waist by your hips: the World Health Organization uses this waist-to-hip ratio as a simple sign of fat stored around the middle.

The formula

  • All four measurements in inches (cm ÷ 2.54); B = bust, W = waist, HH = high hip, H = hips
  • Hourglass: B − H ≤ 1 and H − B < 3.6 and (B − W ≥ 9 or H − W ≥ 10)
  • Bottom hourglass: 3.6 ≤ H − B < 10 and H − W ≥ 9 and HH ÷ W < 1.193
  • Top hourglass: 1 < B − H < 10 and B − W ≥ 9
  • Spoon: H − B > 2 and H − W ≥ 7 and HH ÷ W ≥ 1.193
  • Triangle: H − B ≥ 3.6 and H − W < 9; Inverted triangle: B − H ≥ 3.6 and B − W < 9
  • Rectangle: H − B < 3.6 and B − H < 3.6 and B − W < 9 and H − W < 10
  • Waist-to-hip ratio = W ÷ H; for women 0.85 or more means a substantially higher health risk (WHO)

A worked example

Bust 36, waist 24, high hip 32 and hips 36 inches: bust − hips = 0 (at most 1) and bust − waist = 12 (at least 9), so the shape is hourglass. The waist-to-hip ratio is 24 ÷ 36 = 0.67, below the 0.85 cut-off.

Sources

  • Lee, Istook, Nam and Park (2007): Comparison of body shape between USA and Korean women, International Journal of Clothing Science and Technology
  • World Health Organization (2008): Waist circumference and waist-hip ratio, report of a WHO expert consultation

Questions

Why is my shape shown as no clear match?

Some measurements fall between the seven shapes, for example when the hips are much larger than the bust but the waist is not much smaller. The calculator then names the closest shape. Measuring again with the tape level often helps.

Does this work for men?

The seven shapes were built from women's measurements for clothing fit. Men can use the waist-to-hip ratio, where the WHO cut-off is 0.90 instead of 0.85.

Is one body shape healthier than another?

Shape itself is not a health measure. The waist-to-hip ratio is the useful part: more fat around the waist is linked with a higher risk of heart disease and diabetes.

Where exactly is the high hip?

About 7 inches (18 cm) below the natural waist, across the top of the hip bones. It shows whether the hips curve out early (a full high hip) or later.
