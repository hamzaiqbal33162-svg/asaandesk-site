Shoe size conversion: why one foot has many sizes

Shoe size conversion turns a size from one country's system into another's: UK to EU, EU to US, US men's to women's, or any of them from the length of your foot. There is no single world standard, so the same foot is a UK 8, an EU 41, a US men's 9 and a US women's 10 on this page.

The systems differ because they count in different steps from different starting points. UK and US sizes count barleycorns, an old English unit of one third of an inch (about 8.5 mm), so each full size is 8.5 mm longer than the one before. European (EU) sizes count Paris points of two thirds of a centimetre (about 6.7 mm), which is why EU sizes rise in smaller steps. Japan and Mexico label the foot length itself in centimetres, and the Mondopoint system used for ski boots, many sports shoes and military footwear works in millimetres of foot length and width.

Every size on this page is tied to one measurement, the foot length, so the converter first turns your size into a length and then that length into every other system. Brands still make their shoes on their own lasts (the foot-shaped forms shoes are built on), so treat the answer as the best starting point and check the brand's chart before you order.

Shoe size formulas: how to calculate shoe size from foot length

All the formulas start from the foot length L, from the back of the heel to the tip of the longest toe. Because each UK or US size is one barleycorn (1/3 inch), the size number is three times the foot length in inches minus a fixed starting point. Because each EU size is one Paris point (2/3 cm), the EU number is 1.5 times the foot length in centimetres plus a small allowance.

  • UK adults: size = 3 × L (inches) − 23.
  • US men: size = 3 × L (inches) − 22, one size above UK.
  • US women: size = 3 × L (inches) − 21, one size above US men on this page.
  • UK children: size = 3 × L (inches) − 10.75; US children: 3 × L − 9.75. Above 13.5 the count starts again at 1, so subtract 13.
  • EU: size = 1.5 × L (cm) + 2.
  • Japan and Mexico: size = L in cm, to the nearest half centimetre.
  • China (as on most conversion charts): size = 2 × L (cm) − 10.

Example: a foot 25 cm long is 25 ÷ 2.54 = 9.84 inches. UK = 3 × 9.84 − 23 = 6.53, so UK 6.5; US men 7.5 and US women 8.5; EU = 1.5 × 25 + 2 = 39.5, which the converter rounds to 40; Japan 25; China 2 × 25 − 10 = 40.

The extra in the EU rule and the starting points of the UK and US rules exist because a shoe's inside is longer than the foot it fits. The last is usually about 2 size points longer than the foot, so the size labels already include some room for your toes.

Worked examples: EU to UK, EU to US and men to women

EU 40 to UK and US. An EU 40 means a foot of about 25.3 cm, which is a UK 7, a US men's 8 and a US women's 9. An EU 39 comes out as UK 6, US men's 7 and US women's 8, and an EU 42 as UK 8.5, US men's 9.5 and US women's 10.5.

EU 38 for women. An EU 38 is a foot of about 24 cm: a US women's 7.5 and a UK 5.5. Going the other way, a US women's 7 is a UK 5 and an EU 38 as well, which shows why EU sizes cover more than one half size.

US men's to women's. For the same foot, the US women's size here is one size higher: a men's 9.5 is a women's 10.5, and a women's 7 is a men's 6. Some brands use one and a half sizes instead, so a women's 7 may be marked as a men's 5.5 on their chart.

From inches. A foot 10.5 inches long is a UK 8.5, an EU 42, a US men's 9.5, a US women's 10.5, a Japanese 26.5 and a Chinese 43.

Shoe sizes in Pakistan and India

Pakistan and India use UK shoe sizes, the same barleycorn count as Britain, and shops usually label shoes in UK sizes; imported pairs often carry the EU size as well. So when a shop in Lahore or Karachi asks for your size, give the UK number.

To buy from abroad, convert from your UK size. A Pakistani UK 8 is a US men's 9 (US women's 10), an EU 41 (or 42 with some brands), a Japanese 26 and a Chinese 42. A UK 6 for women is a US women's 8 and an EU 39. For children, a UK kids 12 is a US kids 13 and an EU 31.

Because the UK system is not formally standardised, Pakistani and Indian brands, like British ones, can differ by half a size. If you know your size in one brand, compare that brand's chart with the new one in centimetres of foot length, not only in size numbers.

Kids and baby shoe sizes

Children's UK and US sizes are a separate count. Little kids sizes run from about 8 to 13.5; after that the count starts again at 1 for big kids, so a big kids 1 is the half size after 13.5. Baby and toddler sizes start at 0. EU, Japanese and Chinese sizes simply carry on growing with the foot, without a restart.

How to measure your foot at home

  • Measure late in the day, when feet are at their largest, and wear the kind of socks you will wear with the shoes.
  • Put a sheet of paper on a hard floor against a wall. Stand on it with your heel touching the wall and your weight on that foot.
  • Mark the tip of your longest toe; it is not always the big toe. Keep the pen upright so the mark is not pushed outward.
  • Measure from the wall to the mark in centimetres or inches. Do the same for the other foot and use the longer one.
  • Type that length into Foot length. Every size comes out, along with the length in cm, mm and inches.

Shoe shops often use a Brannock device, a metal measuring plate invented by Charles F. Brannock in 1925, which also measures the arch length and width. Mondopoint, set out in the ISO 9407 standard, labels both the foot length and the foot width in millimetres, and the ISO/TS 19407 technical specification describes how the main size systems relate.

Width, fit and common mistakes

  • Length is not everything. Two feet of the same length can need different widths; North American shoes mark width with letters, where D is the usual men's width and B the usual women's, with E and more for wider feet.
  • Mixing men's and women's sizes. US women's sizes are higher than men's for the same foot; UK and EU sizes are the same for both.
  • Comparing the wrong systems. EU 40 is not a UK 40; always say which system a number belongs to.
  • Ignoring the brand's chart. Brands and even models of one brand can differ by a half or whole size. Check the brand's own chart, ideally in centimetres of foot length.
  • Buying children's shoes with no room. A shoe's inside should be longer than the foot; check that the toes are not pressed against the end when the child stands.
  • Measuring only one foot. Most people have one foot slightly longer; fit the longer one.

How it works

Every shoe size stands for a foot length, so the converter turns your size (or your measured foot) into a length and then into each system. UK and US sizes count thirds of an inch, European sizes count steps of two thirds of a centimetre, Japanese and Mexican sizes are simply the foot length in centimetres, and Chinese sizes count half centimetres. Children's UK and US sizes use their own count, which runs up to 13.5 and then starts again at 1 for bigger kids. US women's sizes are one size above men's for the same foot.

The formula

  • L = foot length (heel to the longest toe); sizes are rounded to the nearest half (UK, US, Japan) or whole size (EU, China)
  • UK adults = 3 × L(in) − 23; US men = 3 × L(in) − 22; US women = 3 × L(in) − 21
  • UK children = 3 × L(in) − 10.75; US children = 3 × L(in) − 9.75; from 14 on the count restarts: big kids size = that number − 13
  • EU = 1.5 × L(cm) + 2 (Paris point = 2/3 cm)
  • Japan / Mexico = L(cm); China = 2 × L(cm) − 10

A worked example

A US men's size 9.5 means 3 × L − 22 = 9.5, so L = 31.5 ÷ 3 = 10.5 in (26.67 cm). Then UK = 3 × 10.5 − 23 = 8.5, US women = 10.5, EU = 1.5 × 26.67 + 2 = 42, Japan 26.5 and China 2 × 26.67 − 10 = 43.

Questions

How do I calculate my shoe size in cm?

Measure your foot from the heel to the longest toe in centimetres. That number, rounded to the nearest half, is your Japanese size, and the EU size is 1.5 × the length + 2. A 25 cm foot is a Japanese 25 and an EU 40.

How do I calculate my shoe size in UK sizes?

Measure your foot in inches, multiply by 3 and take away 23, then round to the nearest half. A 10.5 inch foot gives 3 × 10.5 − 23 = 8.5, a UK 8.5.

What is shoe size 9 in EU?

A UK 9 is an EU 43 and a US men's 9 is an EU 41 here. Always check whether the 9 is a UK or a US size.

What is shoe size 40 in UK?

An EU 40 is a UK 7 by this converter's formula, and a US men's 8 or US women's 9. Because EU sizes fall between UK half sizes, a chart that rounds differently may show the neighbouring half size.

What is shoe size 38 in US?

An EU 38 is a US women's 7.5 and a US men's 6.5. In UK sizes it is 5.5.

What is shoe size 42 in UK?

An EU 42 is a UK 8.5, a US men's 9.5 and a US women's 10.5.

What shoe sizes are used in Pakistan and India?

Most shops use UK sizes (often also printed as EU sizes on imported shoes). A UK 8 is about an EU 41 or 42 and a US men's 9.

How do I convert a Pakistani shoe size to US?

Pakistani sizes are UK sizes, so add one for US men's and two for US women's on this page: a UK 8 is a US men's 9 and a US women's 10.

How do I measure my foot length?

Stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall, mark the tip of your longest toe and measure from the wall to the mark. Do both feet, late in the day when feet are largest, and use the longer one.

Why do EU sizes sometimes come out the same for two UK sizes?

EU steps are 2/3 cm and UK steps are 1/3 inch (0.85 cm), and EU sizes are whole numbers, so two neighbouring UK half sizes can share one EU size. Brands also round differently, which is why their charts do not always agree.

What are little kids and big kids sizes?

Children's UK and US sizes count up to 13.5 and then start again at 1. Sizes 8 to 13.5 are often called little kids and 1 to 7 big kids. A big kids size 1 is one half size above 13.5.

Is a US women's size the same as a men's size?

No. For the same foot the US women's size is higher: this converter uses one size (a men's 10 is a women's 11), and some brands use one and a half. UK sizes are the same for men and women.
