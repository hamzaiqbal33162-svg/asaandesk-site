How it works

Profit is the selling price (revenue) minus the cost. The margin is that profit as a share of the selling price, and the markup is the same profit as a share of the cost, so the two always describe the same sale from different ends. Pick which two values you know and the calculator works out the rest. The stock tool multiplies the price by the number of shares and by the share your broker asks you to pay yourself. The currency tool divides the value of the position by the leverage.

The formula

  • Profit = revenue − cost
  • Margin = profit ÷ revenue × 100; markup = profit ÷ cost × 100
  • From cost and margin m: revenue = cost ÷ (1 − m); from revenue and margin: cost = revenue × (1 − m)
  • Stock margin: cash needed = share price × shares × margin requirement
  • Currency margin: money needed = units × exchange rate ÷ leverage

A worked example

Buy an item for $50 and want a 40% margin: the price is 50 ÷ (1 − 0.40) = $83.33. The profit is $33.33, which is 40% of the price but a 66.67% markup on the cost.

Questions

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Both use the same profit. Margin divides it by the selling price, markup by the cost. Selling for $160 what cost $120 is a 25% margin and a 33.33% markup.

Why can a margin not reach 100%?

A 100% margin would mean the cost is zero. From a real cost, every price gives a margin below 100%, however high the price goes, so the calculator asks for a margin below 100% in that case.

Can I enter a loss?

Yes. Type the profit with a minus sign, or enter a cost higher than the revenue. The margin and markup then come out negative.

What does buying stock on margin mean?

You pay part of the price and borrow the rest from your broker. In the US you must pay at least 50% when you buy, and keep at least 25% of the value (often more, by broker rules) as the price changes.

How does leverage change the currency margin?

Leverage of 50:1 means you put up 1/50 of the position, or 2%. Higher leverage needs less money up front but makes every price move count for more, both ways.
