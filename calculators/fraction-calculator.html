What is a fraction, and what does this fraction calculator do?

A fraction is one number divided by another, written as a top number over a bottom number, and a fraction calculator does the arithmetic with them exactly. The bottom number (the denominator) says how many equal parts the whole is cut into, and the top number (the numerator) says how many of those parts you have. In 3/8, a whole is cut into eight equal parts and you hold three of them.

This fraction calculator adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides two fractions or mixed numbers, simplifies a fraction to lowest terms, turns a decimal into a fraction, and turns a fraction into a decimal and a percentage. Every answer comes with the working, so you can check homework, follow the method for a test, or simply get an exact result for a measurement or a share of money.

Three words come up often. A proper fraction has a top number smaller than the bottom one (5/8). An improper fraction has a top number equal to or bigger than the bottom one (13/8). A mixed number is a whole number and a proper fraction written together (1 5/8), which is the same amount as 13/8.

How to use the fraction calculator, step by step

  • Choose a tab: Calculate, Simplify, Decimal to fraction or Fraction to decimal.
  • On Calculate, fill in the First fraction: the Whole number box is optional and is only for mixed numbers, then type the Top number and the Bottom number.
  • Pick the operation: + Add, − Subtract, × Multiply or ÷ Divide.
  • Fill in the Second fraction the same way. For a fraction with whole numbers, such as 2 1/2 + 3, type 3 as the top number and 1 as the bottom number.
  • On Simplify, type the fraction you want to reduce. On Decimal to fraction, type a Decimal number such as 1.375 or -0.125. On Fraction to decimal, type the fraction (with an optional whole number).
  • Read the answer and open the steps to see each stage of the working. Nothing needs a Calculate button: the result changes as you type.

Top and bottom numbers may be decimals, such as 1.5/4; the calculator first multiplies both by 10 or 100 to make them whole and shows that step. A bottom number of 0 is refused, because dividing by zero has no answer.

Each tab shows the answer in the forms people usually need. The fraction is always reduced to lowest terms, and the decimal is exact unless it is too long, in which case it is rounded to 14 significant digits.

How to add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions

Adding fractions with different denominators needs a common bottom number first. The smallest one is the least common multiple (LCM) of the two denominators. For 2/3 + 1/4, the LCM of 3 and 4 is 12, so 2/3 becomes 8/12 and 1/4 becomes 3/12. Now add the tops: 8/12 + 3/12 = 11/12.

Subtracting works the same way. For 7/8 − 1/6, the LCM of 8 and 6 is 24, so the sum becomes 21/24 − 4/24 = 17/24. Multiplying the two denominators (8 × 6 = 48) also gives a common denominator, but the answer then needs more simplifying, which is why the calculator uses the LCM.

Multiplying needs no common denominator: multiply the tops together and the bottoms together. 4/9 × 3/10 = 12/90, which simplifies to 2/15. You can cancel first to keep numbers small: 3 and 9 share a factor of 3, and 4 and 10 share a factor of 2.

Dividing means multiplying by the flipped second fraction, often taught as keep, change, flip. 5/6 ÷ 2/3 = 5/6 × 3/2 = 15/12 = 5/4, or 1 1/4. This works because dividing by 2/3 asks how many two-thirds fit into five-sixths.

Mixed numbers, whole numbers and negative fractions

To calculate fractions with whole numbers or mixed numbers, turn each one into an improper fraction first. Multiply the whole number by the bottom number and add the top: 2 3/4 = (2 × 4 + 3)/4 = 11/4. The calculator writes this step out for you whenever a Whole number box is filled in.

As an example, 1 1/2 × 2 2/3 becomes 3/2 × 8/3 = 24/6 = 4. A plain whole number is a fraction over 1, so 5 is 5/1 and 3/4 × 5 = 15/4 = 3 3/4.

For a negative mixed number such as −2 3/4, type −2 as the whole number and keep the top number positive; the minus sign applies to the whole amount, giving −11/4. A negative top number on its own, such as −3/4, works too.

How to simplify a fraction to lowest terms

A fraction is in lowest terms when the top and bottom numbers share no factor except 1. To get there in one step, divide both by their greatest common factor (GCF). The calculator finds the GCF with Euclid's algorithm: divide the bigger number by the smaller, keep the remainder, and repeat until the remainder is 0.

For 84/126: 126 ÷ 84 leaves a remainder of 42, and 84 ÷ 42 leaves 0, so the GCF is 42. Dividing top and bottom by 42 gives 2/3. Simplifying never changes the value of a fraction; 84/126 and 2/3 mark exactly the same point on a number line.

The Simplify tab starts with 36/48, whose GCF is 12, so it reduces to 3/4. If the answer says the fraction is already in lowest terms, the GCF is 1 and nothing can be cancelled.

Fractions, decimals and percentages

To convert a fraction to a decimal, divide the top number by the bottom number. To convert a fraction to a percentage, multiply that decimal by 100. To go from a decimal to a fraction, write the digits over 10, 100, 1,000 and so on, one zero for each digit after the point, and simplify: 0.625 = 625/1,000 = 5/8.

Fractions for inches, tape measures and woodworking

Imperial tape measures and rulers mark inches in halves, quarters, eighths and sixteenths, so cutting lists are full of fractions. Adding 2 3/8 in and 1 5/16 in means writing both in sixteenths: 38/16 + 21/16 = 59/16 = 3 11/16 in. The calculator does this with the LCM, then gives the mixed number you can read off the tape.

Since 1959 the inch has been defined as exactly 25.4 mm, so any fraction of an inch converts to millimetres exactly. 3 11/16 in is 3.6875 × 25.4 = 93.6625 mm.

Worked example in rupees, and common mistakes

Two families in Lahore share a house and a monthly electricity bill of PKR 18,000. The ground floor pays 2/5 and the first floor pays 3/8; what fraction is left for the shop on the corner? Add 2/5 + 3/8 with the LCM 40: 16/40 + 15/40 = 31/40. The shop's share is 1 − 31/40 = 9/40, and 9/40 of PKR 18,000 is 18,000 × 9 ÷ 40 = PKR 4,050. As a check, 2/5 is PKR 7,200 and 3/8 is PKR 6,750, and 7,200 + 6,750 + 4,050 = 18,000.

  • Adding the bottom numbers: 1/2 + 1/3 is not 2/5. Rewrite both over a common denominator first (3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6).
  • Forgetting to flip when dividing: only the second fraction is turned upside down, never the first.
  • Mixing a whole number into the top: 2 3/4 is 11/4, not 23/4.
  • Three or more fractions: the calculator takes two at a time. Work out the first two, then type that result as the first fraction and add the third. For 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6, the first step gives 5/6 and the second gives 1.
  • Rounding too early: keep fractions exact until the last step, then convert to a decimal once.

How it works

Pick what you want to do, type the top and bottom numbers (and a whole number for a mixed number such as 2 3/4), and the answer appears as you type, in lowest terms, as a mixed number and as a decimal. The steps show the same working you would write on paper: a common denominator for adding and subtracting, tops times tops and bottoms times bottoms for multiplying, flipping the second fraction for dividing, then dividing by the greatest common factor. Numbers of any length are worked out exactly.

The formula

  • a/b + c/d = (a × L/b + c × L/d) ÷ L, where L is the least common multiple of b and d
  • a/b − c/d = (a × L/b − c × L/d) ÷ L
  • a/b × c/d = (a × c) ÷ (b × d)
  • a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c = (a × d) ÷ (b × c), when c is not 0
  • Lowest terms: divide top and bottom by their greatest common factor (Euclid's algorithm)
  • Mixed number w n/d = (w × d + n) ÷ d; a decimal with k digits after the point = (decimal × 10^k) ÷ 10^k

A worked example

3/4 + 5/6: the least common multiple of 4 and 6 is 12, so 3/4 = 9/12 and 5/6 = 10/12. Then 9/12 + 10/12 = 19/12, which is already in lowest terms. As a mixed number, 19 ÷ 12 = 1 remainder 7, so the answer is 1 7/12, or about 1.5833333333333 as a decimal.

Questions

How do I type a mixed number like 2 3/4?

Put 2 in the whole number box, 3 on top and 4 at the bottom. For a negative mixed number such as −2 3/4, type −2 as the whole number and keep the top number positive.

How do you add fractions with different denominators?

Rewrite both fractions over the least common multiple of their bottom numbers, then add the top numbers. For 1/4 + 1/6 the LCM is 12, so the sum is 3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12. The calculator shows that LCM step before adding.

How do I calculate fractions with whole numbers?

Write the whole number as a fraction over 1, or use the Whole number box for a mixed number. For 4 − 2/3, type 4 as the top and 1 as the bottom of the first fraction; the answer is 10/3, shown as the mixed number 3 1/3.

Can the fraction calculator work with 3 fractions?

It takes two fractions at a time, which keeps the steps clear. Work out the first pair, then type that result as the first fraction and the third fraction as the second. The final answer is the same as doing all three at once.

How do I simplify a fraction?

Divide the top and bottom by their greatest common factor. The Simplify tab finds it for you: 36/48 has a GCF of 12, so it becomes 3/4. A mixed number can be simplified too by filling in the whole number box.

How do I convert a fraction to a percentage?

Divide the top by the bottom and multiply by 100. On the Fraction to decimal tab the percentage is shown under the decimal, so 3/8 appears as 0.375 and 37.5%.

Can I use decimals inside a fraction?

Yes. A fraction such as 1.5/4 is first scaled to whole numbers, here 15/40, and the steps show that. The answer is still exact.

Why is my decimal shown as a long fraction?

A decimal you type is treated as exact: 0.3333 is 3333/10000. If you meant a repeating decimal such as 0.333…, the result tells you its fraction (1/3) in a note.

What is the formula for fractions?

There is one rule per operation: a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd, a/b × c/d = ac/bd and a/b ÷ c/d = ad/bc. Subtraction is like addition with a minus sign. The How it works section lists the versions with the least common multiple, which give smaller numbers.

How do I do fractions on a scientific calculator?

Many scientific calculators have a fraction key, but its label and behaviour differ from model to model, so check the manual for yours. Here you can type the top and bottom numbers in separate boxes and see the working, which a handheld calculator does not show.

How big can the numbers be?

As long as you like. Every tool works with exact whole-number maths, so a fraction with 30-digit numbers is added just as exactly as 1/2 + 1/3. Only the decimal is rounded, to 14 significant digits.
