Steps

  1. Start with the first digit: 9.
  2. 4 goes into 9 2 times: 2 × 4 = 8, and 9 − 8 = 1.
  3. Bring down the 3 to make 13.
  4. 4 goes into 13 3 times: 3 × 4 = 12, and 13 − 12 = 1.
  5. Bring down the 5 to make 15.
  6. 4 goes into 15 3 times: 3 × 4 = 12, and 15 − 12 = 3.
  7. No digits are left to bring down, so the quotient is 233 and the remainder is 3.
  8. Check: 4 × 233 + 3 = 935.

How it works

Type the dividend (the number being divided) and the divisor. The calculator works the division the way it is written on paper: take the first digits that the divisor goes into, write how many times it goes, multiply, subtract, bring down the next digit, and repeat. The layout shows the working in columns and the steps say each move in words. Decimals are first made whole by moving both decimal points, and under More options the division can carry on past the decimal point.

The formula

  • dividend = divisor × quotient + remainder, with the remainder smaller than the divisor
  • Each cycle: bring down a digit, quotient digit = how many whole times the divisor fits, subtract that many divisors
  • With decimals: multiply both numbers by 10, 100, … until they are whole; the quotient does not change
  • Mixed number = quotient and remainder ÷ divisor; the decimal is the remainder divided on

A worked example

935 ÷ 4: 4 goes into 9 two times (8), leaving 1; bring down the 3 to make 13, 4 goes in three times (12), leaving 1; bring down the 5 to make 15, 4 goes in three times (12), leaving 3. So 935 ÷ 4 = 233 R3 = 233 3/4 = 233.75. Check: 4 × 233 + 3 = 935.

Questions

What do dividend, divisor, quotient and remainder mean?

In 935 ÷ 4 = 233 R3, 935 is the dividend, 4 is the divisor, 233 is the quotient (how many whole times 4 fits) and 3 is the remainder (what is left over).

How do I divide decimals by long division?

Move the decimal point of both numbers the same number of places to the right until both are whole. 3.5 ÷ 4 becomes 35 ÷ 40; the answer, 0.875, is the same. The calculator does this for you and says so in the steps.

How do I get decimal places instead of a remainder?

Open More options and choose how many decimal places to work out. The division then writes a decimal point, keeps bringing down zeros, and shows the digits it found and the rounded answer.

What happens with negative numbers?

The division is done on the sizes and the sign is put on the answer, so −100 ÷ 7 = −14 R2, which is the mixed number −14 2/7. A note also gives the form with a remainder that is never negative: −100 = 7 × (−15) + 5.
