How it works

Enter the first day of your last period and your usual cycle length. Ovulation is placed about 14 days before your next period. Conception can happen from two days before ovulation until the day after it, when the egg is still alive, so those six days are shown as the most likely conception days. Sex in the five days before ovulation can also lead to a pregnancy, because sperm survive for several days. The same pattern is repeated for your next six cycles.

The formula

  • Ovulation day O = first day of last period + (cycle length − 14) days
  • Most likely conception days = O − 2 to O + 3 days
  • Ovulation window = O − 2 to O + 2 days; fertile days for sex = O − 5 to O + 2 days
  • Pregnancy test from O + 9 days; next period = first day of last period + cycle length
  • Due date if you conceive this cycle = first day of last period + 280 + (cycle length − 28) days

A worked example

A period that started on October 20, 2026 with a 35-day cycle puts ovulation on October 20 + 21 = November 10. The most likely conception days are November 8 to 13, the next period is due on November 24, and a baby conceived this cycle would be due around August 3, 2027.

Questions

How often should we have sex to conceive?

Every two to three days through the cycle gives a good chance without needing exact timing. If you prefer to time it, focus on the fertile days shown, especially the two days before ovulation.

Is this different from the ovulation calculator?

It uses the same cycle maths. This page puts the days when conception itself can happen first (ovulation minus two days to the day after), while the ovulation calculator leads with the ovulation day and window.

How long does it usually take to get pregnant?

Most couples who try regularly conceive within a year, many within the first few months. If you are under 35 and have not conceived after a year, or 35 or older after six months, it is worth seeing a doctor.

What if my cycles are irregular?

Then the dates here are rough. Use your average cycle length, and consider ovulation test kits or tracking body temperature to find your fertile days more exactly.
