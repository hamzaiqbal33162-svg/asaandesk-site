What horsepower is and what it measures

Horsepower is a unit of power: how fast work is done or energy is used. A horsepower calculator works out that rate from a force, the distance it moves and the time it takes, or converts a power figure between watts, kilowatts, the several kinds of horsepower and BTU per hour.

The unit comes from the steam-engine maker James Watt, who wanted to sell engines by comparing them with the mill horses they replaced. From measurements of horses turning mill wheels he settled, in the 1780s, on 33,000 foot-pounds of work per minute, which is 550 foot-pounds per second: lifting 550 pounds by one foot every second. In the SI system the unit of power is the watt (one joule per second), named after him, and one mechanical horsepower is 745.7 W.

Horsepower is not the same as force or torque. Force is a push or pull; torque is a twisting force; work is force times distance; power is work per second. Two engines can produce the same torque, yet the one that delivers it at higher rpm makes more horsepower.

The kinds of horsepower and their values

Several trades defined their own horsepower, and they differ by up to 1.4%, except boiler horsepower, which is a different idea altogether. Car makers in the US and UK quote mechanical horsepower (hp or bhp); much of Europe and Asia uses metric horsepower, called PS in German, CV in Italian and pk in Dutch. Electric motors are rated in electrical horsepower, and steam boilers in boiler horsepower, the heat needed to boil 34.5 lb of water at 212°F every hour.

Worked examples: stairs, a crane and a water tank

Running up stairs. A 75 kg person climbing 3 m in 4 seconds produces 551.62 W, which is 0.7397 hp or exactly 0.75 PS (75 kgf × 3 m ÷ 4 s = 56.25 kgf·m/s, three quarters of 75). For comparison, a healthy person can briefly reach about 1.2 hp but keep up only about 0.1 hp for long periods.

Hoist or lift. Raising 1,000 kgf (one tonne) by 20 m in 30 seconds takes 6,537.77 W, or 8.7673 hp, before any losses in gears and cables. Lifting the same load in 15 seconds would need twice the power.

Rooftop water tank. Pumping 1,000 liters of water (1,000 kgf) up 12 m in 20 minutes needs 98.07 W of water power, only 0.1315 hp. Pumps and pipes waste a good share of the motor's power as friction and heat, which is why the motor fitted is several times larger than this figure.

Imperial units. Dragging a 200 lbf load 50 ft in 10 seconds is 1,355.82 W, or 1.8182 hp. Check: 200 × 50 ÷ 10 = 1,000 ft·lbf per second, and 1,000 ÷ 550 = 1.8182.

Horsepower from torque and rpm, and from an electric motor

For anything that turns, power = torque × angular speed. In US units that becomes hp = torque (lb·ft) × rpm ÷ 5,252, where 5,252 is 33,000 ÷ 2π. In metric units, kW = torque (N·m) × rpm ÷ 9,549. An engine making 250 N·m at 4,000 rpm produces 250 × 4,000 ÷ 9,549 = 104.72 kW, which the converter turns into 140.432 hp. This is also why torque and horsepower curves cross at 5,252 rpm when torque is plotted in lb·ft.

Electric motor nameplates give output power, the mechanical power at the shaft. The electrical power drawn is higher because no motor is 100% efficient; for a rough check, input watts ≈ volts × amps (times the power factor for AC motors), and output ≈ input × the efficiency on the plate. A 0.5 hp motor delivers 372.85 W at the shaft.

Engine horsepower in car brochures is usually brake horsepower (bhp), measured at the crankshaft. Wheel horsepower (whp), measured on a chassis dynamometer, is lower because the gearbox, differential and tires take some of it. To estimate a car's horsepower from a quarter-mile time or trap speed, use the Engine Horsepower Calculator linked below.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Use force, not mass, in the first tab. With kilograms, choose kgf; with pounds, choose lbf. In newtons, a mass's weight is its mass in kg × 9.80665.
  • Count only the vertical distance when lifting. Carrying a load up a ramp takes the same work as lifting it straight up the same height (ignoring friction).
  • Do not mix hp and PS. A car listed at 150 PS has 147.948 hp, a small but real difference when comparing specifications.
  • Air conditioner sizes quoted as tons or BTU/h are cooling capacity, not the power the unit draws; read the input watts on the label for running cost.
  • Boiler horsepower is a heat output rating. Converting it to mechanical horsepower gives a large number (1 boiler hp is 13.155 hp) that says nothing about shaft power.

How it works

Power is how fast work is done. When a force moves something over a distance, the work is the force times the distance (in joules), and the power is that work divided by the time it took (in watts, joules per second). Enter the force, the distance and the time in any of the units offered and the calculator gives the power in watts and kilowatts, in the four kinds of horsepower and in BTU per hour. The second tab converts any amount of power from one unit to another and lists it in every unit.

The formula

  • Power (W) = force (N) × distance (m) ÷ time (s)
  • 1 mechanical hp = 550 ft·lbf/s = 745.69987 W
  • 1 metric hp (PS) = 75 kgf·m/s = 735.49875 W; 1 electrical hp = 746 W; 1 boiler hp = 9,809.5 W
  • 1 BTU/h = 0.29307107 W (International Table BTU); 1 lbf = 4.4482216 N; 1 kgf = 9.80665 N
  • From torque: hp = torque (lb·ft) × rpm ÷ 5,252; kW = torque (N·m) × rpm ÷ 9,549

A worked example

A 75 kg person running up a 3 m flight of stairs in 4 seconds lifts 75 kgf (735.5 N) through 3 m: 2,206.5 J of work, and 2,206.5 ÷ 4 = 551.62 W of power. That is 0.7397 mechanical hp, or exactly 0.75 metric hp.

Questions

How do I calculate horsepower?

Multiply the force by the distance it moves to get the work, divide by the time to get watts, then divide by 745.7 for mechanical horsepower. In US units, divide foot-pounds per second by 550.

How is horsepower calculated from torque and rpm?

hp = torque in lb·ft × rpm ÷ 5,252, or kW = torque in N·m × rpm ÷ 9,549. For 250 N·m at 4,000 rpm that is 104.72 kW, about 140.4 hp.

How many watts is 1 horsepower?

One mechanical horsepower is 745.7 W and one metric horsepower is 735.5 W, so 100 kW is about 134.1 hp or 136 PS.

How do I calculate horsepower from kW?

Multiply kilowatts by 1.341 for mechanical horsepower, or by 1.36 for metric horsepower (PS). A 150 kW engine makes 201.153 hp.

Why are there several kinds of horsepower?

Different trades defined their own. Mechanical (imperial) horsepower is 550 foot-pounds per second, about 745.7 W, and is used for cars in the US and UK. Metric horsepower (PS, CV) is 75 kilogram-force metres per second, about 735.5 W, and is common in Europe and Asia. Electric motors use 746 W, and boiler horsepower measures steam output at 9,809.5 W.

How much horsepower does a horse have?

A horse can sustain about 1 hp for long periods, which is what Watt's unit describes, but it can briefly peak at close to 15 hp.

What is the difference between work and power?

Work is the energy used to move something (force × distance, in joules). Power is how quickly that work is done (work ÷ time, in watts). Lifting a box slowly or quickly takes the same work, but doing it quickly needs more power.

How do I calculate the horsepower of an electric motor?

The nameplate horsepower is its output. To estimate it, multiply the input watts (volts × amps, times the power factor for AC) by the efficiency, then divide by 746.

Can I convert BTU per hour to horsepower?

Yes. Use the Convert power units tab: 1 hp is about 2,544 BTU/h, and 1 boiler hp is about 33,471 BTU/h.
