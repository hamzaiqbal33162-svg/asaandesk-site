Steps

  1. Force = 500 N
  2. Distance = 10 m; time = 5 s
  3. Work = force × distance = 500 N × 10 m = 5,000 J
  4. Power = work ÷ time = 5,000 J ÷ 5 s = 1,000 W
  5. Mechanical horsepower = watts ÷ 745.69987 = 1.341
  • 1 mechanical hp = 550 foot-pounds per second; 1 metric hp = 75 kilogram-force metres per second; 1 electrical hp = 746 W; 1 boiler hp = 9,809.5 W.

How it works

Power is how fast work is done. When a force moves something over a distance, the work is the force times the distance (in joules), and the power is that work divided by the time it took (in watts, joules per second). Enter the force, the distance and the time in any of the units offered and the calculator gives the power in watts and kilowatts, in the four kinds of horsepower and in BTU per hour. The second tab converts any amount of power from one unit to another and lists it in every unit.

The formula

  • Power (W) = force (N) × distance (m) ÷ time (s)
  • 1 mechanical hp = 550 ft·lbf/s = 745.69987 W
  • 1 metric hp (PS) = 75 kgf·m/s = 735.49875 W; 1 electrical hp = 746 W; 1 boiler hp = 9,809.5 W
  • 1 BTU/h = 0.29307107 W (International Table BTU); 1 lbf = 4.4482216 N; 1 kgf = 9.80665 N

A worked example

Pushing with 500 N over 10 m in 5 seconds is 500 × 10 = 5,000 J of work, and 5,000 ÷ 5 = 1,000 W of power, which is 1.341 mechanical hp or 1.3596 metric hp. James Watt's own unit is 550 pounds lifted 1 foot every second: 550 lbf over 1 yard in 3 seconds is exactly 1 hp, about 745.7 W.

Questions

Why are there several kinds of horsepower?

Different trades defined their own. Mechanical (imperial) horsepower is 550 foot-pounds per second, about 745.7 W, and is used for cars in the US and UK. Metric horsepower (PS, CV) is 75 kilogram-force metres per second, about 735.5 W, and is common in Europe and Asia. Electric motors use 746 W, and boiler horsepower measures steam output at 9,809.5 W.

How many watts is 1 horsepower?

One mechanical horsepower is 745.7 W and one metric horsepower is 735.5 W, so 100 kW is about 134.1 hp or 136 PS.

What is the difference between work and power?

Work is the energy used to move something (force × distance, in joules). Power is how quickly that work is done (work ÷ time, in watts). Lifting a box slowly or quickly takes the same work, but doing it quickly needs more power.

Can I convert BTU per hour to horsepower?

Yes. Use the Convert power units tab: 1 hp is about 2,544 BTU/h, and 1 boiler hp is about 33,471 BTU/h.
