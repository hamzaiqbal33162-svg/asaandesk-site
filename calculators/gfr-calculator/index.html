What GFR is and what this GFR calculator estimates

GFR, the glomerular filtration rate, is the volume of blood the kidneys' tiny filters (glomeruli) clean every minute. It is the best single measure of kidney function. Results are given in mL/min/1.73 m², which means millilitres a minute scaled to an average adult body surface of 1.73 m², so people of different sizes can be compared.

Measuring GFR directly needs special tracer tests, so on a normal blood test the lab reports an estimated GFR (eGFR) worked out from serum creatinine. Creatinine is a waste product of muscle; when the kidneys filter less, it builds up in the blood and eGFR falls.

This GFR calculator gives the eGFR from creatinine, age and sex with the race-free CKD-EPI 2021 equation, compares three older equations, and places the result on the KDIGO stage scale. For children it uses height instead. It is an estimate to help you read your report, not a diagnosis; only your doctor can judge it with your other results.

How to use this calculator, step by step

GFR stages and the normal range

Doctors stage kidney function with the KDIGO GFR categories, which were kept in the 2024 KDIGO guideline. A low eGFR has to last at least 3 months before it counts as chronic kidney disease (CKD).

KDIGO also grades albumin in the urine: A1 below 30 mg/g (3 mg/mmol), A2 30 to 300 mg/g and A3 above 300 mg/g. Both numbers together set the risk, so a person with an eGFR of 70 and A3 albuminuria may need more care than someone at 55 with none.

What is a normal GFR? The National Kidney Foundation gives average eGFR values from a large U.S. survey of about 116 at ages 20 to 29, 99 at 40 to 49, 85 at 60 to 69 and 75 at 70 and over. GFR falls slowly with age, so a value in the 60s can be ordinary for an older adult with no other signs of kidney disease.

The GFR formula for men and women

CKD-EPI 2021 uses creatinine (Scr, in mg/dL), age and sex: eGFR = 142 × min(Scr/κ, 1)^α × max(Scr/κ, 1)^−1.200 × 0.9938^age, times 1.012 for women. κ is 0.9 for men and 0.7 for women, and α is −0.302 for men and −0.241 for women. The min and max terms let the curve bend at the typical creatinine for each sex.

For a man with creatinine below 0.9, only the min term changes the result; above 0.9, only the max term does. For a woman the switch point is 0.7. The 0.9938^age term lowers the estimate by about 0.6% for each year of age.

Children use the bedside Schwartz formula: eGFR = 0.413 × height in cm ÷ creatinine. Height stands in for muscle mass, which is why age is not needed.

Worked examples

Man, default. Creatinine 1.0 mg/dL at 50: Scr/κ = 1.0 ÷ 0.9 = 1.111. The max term is 1.111^−1.2 = 0.881 and 0.9938^50 = 0.733, so eGFR = 142 × 0.881 × 0.733 = 91.7, stage G1.

Woman in Pakistan. A 62-year-old woman in Islamabad has a creatinine of 1.3 mg/dL. Scr/κ = 1.3 ÷ 0.7 = 1.857, so the max term is 1.857^−1.2 = 0.476; 0.9938^62 = 0.680. eGFR = 142 × 0.476 × 0.680 × 1.012 = 46.5, stage G3a. If a repeat test after 3 months gives a similar figure, her doctor would treat it as CKD stage 3a.

Report in µmol/L. A 45-year-old man's report says 110 µmol/L. Divide by 88.4 to get 1.24 mg/dL; choose µmol/L in the calculator and it converts for you, giving an eGFR of 72.8 (G2).

Child. A child 110 cm tall with a creatinine of 0.5 mg/dL: 0.413 × 110 ÷ 0.5 = 90.9, stage G1.

Creatinine, cystatin C, BUN and Cockcroft-Gault

Creatinine depends on muscle, so eGFR can mislead in very muscular people, people with little muscle or an amputation, and during a sudden illness (acute kidney injury), when creatinine has not caught up. A second blood marker, cystatin C, is less affected by muscle; the 2021 study also published cystatin C and combined equations, and KDIGO advises using cystatin C when a creatinine eGFR may be unreliable. This calculator does not use cystatin C.

Blood urea nitrogen (BUN) is not part of today's eGFR equations. Doctors read BUN and the BUN-to-creatinine ratio alongside eGFR, for example to spot dehydration.

The Cockcroft-Gault formula (1976) estimates creatinine clearance in mL/min rather than eGFR, and uses weight: (140 − age) × weight in kg ÷ (72 × creatinine), × 0.85 for women. For the 62-year-old woman above, at 60 kg, that is 78 × 60 ÷ 93.6 × 0.85 = 42.5 mL/min. Many medicine labels still give doses by Cockcroft-Gault, so pharmacists may use it alongside eGFR.

Pakistan and other practical notes

Diabetes and high blood pressure are the commonest causes of chronic kidney disease. The IDF Diabetes Atlas (2025) estimates that about 34.5 million adults in Pakistan were living with diabetes in 2024, an age-standardised rate of 31.4%, one of the highest in the world. Anyone with diabetes or high blood pressure should have creatinine (eGFR) and urine albumin checked at the intervals their doctor sets, because early kidney disease causes no symptoms.

Before a CT scan with contrast, radiology teams check eGFR. Joint ACR and NKF guidance (2020) recommends preventive intravenous fluids only below an eGFR of 30, or with acute kidney injury, and says a needed contrast scan should not be withheld just because of kidney risk.

Tips for an accurate eGFR

  • Copy the unit exactly: 1.2 mg/dL and 1.2 µmol/L are very different. The calculator warns when a number looks like the wrong unit.
  • Avoid hard exercise and a very large meat meal in the day before the test; both can raise creatinine for a while.
  • Tell your doctor about medicines such as trimethoprim or cimetidine and creatine supplements, which can raise creatinine without harming the kidneys.
  • Compare results calculated with the same equation; switching from MDRD to CKD-EPI 2021 can move the number by several points.
  • Pregnancy, acute illness and extremes of body size make eGFR less reliable; ask whether a measured GFR or cystatin C test is needed.

How it works

Your glomerular filtration rate (GFR) is how much blood your kidneys clean each minute, scaled to an average body size of 1.73 m². It cannot be measured from a normal blood test, so labs estimate it (eGFR) from creatinine, a waste product that builds up in the blood when the kidneys slow down. Enter the creatinine value and unit from your report, your age and your sex. The first result uses the CKD-EPI 2021 equation, which does not use race and is the one recommended for adults today. The CKD-EPI 2009, MDRD and Mayo equations are shown under it so you can compare with older reports. For anyone under 18, the Children tab uses the bedside Schwartz equation, which needs height instead of age. The stage follows the KDIGO GFR categories.

The formula

  • Creatinine (mg/dL) = creatinine (µmol/L) ÷ 88.4
  • CKD-EPI 2021: eGFR = 142 × min(Scr/κ, 1)^α × max(Scr/κ, 1)^−1.200 × 0.9938^age × 1.012 if female (κ = 0.7 women, 0.9 men; α = −0.241 women, −0.302 men)
  • CKD-EPI 2009: eGFR = C × min(Scr/κ, 1)^α × max(Scr/κ, 1)^−1.209 × 0.993^age (C = 144 women, 141 men, 166 Black women, 163 Black men; α = −0.329 women, −0.411 men)
  • MDRD (4 variables): eGFR = 175 × Scr^−1.154 × age^−0.203 × 0.742 if female × 1.212 if Black
  • Mayo quadratic: eGFR = e^(1.911 + 5.249/Scr − 2.114/Scr² − 0.00686 × age − 0.205 if female), with any Scr below 0.8 counted as 0.8
  • Children (bedside Schwartz): eGFR = 0.413 × height in cm ÷ Scr
  • Stages (KDIGO): G1 90 or more, G2 60 to 89, G3a 45 to 59, G3b 30 to 44, G4 15 to 29, G5 below 15

A worked example

A 50-year-old man with a creatinine of 0.9 mg/dL: for men κ = 0.9, so Scr/κ = 1 and both min and max terms equal 1. eGFR = 142 × 0.9938^50 = 142 × 0.733 ≈ 104.0 mL/min/1.73 m², stage G1. The same blood test gives 99.2 with CKD-EPI 2009, 89.3 with MDRD and 120.3 with the Mayo equation.

Questions

What is GFR in a blood test?

It is your estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR), worked out by the lab from your creatinine, age and sex. It shows how well your kidneys filter blood, in mL/min/1.73 m². Higher is better, and below 60 for 3 months or more suggests chronic kidney disease.

What is a normal GFR?

For young adults it is usually around 100 to 120 mL/min/1.73 m², and it falls slowly with age; average values are about 99 at 40 to 49 and 75 at 70 and over. A value of 60 or more is normal for most people unless there are other signs of kidney damage.

How do I calculate GFR from creatinine?

Put your creatinine (mg/dL), age and sex into the CKD-EPI 2021 equation, or enter them above. For a 50-year-old man with 1.0 mg/dL it gives 142 × 0.881 × 0.733 = 91.7 mL/min/1.73 m².

Why does the calculator show four different numbers?

Each equation was built from a different group of patients, so they give somewhat different estimates from the same blood test. The CKD-EPI 2021 result is the one to use today. The others are there because older lab reports and studies use them.

Why does the 2021 equation leave out race?

The older equations raised the result for Black patients, which could delay diagnosis and care. In 2021 the US kidney societies replaced them with the CKD-EPI 2021 equation, which works without race and is about as accurate for everyone.

Can I calculate GFR with weight or from BUN?

Today's eGFR equations do not use weight or BUN. The older Cockcroft-Gault formula uses weight to estimate creatinine clearance, often for medicine doses, and BUN is read alongside eGFR rather than used to calculate it.

Which equation is used for children?

The bedside Schwartz equation, made for children and teenagers aged about 1 to 17. It uses height instead of age because a growing child's muscle mass, and so their creatinine, depends mostly on their size. Babies under 1 year normally have a lower GFR, so ask a paediatrician.

Can one blood test show kidney disease?

No. Creatinine changes with muscle mass, diet, a large meat meal, hard exercise, some medicines and short illnesses. Doctors look at repeated tests over at least 3 months, a urine test for albumin, and sometimes a cystatin C test before they diagnose kidney disease.

What GFR is needed for a CT scan with contrast?

There is no single cut-off that forbids a scan. ACR and NKF guidance from 2020 recommends preventive fluids for an eGFR below 30 or acute kidney injury, and leaves 30 to 44 to the doctor's judgement. Your radiology team will decide with your full history.

Is there a GFR calculator for Pakistan?

This one works anywhere: Pakistani labs, like labs elsewhere, report creatinine in mg/dL or µmol/L, and you just pick the unit. The equations and KDIGO stages are the same international standards that kidney specialists use.
