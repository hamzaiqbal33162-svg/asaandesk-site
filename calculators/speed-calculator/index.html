What the speed calculator does

This speed calculator solves the speed, distance and time triangle. Give any two and it finds the third: speed from a distance and a time, distance from a speed and a time, or travel time from a distance and a speed. A fourth tab converts a speed between 26 units, such as km/h to mph or knots to m/s.

Speed tells you how far something goes in a unit of time. In physics the SI unit is metres per second (m/s); on roads people use kilometres per hour or miles per hour, at sea and in the air knots, and runners often think in pace, minutes per kilometre or per mile. The calculator shows all of these together.

How to use the speed calculator

  • Pick a tab: Find speed, Find distance, Find time, or Convert units.
  • Distance: type the number and choose from millimetres up to light-years, including miles, feet, yards and nautical miles.
  • Speed: type the number and choose one of the 26 units, such as km/h, mph, m/s, knots or ft/s.
  • Time: use the hours, minutes and seconds boxes; leave a box empty for 0 and use decimals such as 4.4 seconds when needed.
  • Choose the unit for the answer with "Show the speed in" or "Show the distance in". The steps show the sum in metres and seconds and the final conversion.

The speed formula and the speed triangle

The speed formula is speed = distance ÷ time, written v = d ÷ t. Rearranged, distance = speed × time and time = distance ÷ speed. The speed triangle puts distance on top and speed and time underneath: cover the one you want and the other two show you whether to multiply or divide.

Units must match. A distance in kilometres and a time in hours give km/h; metres and seconds give m/s. To change km/h to m/s divide by 3.6, and to change m/s to km/h multiply by 3.6. One mile per hour is exactly 0.44704 m/s, and one knot is one nautical mile (1,852 m) per hour.

Worked examples: cycling, driving and running

  • Speed from distance and time: a cyclist rides 42 km in 1 h 45 min. That is 42,000 m ÷ 6,300 s = 6.67 m/s, which is 24 km/h, 14.91 mph or a pace of 2:30 per km.
  • Distance from speed and time: a car holds 120 km/h for 2 h 20 min and covers 280 km, or 173.98 miles.
  • Time from distance and speed: 375 km at an average of 100 km/h takes 13,500 seconds, which is 3 h 45 min.
  • Running pace: 10 km in 52 min 30 s is 11.43 km/h, a pace of 5:15 per km or 8:26.9 per mile.
  • Very fast: 1.5 km in 4.4 seconds is 1,227.27 km/h, close to the speed of sound in air.

Average speed, speed and velocity

Average speed is the total distance divided by the total time, stops included. If a 120 km trip takes 2 hours including a half-hour break, the average is 60 km/h even if you drove at 80 km/h while moving. That is the figure to use for travel times, and it is why the calculator's time answer has no stops built in.

Speed and velocity are not quite the same in physics. Speed is how fast something moves; velocity is speed in a given direction, so it is a vector. A car driving round a roundabout at a steady 30 km/h has a constant speed but a changing velocity. For straight-line motion the two numbers are the same.

Speed in physics: graphs, acceleration, sound and light

  • Distance-time graph: the slope (rise ÷ run) is the speed; a straight line means a steady speed and a steeper line a faster one.
  • Speed-time graph: the slope is the acceleration and the area under the line is the distance travelled.
  • With acceleration: final speed v = u + a × t, where u is the starting speed, a the acceleration and t the time.
  • Waves: wave speed = frequency × wavelength (v = f × λ).
  • Sound travels at about 343 m/s in dry air at 20 °C, faster in warm air and much faster in water or steel.
  • Light in a vacuum travels at exactly 299,792,458 m/s; the SI defines the metre from it. A light-year is the distance light covers in 365.25 days, and the calculator gives that time as 8,766 hours.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Convert minutes properly: 1 h 15 min is 1.25 hours, not 1.15. Typing hours and minutes in their own boxes avoids the mistake.
  • Do not average two speeds for a round trip. Driving there at 60 km/h and back at 40 km/h averages 48 km/h, not 50, because you spend longer at the slower speed. Work out each leg's time, add them, and divide the whole distance by the total time.
  • Check the mile: a land mile is 1,609.344 m, a nautical mile 1,852 m.
  • For runners, pace and speed are opposites: a lower pace (minutes per km) is a higher speed.
  • For car speed from gears or tyres, see the Tire Size Calculator; a speedometer can read differently after a tyre change.

How it works

Speed, distance and time are tied together: speed is the distance covered divided by the time it took. Pick what you want to find and give the other two, each in the unit you have; the calculator turns everything into metres and seconds, does the sum, and shows the answer in the unit you choose, with the steps. A speed is also given in metres per second, km/h, mph and knots and, for everyday speeds, as a pace per kilometre and per mile. The last tab converts a speed between any two of 26 units, from millimetres per hour to the speed of light.

The formula

  • Speed = distance ÷ time
  • Distance = speed × time
  • Time = distance ÷ speed
  • 1 mph = 0.44704 m/s exactly; 1 km/h = 1,000 ÷ 3,600 m/s; 1 knot = 1,852 ÷ 3,600 m/s
  • Speed of light c = 299,792,458 m/s; 1 light-year = c × 365.25 days

A worked example

A car covers 250 km at 90 km/h: time = 250 ÷ 90 = 2.7778 hours = 10,000 seconds, which is 2 h 46 min 40 s. At 65 mph for 2 hours 30 minutes it covers 65 × 2.5 = 162.5 miles. A runner who does 100 m in 20 seconds runs at 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s, which is 18 km/h or a pace of 3:20 per km.

Questions

How do I calculate speed?

Divide the distance by the time. 42 km in 1 hour 45 minutes is 42 ÷ 1.75 = 24 km/h. The calculator accepts any distance and time units and shows the answer in the speed unit you choose.

What is the speed formula in physics?

v = d ÷ t: speed equals distance divided by time, in metres per second in SI units. Rearranged, d = v × t and t = d ÷ v. With acceleration, the final speed is v = u + a × t.

How do I calculate speed in km/h?

Use the distance in kilometres and the time in hours, or let the calculator convert: choose km/h under "Show the speed in". To turn m/s into km/h multiply by 3.6, so 25 m/s is 90 km/h.

How do I calculate time from distance and speed?

Divide the distance by the speed: 375 km at 100 km/h takes 3.75 hours, which is 3 h 45 min. Use the Find time tab to get the answer in hours, minutes and seconds.

How do I turn km/h into mph?

Divide by 1.609344, or use the Convert units tab: 100 km/h is about 62.14 mph and 60 mph is about 96.56 km/h.

What is average speed?

It is the whole distance divided by the whole time, stops included. A trip of 120 km that takes 2 hours, with a half-hour break, has an average speed of 60 km/h even if you drove at 80 km/h.

What is the speed of light in km?

Exactly 299,792.458 km per second, or 299,792,458 m/s; the SI uses this value to define the metre. Choose "Speed of light (c)" in the converter to compare any speed with it.

What is the speed of sound in air?

About 343 m/s, or 1,234.8 km/h, in dry air at 20 °C. It rises in warmer air and is several times faster in water and in solids.

How do I type the time?

Use the hours, minutes and seconds boxes. Leave a box empty for 0, and use decimals if you need them, for example 12.5 seconds. 90 minutes can be typed as 1 hour 30 minutes or as 90 in the minutes box.

What is a knot?

One nautical mile (1,852 metres) per hour, used at sea and in the air. 1 knot is about 1.852 km/h or 1.151 mph.
