How it works

Speed, distance and time are tied together: speed is the distance covered divided by the time it took. Pick what you want to find and give the other two, each in the unit you have; the calculator turns everything into metres and seconds, does the sum, and shows the answer in the unit you choose, with the steps. A speed is also given in metres per second, km/h, mph and knots and, for everyday speeds, as a pace per kilometre and per mile. The last tab converts a speed between any two of 26 units, from millimetres per hour to the speed of light.

The formula

  • Speed = distance ÷ time
  • Distance = speed × time
  • Time = distance ÷ speed
  • 1 mph = 0.44704 m/s exactly; 1 km/h = 1,000 ÷ 3,600 m/s; 1 knot = 1,852 ÷ 3,600 m/s
  • Speed of light c = 299,792,458 m/s; 1 light-year = c × 365.25 days

A worked example

A car covers 250 km at 90 km/h: time = 250 ÷ 90 = 2.7778 hours = 10,000 seconds, which is 2 h 46 min 40 s. At 65 mph for 2 hours 30 minutes it covers 65 × 2.5 = 162.5 miles. A runner who does 100 m in 20 seconds runs at 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s, which is 18 km/h or a pace of 3:20 per km.

Questions

How do I turn km/h into mph?

Divide by 1.609344, or use the Convert units tab: 100 km/h is about 62.14 mph and 60 mph is about 96.56 km/h.

What is average speed?

It is the whole distance divided by the whole time, stops included. A trip of 120 km that takes 2 hours, with a half-hour break, has an average speed of 60 km/h even if you drove at 80 km/h.

How do I type the time?

Use the hours, minutes and seconds boxes. Leave a box empty for 0, and use decimals if you need them, for example 12.5 seconds. 90 minutes can be typed as 1 hour 30 minutes or as 90 in the minutes box.

What is a knot?

One nautical mile (1,852 metres) per hour, used at sea and in the air. 1 knot is about 1.852 km/h or 1.151 mph.
