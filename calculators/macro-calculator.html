How it works

The calculator first estimates the calories you burn in a day (your BMR from the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, times your activity factor) and adds or takes away 250, 500 or 1,000 kcal for your goal. It then splits those calories between protein, carbohydrate and fat by the diet style you choose, and turns each share into grams with the standard energy values: 4 kcal in a gram of protein or carbohydrate and 9 kcal in a gram of fat. The ranges come from widely used guidelines: at least 1 g of protein per kg of body weight and no more than 35% of calories, at least 40% of calories from carbs, and 20 to 35% from fat. If a weight-loss goal would take you under 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) a day, no plan is shown.

The formula

  • Daily calories C = BMR × activity factor (rounded) + goal change (−1,000 to +1,000 kcal)
  • Grams of protein = C × protein share ÷ 4; carbs = C × carb share ÷ 4; fat = C × fat share ÷ 9
  • Balanced 25 / 50 / 25%, low fat 27.5 / 52.5 / 20%, low carb 30 / 40 / 30%, high protein 35 / 42.5 / 22.5% (protein / carbs / fat)
  • Protein range: 1 g × body weight (kg) to 35% of C ÷ 4; fat range: 20% to 35% of C ÷ 9
  • Carb range: 40% of C ÷ 4 to (C − protein minimum × 4 − 20% of C) ÷ 4
  • Free sugar under 10% of C ÷ 4; saturated fat under 10% of C ÷ 9; 1 kcal = 4.1868 kJ

A worked example

A 25-year-old man, 5 ft 10 in and 165 lb, who exercises 4 to 5 times a week needs about 2,549 kcal a day to keep his weight. On the balanced plan: protein 2,549 × 25% ÷ 4 ≈ 159 g, carbs 2,549 × 50% ÷ 4 ≈ 319 g and fat 2,549 × 25% ÷ 9 ≈ 71 g a day. His protein range is 75 g (1 g per kg of his 74.8 kg) to 223 g.

Questions

Which diet style should I pick?

Balanced suits most people. High protein helps keep muscle while losing weight or when training hard. Low carb can make it easier to eat less for some people, and low fat suits people who prefer starchy foods. The best plan is the one you can keep up; total calories matter most for weight.

Why 4 and 9 calories per gram?

These are the standard average energy values for food: a gram of protein or carbohydrate gives about 4 kcal and a gram of fat about 9 kcal. Food labels use the same values. Some calculators use slightly different factors, so their grams can differ by a few percent.

What does My own split do?

Enter your own shares of protein, carbs and fat in percent. The calculator shows the grams, warns if the shares do not add up to 100%, and tells you when a nutrient falls outside its suggested range.

Why is no plan shown for my weight-loss goal?

If the goal would put you under 1,200 kcal a day for women or 1,500 kcal for men, it is hard to get enough nutrients, so the calculator suggests a slower rate instead. Very low calorie diets should only be done with a doctor.

Is free sugar the same as all sugar?

No. Free sugar means sugar added to food and drinks plus the sugar in honey, syrups and fruit juice. The sugar inside whole fruit, vegetables and milk does not count towards the 10% limit.
