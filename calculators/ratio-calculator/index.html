What a ratio is

A ratio calculator works with comparisons of the form A : B, read "A to B". A ratio of 2 : 3 between boys and girls means there are 2 boys for every 3 girls, whatever the actual class size. The numbers in a ratio are called its terms, and their order matters: 2 : 3 and 3 : 2 describe opposite situations.

A ratio can be written three ways: 2 : 3, 2 to 3, or the fraction 2/3. The fraction form compares one part with the other part. That is different from a share of the whole: with 2 boys to 3 girls there are 5 children per group, so boys are 2/5 = 40% of the class.

A proportion is a statement that two ratios are equal, such as 2 : 3 = 10 : 15. Proportions are how ratios become useful: if you know one full ratio and one term of the other, you can find the missing term.

How to use the ratio calculator

How to calculate a ratio and the missing term

To find the ratio of two numbers, write them with a colon in the order the question asks, then simplify. For 18 boys and 24 girls the ratio is 18 : 24; both divide by 6, so it is 3 : 4.

To find a missing term, cross-multiply. In A : B = C : D the outer terms multiply to the same product as the inner ones, A × D = B × C. Put in the three numbers you know and divide. For 16 : 9 = 1920 : D, 16 × D = 9 × 1920 = 17,280, so D = 17,280 ÷ 16 = 1,080.

To split an amount in a ratio, add the parts, divide the total by that sum to get one part, then multiply. For PKR 240,000 shared in the ratio 3 : 5 there are 8 parts of PKR 30,000, so the shares are PKR 90,000 and PKR 150,000. On this page, 3 : 5 = 90,000 : D confirms D = 150,000, and the share row shows 37.5% for the first partner.

Worked examples

  • Resizing a photo: a 3 : 4 picture must be 600 px wide. 3 : 4 = 600 : D gives D = 4 × 600 ÷ 3 = 800 px tall.
  • Decimals: 1.5 : 2 = 6 : D gives D = 2 × 6 ÷ 1.5 = 8. The simplest whole-number form of 1.5 : 2 is 3 : 4, found by scaling to 15 : 20 and dividing by 5.
  • Checking a proportion: is 2 : 3 equal to 5 : 8? Cross products are 2 × 8 = 16 and 3 × 5 = 15, so No. The D that would make them equal is 7.5.
  • Simplifying: 10 : 35 = 2 : 7 is Yes, because 10 × 7 = 70 and 35 × 2 = 70. In 1 : x form the ratio is 1 : 3.5.
  • Shrinking a screen size: 1920 : 1080 shrunk 1.5 times is 1280 : 720, still 16 : 9.
  • Enlarging: 4 : 3 enlarged 256 times is 1024 : 768, a classic 4 : 3 screen size.
  • A three-part mix such as 1 : 2 : 4 shares the total in 7 parts. For 140 kg that is 20 kg, 40 kg and 80 kg. This page handles two terms at a time, so solve 1 : 2 and 1 : 4 separately if you need it.

Ratios in Excel and as percentages

Excel has no ratio function, but =A1/B1 gives the x : 1 form and a GCD formula gives the simplest form: =A1/GCD(A1,B1)&":"&B1/GCD(A1,B1) turns 1920 and 1080 into 16:9. GCD works on whole numbers and cuts off any decimal part, so scale decimals to whole numbers first.

To turn a ratio into percentages of the whole, divide each term by the total. 3 : 5 is 3 ÷ 8 = 37.5% and 5 ÷ 8 = 62.5%. To turn a ratio into a single percentage of one part over the other, divide A by B: 3 ÷ 5 = 60%. The Percentage Calculator handles the rest.

Common mistakes

  • Swapping the order. Width to height is 16 : 9, not 9 : 16, and the answer changes completely if you swap them.
  • Mixing units. Change 2 m : 50 cm to 200 cm : 50 cm before simplifying; it is 4 : 1, not 2 : 50.
  • Adding the same number to both terms. 2 : 3 plus 1 each is 3 : 4, a different ratio. Only multiplying or dividing keeps it equal.
  • Confusing part-to-part with part-to-whole. With a 2 : 3 ratio, the first part is 2/5 of the whole, not 2/3.
  • Rounding too early. Keep decimals until the last step, as the calculator does.
  • Related tools: Fraction Calculator for ratios written as fractions and LCM Calculator for common denominators.

How it works

A ratio compares two amounts, such as 16 : 9 for the width and height of a screen. Two ratios that are equal make a proportion, A : B = C : D, and in any proportion A × D = B × C. So when you fill in three of the four boxes, the empty one is found by multiplying across and dividing: here 9 × 1920 ÷ 16 = 1080. The result also shows the ratio as 1 : x and x : 1, in its simplest whole numbers, and the left part as a share of the total, with a pie chart and a rectangle drawn in the ratio's shape. The second tab multiplies or divides both sides by the same number, which keeps the shape: shrinking 1920 : 1080 by 2 gives 960 : 540. Decimals are worked out exactly and only rounded for display.

The formula

  • A : B = C : D means A ÷ B = C ÷ D, so A × D = B × C
  • D = B × C ÷ A, C = A × D ÷ B, B = A × D ÷ C, A = B × C ÷ D
  • A : B = 1 : (B ÷ A) = (A ÷ B) : 1
  • Simplest form: scale decimals to whole numbers, then divide both by their greatest common factor
  • Share of the left part = A ÷ (A + B) × 100%
  • Enlarge k times: (A × k) : (B × k); shrink k times: (A ÷ k) : (B ÷ k)

A worked example

A photo is 3 : 4 and you want it 600 pixels wide. Then 3 : 4 = 600 : D, and D = 4 × 600 ÷ 3 = 800 pixels tall. As a single number the ratio is 1 : 1.3333 (or 0.75 : 1), and the 3 parts are 3 ÷ 7 = 42.86% of the whole.

Questions

How do I calculate the ratio of 2 numbers?

Write them as A : B in the order the question gives, then divide both by their greatest common factor. 18 and 24 become 18 : 24, and dividing by 6 gives 3 : 4. The calculator shows the simplest form and the 1 : x form.

How do I find the missing number in a ratio?

Type the three numbers you know into A, B, C or D and leave the unknown box empty. The calculator multiplies across the equals sign and divides by the number opposite the empty box. For 2 : 7 = 10 : D it works out D = 7 × 10 ÷ 2 = 35.

What is the ratio formula?

For a proportion A : B = C : D the rule is A × D = B × C, so D = B × C ÷ A. To split a total T in the ratio A : B, the parts are T × A ÷ (A + B) and T × B ÷ (A + B).

How do I simplify a ratio such as 10 : 35?

Divide both numbers by their greatest common factor, here 5, to get 2 : 7. Ratios with decimals are first scaled to whole numbers, so 1.5 : 2 becomes 15 : 20 and then 3 : 4. The result shows this as Simplest whole numbers.

What is the difference between ratio and proportion?

A ratio compares two amounts, such as 3 : 4. A proportion says two ratios are equal, such as 3 : 4 = 600 : 800. You use a proportion to scale a ratio up or down or to find a missing amount.

How do I share money in a ratio?

Add the ratio's parts, divide the money by that total, and multiply by each part. PKR 240,000 in the ratio 3 : 5 is 8 parts of PKR 30,000, so the shares are PKR 90,000 and PKR 150,000.

How do I convert a ratio to a percentage?

Divide each term by the sum of the terms and multiply by 100. For 3 : 5 the first part is 3 ÷ 8 = 37.5% and the second 62.5%. The page shows the left part's share for you.

How do I resize an image and keep its shape?

Use Enlarge or shrink, type the width and height, and the number of times. Both sides are multiplied or divided by the same number, so the picture is not stretched. When the answer has decimals, a rounded whole-number size is shown too.

Can I check whether two ratios are equal?

Yes. Fill in all four boxes. The calculator compares A × D with B × C and tells you whether the ratios match; if they do not, it shows which D would make them equal.

How do I calculate a ratio in Excel?

Use =A1/GCD(A1,B1)&":"&B1/GCD(A1,B1) for the simplest whole-number ratio, or =A1/B1 for the decimal x : 1 form. GCD needs whole numbers, so multiply decimal values by 10 or 100 first.
