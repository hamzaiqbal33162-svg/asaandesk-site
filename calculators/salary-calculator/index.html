How it works

Type a pay amount and say what it is per: an hour, a day, a week, two weeks, half a month, a month, a quarter or a year. Set the hours and days you work in a week, and under Paid time off the holidays and vacation days you get. The calculator uses a 52-week year. An hourly or daily amount is treated as a rate for the days you work, so it is multiplied out to a year; a weekly, monthly or yearly amount is treated as the pay you actually receive, with paid days off already inside it. Every other period is then that yearly figure divided up, shown two ways: counting every working day, and with your days off taken out.

The formula

  • Working days a year D = 52 × days a week; working hours a year H = 52 × hours a week; days off O = holidays + vacation
  • Hourly or daily rate: unadjusted year = rate × H (or × D); adjusted year = unadjusted × (D − O) ÷ D
  • Weekly or longer pay: adjusted year = pay × periods a year (52, 26, 24, 12, 4, 1); unadjusted year = adjusted × D ÷ (D − O)
  • Each period = yearly figure ÷ H (hour), ÷ D (day), ÷ 52, ÷ 26, ÷ 24, ÷ 12 or ÷ 4

A worked example

At $50 an hour, 40 hours and 5 days a week, a year has 2,080 hours, so the unadjusted pay is $104,000. With 10 holidays and 15 vacation days taken out of 260 working days, 235 days are worked: $104,000 × 235 ÷ 260 = $94,000, or $45.19 an hour averaged over all 2,080 hours.

Sources

  • US Office of Personnel Management: computing hourly rates of pay
  • 5 U.S.C. 6103: federal public holidays

Questions

How many working hours are in a year?

With 40 hours a week and 52 weeks it is 2,080 hours, which is the number most employers use. The US federal government uses 2,087 to allow for leap years. Change the hours a week here to match your job.

Why are there two columns?

An hourly worker is usually paid only for hours worked, while a salary already pays for holidays and vacation. The unadjusted column counts every working day; the adjusted one takes your days off out. For a salary, the gap shows how much your paid time off is worth.

Is twice a month the same as every two weeks?

No. Twice a month is 24 paychecks a year, while every two weeks is 26, so each biweekly check is a little smaller. The yearly total is the same.

Does this include tax?

No, all amounts are before tax and deductions. To see what you take home, use the take-home paycheck calculator.
