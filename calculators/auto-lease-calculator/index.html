What an auto lease calculator works out

An auto lease calculator turns the figures on a dealer's lease offer into a monthly payment you can check line by line. You enter the car price, the lease length, the rate (as an APR or a money factor), any down payment or trade-in, the residual value and your sales tax. It returns the monthly lease payment and shows how much of it is depreciation, how much is finance charge and how much is tax.

A lease is a long-term rental of a car. You pay for the slice of the car's value you use up while you have it, a finance charge for the money the leasing company has tied up in the car, and tax on both. When the term ends you return the car, or buy it for the residual value fixed in the contract. Because you never pay off the whole price, a lease payment is usually far lower than a loan payment on the same car.

The calculator also runs backwards. On the Find the car price tab you type the monthly payment you can afford, and it shows the most expensive car that payment can lease, with the same breakdown. Under every result, a side-by-side table compares the lease with buying the same car on a loan at the same rate over the same number of months.

How to use the auto lease calculator

  • Pick a tab. Find the payment starts from a car price. Find the car price starts from the monthly payment you want, with tax included.
  • Car price: the price agreed with the dealer before any down payment, often called the gross capitalized cost. You can negotiate it just like a purchase price. The default is $50,000.
  • Monthly payment you want (second tab only): your monthly budget including sales tax. The default is $600.
  • Lease length: the number of monthly payments, from 1 to 120 months. Most offers are for 24, 36 or 48 months; the default is 36.
  • Rate given as: choose Interest rate (APR) when you know a yearly percentage, or Money factor when the offer shows a small decimal such as 0.00208. The result always shows the other form as well, so offers quoted either way can be compared.
  • Down payment and Trade-in value: cash paid at signing and the value given for the car you hand in. Both cut the amount being leased. Leave them at 0 for a zero-down lease.
  • Residual value at lease end: what the leasing company expects the car to be worth when you return it. Set the unit to % to type it as a share of the car price (55% by default), or to $ to type the dollar figure written in the contract.
  • Sales tax: your state or local rate in percent, 7% by default. Type 0 if lease payments are not taxed where you live.

Two safety checks run as you type. A money factor above 0.1 is treated as an interest rate typed into the wrong box, converted, and flagged in a warning. A residual of 100% or more, or one larger than the amount being leased, is refused, because no real lease works that way.

How to calculate a car lease payment by hand

Every US-style lease payment comes from the same four steps. Here they are for a $42,000 car with $3,000 down, a 58% residual, a 6.5% APR, 8.25% sales tax and 36 months. Type these numbers into the calculator and you will see the same figures.

  • Amount leased (net capitalized cost): $42,000 − $3,000 = $39,000. The residual is 58% of $42,000 = $24,360.
  • Depreciation: ($39,000 − $24,360) ÷ 36 = $14,640 ÷ 36 = $406.67 a month.
  • Finance charge: the money factor is 6.5 ÷ 2,400 = 0.0027083, so ($39,000 + $24,360) × 0.0027083 = $171.60 a month.
  • Tax: ($406.67 + $171.60) × 8.25% = $47.71 a month.
  • Monthly payment: $406.67 + $171.60 + $47.71 = $625.97.

Over 36 months the payments add up to $22,535.05. Add the $3,000 down and the $247.50 tax on it, and handing the car back costs $25,782.55. Buying it at the end for $24,360 plus tax takes the total cost to own to $52,152.25. Buying the car on a 36-month loan of $39,000 at 6.5% instead costs $1,195.31 a month and $49,496.20 in total.

Money factor to APR: how lease interest works

US lease contracts usually state the interest as a money factor, a small decimal, instead of a yearly percentage. Multiply the money factor by 2,400 to get the APR, or divide an APR by 2,400 to get the money factor. A money factor of 0.00175 equals a 4.2% rate, and a 6% APR equals a money factor of 0.0025.

Why 2,400? During a lease, the money the leasing company has in the car falls from the amount leased at the start to the residual at the end, so the average balance is about (amount leased + residual) ÷ 2. One month of interest on that average is (C + R) ÷ 2 × APR ÷ 1,200, where 1,200 turns a yearly percentage into a monthly decimal. That simplifies to (C + R) × APR ÷ 2,400, which is why the finance charge multiplies the sum of the two amounts by the money factor.

It is a convention rather than an exact loan schedule, but it is the method used on most US lease contracts. The table shows what common money factors cost on a $40,000 lease with a $22,000 residual.

Lease or buy: reading the comparison table

Leasing wins on monthly cash. In the default case the lease costs $681.75 a month, against $1,348.69 for a 36-month loan on the same $45,000. That is why leasing appeals to people who want a newer car for a set monthly budget.

Buying usually wins on total cost if you want to keep the car. The loan route costs $57,052.85 to own the default car, about $2,265 less than leasing it and buying it out at the end for $59,318.13. The difference is mostly finance charge: the lease charges interest on the residual for all 36 months ($5,437.50 in total), while a loan balance shrinks with every payment ($3,552.85 of interest).

So the real question is how long you keep cars and how far you drive. Leasing suits someone who wants a new car every two to four years, drives a steady and moderate distance and likes a payment that stays low. Buying suits someone who drives a lot, keeps a car well past the loan, or wants the freedom to sell, modify or move abroad with it.

Mileage limits, wear, fees and ending a lease early

The FTC notes that the yearly mileage limit in most standard leases is 15,000 miles or less. A higher allowance usually raises the payment, because the car will be worth less at the end, and going over the limit means a per-mile fee when you return the car. Estimate your yearly driving before you sign.

You also pay for excess wear and damage and for any missing equipment at return, and many leases add an acquisition fee at signing and a disposition fee at the end. These charges are not part of the monthly payment, so this calculator does not include them; add them to your own total when you compare offers.

Ending a lease early can be costly. The FTC warns of a substantial early termination charge, so read that clause before signing. The usual alternatives are buying the car early for the payoff amount the leasing company quotes, or transferring the lease to someone else if the contract allows it.

In the US, the Consumer Leasing Act and Regulation M require a standard disclosure for consumer leases, showing items such as the amount due at signing, the gross capitalized cost, the residual value, the rent charge, the total of payments and the early termination and excess mileage terms. In 2026 these rules cover leases with a total contractual obligation of $73,400 or less; the limit rises with inflation each year.

Sales tax and local notes: US states, Canada and Pakistan

US states tax leases in different ways. This calculator taxes each monthly payment and the down payment, which matches how many states do it. Some states charge tax on the whole price at signing instead, or credit a trade-in. Texas, for example, charges motor vehicle sales tax of 6.25% of the sales price minus any trade-in on a purchase (Texas Comptroller, 2026), so ask the dealer how tax is applied to your lease and type the rate that applies.

In Canada, enter your sales tax as one combined rate: 13% HST in Ontario, for instance. If the offer shows a yearly lease rate rather than a money factor, choose Interest rate (APR). Canadian contracts often set a yearly kilometre limit; it does not change the payment formula, only the charge at return.

For business use in the US, IRS Publication 463 (2025 edition) lets you deduct only the business-use part of each lease payment. For passenger vehicles first leased in 2024 or 2025 with a fair market value above $62,000, you also add back an inclusion amount that trims the deduction.

In Pakistan, what banks call car leasing is really auto financing that ends with you owning the car: the bank keeps ownership while you pay the monthly rentals, and the car is transferred to you once the last one is paid. Islamic banks offer the same idea as car Ijarah. As reported in October 2025, State Bank of Pakistan rules require at least 30% down, cap auto financing at Rs3 million per person across all banks, and limit the term to 5 years for cars up to 1,000cc and 3 years above that. Because you end up owning the car, use the Auto Loan Calculator for these deals.

Tips, common mistakes and related tools

  • Negotiate the car price first. Every other number in the lease is built on it, and a $1,000 lower price cuts both the depreciation and the finance charge.
  • Convert every money factor to an APR before comparing offers, and check that the rate in the contract matches the one you were quoted.
  • Check what the residual percentage is based on. Our % option applies it to the car price you typed; if the leasing company bases it on the list price (MSRP), switch the unit to $ and type its dollar figure.
  • Rolling negative equity into a lease: switch the residual to $ and add the amount you still owe above the trade-in value to the car price. For the default car with a $27,500 residual, adding $3,000 of negative equity raises the payment from $681.75 to $777.61, so the old loan costs $95.86 more every month.
  • Do not judge a lease on the payment alone. Compare the total if you hand the car back with what you would lose in value by buying and selling.
  • Keep the amount due at signing small, and budget for mileage, wear and end-of-lease fees.

How it works

A car lease payment has three parts. Depreciation is the value the car loses while you drive it: the amount being leased (price minus down payment and trade-in) minus the residual value, spread over the months. The finance charge is the lender's interest, worked out with a money factor on the leased amount plus the residual. Sales tax is then added to both. The second tab runs the same sum backwards to find the car price a monthly budget allows. Below the result, the same car bought with a loan at the same rate over the same months shows which way costs less to own.

The formula

  • Amount leased C = car price − down payment − trade-in; R = residual value
  • Money factor MF = APR ÷ 2,400 (and APR = MF × 2,400)
  • Depreciation D = (C − R) ÷ months; finance charge F = (C + R) × MF
  • Tax T = (D + F) × sales tax; monthly payment = D + F + T
  • Up-front tax = down payment × sales tax (the trade-in is not taxed)
  • Cost to own after the lease = all payments + down payment + up-front tax + trade-in + R × (1 + sales tax)
  • Loan instead: payment = C × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−months) with r = APR ÷ 12; sales tax = (price − trade-in) × tax, paid up front

A worked example

A $42,000 car with $3,000 down, a 58% residual ($24,360), a 6.5% APR (money factor 0.00271) and 8.25% sales tax over 36 months: depreciation = (39,000 − 24,360) ÷ 36 = $406.67, finance charge = (39,000 + 24,360) × 0.0027083 = $171.60, tax = 8.25% × 578.27 = $47.71, so the lease payment is $625.97 a month.

Questions

How are auto leases calculated?

Take the car price minus the down payment and trade-in, subtract the residual value and divide by the months to get depreciation. Add the finance charge, which is the amount leased plus the residual multiplied by the money factor, then add sales tax on both. The total is your monthly payment.

How do I calculate the money factor on a car lease?

Divide the APR by 2,400: a 5% rate is a money factor of 0.00208 and a 6.5% rate is 0.00271. To check a quoted money factor, multiply it by 2,400; 0.00175 means 4.2% a year. The calculator shows the conversion either way.

How do I calculate the residual value of a car lease?

Multiply the residual percentage by the price it is based on. A 55% residual on a $50,000 car is $27,500. The leasing company sets the percentage, so take it from the offer, and type the dollar figure if it is based on the list price rather than the price you agreed.

How do I find the interest rate on an auto lease?

Ask for the money factor and multiply it by 2,400. If the dealer gives only the payment, try rates in this calculator with the same price, residual and down payment until the payment matches; that rate is what the lease really charges.

How does a trade-in work in an auto lease calculator?

The trade-in value is subtracted from the car price, exactly like a down payment, so it lowers both the depreciation and the finance charge. Here it is not taxed, while the cash down payment is taxed at signing.

Can I include negative equity in a lease?

Yes, many dealers add what you still owe on your old car above its trade-in value to the new lease. To see the effect, set the residual to a dollar amount and add the negative equity to the car price. You then pay depreciation and finance charge on the old debt too.

Is a big down payment a good idea on a lease?

It lowers the monthly payment, but the money is normally not returned if the car is stolen or written off early. Many people put down only the fees and taxes due at signing and accept a slightly higher payment.

What is the difference between a car lease and car finance?

With finance you borrow the full price, pay it off and own the car. With a lease you pay only for the value used up plus a finance charge, then return the car or buy it for the residual. Leasing costs less each month; financing usually costs less in total if you keep the car.

How is sales tax handled on a lease?

This calculator adds sales tax to each monthly payment and taxes the down payment at signing, while the trade-in is not taxed. Some US states tax the full price up front or treat trade-ins differently, so check your state's rule before relying on the total.

Does this work as an auto lease calculator for Canada or Ontario?

Yes. Choose Interest rate (APR) if the offer gives a yearly lease rate, and type your combined sales tax, for example 13% HST in Ontario. The currency symbol shows dollars, and the maths is the same.

How do I lease a car in Pakistan?

Banks offer car leasing or Ijarah through their auto finance desks, and you own the car at the end. As reported in late 2025, you need at least 30% down, total auto financing is capped at Rs3 million per person, and terms run up to 5 years for cars up to 1,000cc and 3 years above. Use the auto loan calculator for these monthly instalments.

Can I deduct car lease payments for my business?

In the US you can deduct the business-use share of each payment under the actual expense method, according to IRS Publication 463. For cars first leased in 2024 or 2025 worth more than $62,000, an inclusion amount reduces the deduction. Rules change, so check the latest edition.
