What this age calculator works out

This age calculator gives your exact age from your date of birth: the whole years first, then the months since your last birthday, then the days since your last month-day. It follows the way birthdays work, so a year is added on the birthday itself and not at the start of a new year.

The age is worked out on today's date unless you pick another one. That second date is what most forms mean when they ask for your age "as on" a closing or cut-off date. The same span is also shown in months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds, with the weekday you were born on and a countdown to your next birthday.

How to use the age calculator

  • Enter your date of birth in the Date of birth box.
  • Leave "Age on this date" as it is to get your age today, or change it to the date a form asks about, such as the cut-off date of an exam or the closing date of a job advert. If you clear the box, today is used.
  • Read the big line: that is your age in years, months and days on the chosen date. The lines under it give the same span in other units and the days left until your next birthday.
  • Check the month calendars at the bottom. Both dates are marked, so a slip in the day or the month is easy to spot before you copy the answer onto a form.

Age "as on" a cut-off date for CSS, PMS, PPSC and NADRA

Pakistani exam and job forms usually ask for your age on a fixed date, not on the day you apply. Put that date in "Age on this date" and copy the years, months and days exactly as shown. Always take the cut-off date and the limits from the official notice for your exam year, because they can change.

For CSS, the FPSC notice for the CSS Competitive Examination 2027 (dated 2 August 2026) says candidates must be 21 to 30 years old on the cut-off date of 31 December 2026, with a two-year relaxation for the categories listed in Rule 6 of the CSS rules. Someone born on March 10, 2002 is 24 years 9 months 21 days old on that date. Someone born on February 1, 2006 is 20 years 10 months 30 days old, so still under 21 for that exam.

PMS and other posts advertised by the Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) and the other provincial commissions each state their own age limits and the date on which age is counted. Read them in the advertisement for your post, then enter that date here.

For identity documents, NADRA issues a CNIC to Pakistani citizens who have attained the age of 18, and a Child Registration Certificate (CRC, often called the B-Form) or a Juvenile Card to children under 18. Someone born on December 5, 2008 is 17 years 11 months 17 days old on November 22, 2026, and the calculator shows the 18th birthday 13 days later.

Other ways of counting age

Not every system counts age from the birthday. In the traditional Korean count, a newborn was one year old at birth and everyone gained a year on January 1. South Korea moved legal and public documents to the international, birthday-based count from June 28, 2023, which made most people a year or two younger on paper. This calculator always uses the birthday-based count.

People born on February 29 have a real birthday only in leap years. Here the age goes up on March 1 in other years, so on February 28, 2027 someone born on February 29, 2000 is 26 years 11 months 30 days old. Legal rules for leap-day birthdays are not the same everywhere, so check the rule behind your form if one day matters.

Dates before 1583 are counted on the Gregorian calendar carried back in time. Old records from those years often used the Julian calendar, so an age worked out from them can be a few days off.

Working out age in Excel or Google Sheets

Both spreadsheets have a DATEDIF function. With the date of birth in A1 and the other date in B1, =DATEDIF(A1,B1,"Y") gives the whole years, "YM" the months left after those years, and "D" the total days. Microsoft warns that the "MD" unit, often used for the leftover days, can give a negative, zero or wrong result, which is one reason to check a spreadsheet answer here.

In Excel, DATEDIF returns the #NUM! error when the start date is later than the end date, so keep the date of birth in the first argument and the later date in the second.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Use the date the form names, not the day you fill it in. A few weeks can change the months and days, and near a birthday they can change the years.
  • Check the day and month order on your documents. 05/12/2008 can mean December 5 or May 12, depending on how the document writes dates, and the calendar under the result shows which one you entered.
  • Your time of birth is not used, so the hours, minutes and seconds are whole days multiplied out.
  • If the answer looks one day off, check whether your form counts the cut-off date itself as a full day; this calculator does not add it.

How it works

Pick a date of birth. The age is worked out on today's date unless you choose another one, which is handy for forms that ask for your age on a set date. It is counted the usual way, where age goes up on each birthday: first the whole years, then the whole months, then the days left. The same span is also shown as total months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds, together with the weekday you were born on and how long it is to the next birthday.

The formula

  • Whole months = 12 × (end year − birth year) + (end month − birth month), minus 1 when the birth day of the month is later than the end day
  • Years = whole months ÷ 12 (rounded down); months = what is left over
  • Days = end date − (birth date moved on by the whole months, using the last day of a shorter month when needed)
  • Total days = end date − birth date (the end day itself is not added); hours = days × 24, minutes = days × 1,440, seconds = days × 86,400
  • Weeks = total days ÷ 7 (rounded down), with the remainder in days

A worked example

Born Jan 15, 1990, on Nov 21, 2026: from January 1990 to November 2026 is 36 × 12 + 10 = 442 whole months, and Jan 15 + 442 months is Nov 15, 2026, 6 days before the end. So the age is 36 years 10 months 6 days, or 13,459 days in total (1,922 weeks 5 days).

Questions

How do I calculate my age from my date of birth?

Count the whole years since your date of birth, then the whole months since your last birthday, then the days since the last month-day. Enter the date of birth here and the calculator does all three, so someone born on Jan 15, 1990 is 36 years 10 months 6 days old on Nov 21, 2026.

How do I find my age as on a specific date?

Change "Age on this date" to the date you need, for example the cut-off date of an exam or the closing date of an application. The age, the totals and the next birthday are then all counted from that date, past or future.

How do I calculate my age for CSS 2027?

Use 31 December 2026, the cut-off date in the FPSC notice for the CSS Competitive Examination 2027, as the age date. That notice asks for an age of 21 to 30 on that day, with a two-year relaxation for the categories in Rule 6. Check the latest FPSC notice before you apply, as rules can change.

What age do I need for a NADRA CNIC?

NADRA issues a CNIC to Pakistani citizens who have attained the age of 18. Children under 18 are registered with a Child Registration Certificate (B-Form) or a Juvenile Card. Put today's date or your planned visit date in the calculator to see how close you are to 18.

How is age counted in years, months and days?

Age goes up by one year on each birthday. After the whole years come the whole months since the last birthday, then the days since the last month-day. So someone born on Jan 15 is exactly 36 years old on Jan 15, 2026, and 36 years 10 months 6 days on Nov 21, 2026.

What if I was born on February 29?

In years without a Feb 29 the years count goes up on Mar 1 here, so on Feb 28, 2025 someone born on Feb 29, 2000 is 24 years 11 months 30 days old, and on Mar 1 they turn 25. Legal rules for leap-day birthdays differ from place to place, so check the rule that applies to your form.

Why does Jan 31 to Mar 1 show 1 month and 1 day?

A month after Jan 31 is the last day of February (Feb 29 in 2024), because February has no 31st. From there to Mar 1 is one more day. Dates near the end of a month always need this rule, so the days part can look uneven.

What is the age formula in Excel?

=DATEDIF(birth date, other date, "Y") gives the whole years, "YM" the extra months and "D" the total days. Google Sheets has the same function. Microsoft advises against the "MD" unit for the leftover days, because it can give wrong results.

How old was I on a past date?

Set "Age on this date" to that past date. Any date on or after your date of birth works, so you can see how old you were on your first day at work or when a document was issued.

Are the hours exact to my time of birth?

No. The totals count whole days from the date of birth to the chosen date, so hours, minutes and seconds are those days times 24, 1,440 and 86,400. The time of day and clock changes are not included.
