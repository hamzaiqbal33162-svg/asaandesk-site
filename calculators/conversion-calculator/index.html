What this conversion calculator does

A conversion calculator turns a measurement in one unit into the same measurement in another unit: miles into kilometres, pounds into kilograms, cups into millilitres, Fahrenheit into Celsius. This one covers six groups of units (length, temperature, area, volume, weight and time) across the metric system, US customary units and British imperial units.

Each answer comes from the legal definition of the unit, not a rounded rule of thumb, so 1 pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg and 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm. You also see the amount in every other unit of the same group at once, which saves you converting the same value again and again.

How to use the conversion calculator

  • Choose a tab: Length, Temperature, Area, Volume, Weight or Time. Each tab has its own unit lists.
  • Type the Amount. It starts at 1, which is handy when you only want the conversion factor (1 mile = 1.609344 km).
  • Pick the unit you have in From and the unit you want in To. The defaults are miles to kilometres, Fahrenheit to Celsius, square feet to square metres, US gallons to litres, pounds to kilograms and hours to minutes.
  • Read the result at the top, written out in words as well (for example 150 pounds = 68.0388555 kilograms), and copy it with one click.
  • Under the result, two rows show 1 of the first unit in the second and the reverse, and the step line shows the multiplication through the base unit.
  • The table lists your amount in every unit of the group. For temperature the rows show the formula used instead of a factor.

If both lists show the same unit, the amount is returned unchanged with a note. A temperature below absolute zero (−273.15 °C, −459.67 °F or 0 K) is refused, because nothing can be colder.

Temperature conversion formulas: Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin

Temperature is the one group without a single factor, because the scales put zero in different places. Water freezes at 0 °C and 32 °F, so you must remove the 32 before scaling: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9, and °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. The Kelvin scale uses the same step size as Celsius but starts at absolute zero, so K = °C + 273.15.

A change in temperature converts differently from a reading. A rise of 10 °C is a rise of 18 °F and of 10 K, with no 32 involved. The calculator converts readings, and the note under the result reminds you of the rule for differences.

Metric, US customary and imperial units

Most of the world measures in the International System of Units (SI), which rests on seven base units: the second, metre, kilogram, ampere, kelvin, mole and candela. Since 20 May 2019 all of them are defined by fixed values of seven constants of nature, so the kilogram no longer depends on a metal cylinder kept near Paris. The metre is the distance light travels in a vacuum in 1/299,792,458 of a second.

The United States still uses customary units in daily life, and the UK keeps some imperial ones, such as the pint and the mile. Both are tied to the metric system: under the 1959 international agreement the yard is exactly 0.9144 m and the pound exactly 0.45359237 kg. Pakistan set its legal standards on the International System with the Weights and Measures (International System) Act, 1967, and the provinces later passed enforcement laws, such as Punjab's in 1975.

The trap is that some names mean different sizes in the US and UK systems. Always pick the right one in the list.

Worked examples

  • Height in metres: 6 feet × 0.3048 = 1.8288 m. In the calculator choose Length, type 6, From Foot, To Metre.
  • Body weight in kilograms: 150 pounds × 0.45359237 = 68.0388555 kg. A converter that rounds the pound to 0.453592 shows 68.0388 kg, which is why answers can differ in the last digits.
  • Baking from a US recipe: 2 US cups × 236.5882365 = 473.176473 mL, just under half a litre. Note that US nutrition labels use a rounded 240 mL cup, so a label cup and a measuring cup are not quite the same.
  • A plot in Pakistan: a 1,125 square foot plot is 1,125 × 0.09290304 = 104.51592 square metres. Choose Area, From Square foot, To Square metre.
  • Baggage allowance: a 20 kg limit is 20 ÷ 0.45359237 = 44.092452437 pounds, so a bag weighed at home as 45 lb is over the limit.
  • Oven temperature: a recipe that says 350 °F needs (350 − 32) × 5/9 = 176.666666667 °C, so set about 175 to 180 °C.

What a conversion factor is and how to calculate one

A conversion factor is the number that links two units of the same quantity, written as a ratio equal to 1, such as 2.54 cm per inch. Multiplying by it changes the unit without changing the amount. To build one yourself, write both units in the same base unit and divide: 1 mile is 1,609.344 m and 1 km is 1,000 m, so 1 mile = 1,609.344 ÷ 1,000 = 1.609344 km.

For a longer chain, multiply the ratios so the unwanted units cancel: 12 inches × 2.54 cm per inch × 1 m per 100 cm = 0.3048 m. That is exactly what the step line in the calculator shows, with both factors written in the group's base unit.

Area and volume factors are the length factor squared or cubed. One metre is about 3.28 feet, but one square metre is about 10.76 square feet and one cubic metre is about 35.31 cubic feet.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Check US against imperial for gallons, pints, fluid ounces, tablespoons and tons; for gallons and tablespoons the gap is about 20%.
  • Do not square a length answer by hand for area. Use the Area tab, so square feet to square metres uses 0.09290304, not 0.3048.
  • Round only at the end. Rounding the factor first (2.2 lb per kg) adds an error of about 0.2% to every result.
  • Months and years here are averages (30.4375 and 365.25 days). For real calendar spans use a date tool.
  • Weight in this calculator means mass (kilograms, pounds), which is what scales and shops show.
  • For money, a currency converter is needed: exchange rates change every day, unlike unit definitions.

How it works

Pick a group (length, temperature, area, volume, weight or time), type an amount and choose the two units. Every unit has an exact size in a base unit (metres, square metres, cubic metres, kilograms or seconds), so the amount is multiplied by the size of the first unit and divided by the size of the second. Temperatures are different because their scales start at different points, so they go through degrees Celsius with their own formulas. The table under the result shows the same amount in every unit of the group, and results keep 12 significant digits.

The formula

  • Result = amount × (size of the 'from' unit) ÷ (size of the 'to' unit), both in the group's base unit
  • Exact sizes: 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 foot = 0.3048 m, 1 mile = 1,609.344 m, 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg, 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L, 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 L, 1 acre = 4,046.8564224 m²
  • 1 light year = 9,460,730,472,580,800 m; 1 month = 30.4375 days and 1 year = 365.25 days (averages)
  • °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9; °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; K = °C + 273.15

A worked example

1 mile in kilometres: 1 × 1,609.344 m ÷ 1,000 m = 1.609344 km. And 98.6 °F in Celsius: (98.6 − 32) × 5/9 = 37 °C.

Questions

How do I convert cm to inches?

Divide the centimetres by 2.54, because one inch is exactly 2.54 cm. So 30 cm is 30 ÷ 2.54 = 11.811 inches. In the calculator choose Length, From Centimetre, To Inch.

How do I convert mm to inches?

Divide the millimetres by 25.4. A 10 mm bolt is 10 ÷ 25.4 = 0.3937 inches, a little under 0.4 inch. The reverse is to multiply inches by 25.4.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

One kilogram is about 2.20462262185 pounds, because one pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kg. To change kg to lbs multiply by 2.20462; to change lbs to kg multiply by 0.45359237.

What is the formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius reading by 9/5 (1.8) and add 32: 25 °C × 1.8 + 32 = 77 °F. Going the other way, subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9.

How do I convert Celsius to Kelvin?

Add 273.15 to the Celsius reading, so 25 °C is 298.15 K. Kelvin has no degree sign and can never be negative, since 0 K is absolute zero.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius in my head?

Subtract 30 and halve it for a rough answer (80 °F is about 25 °C). The exact rule is: subtract 32, then multiply by 5/9, so 80 °F is 26.7 °C.

Is a US gallon the same as an imperial gallon?

No. A US gallon is 3.785 litres and an imperial (UK) gallon is 4.546 litres, about 20% more. US pints, quarts and fluid ounces are also different from the imperial ones, so pick the right one in the list.

Can I use it as a cooking or baking conversion calculator?

Yes, for volumes. The Volume tab has US and imperial cups, tablespoons and teaspoons, fluid ounces and millilitres. It cannot turn a cup of flour into grams, because that depends on how dense the ingredient is.

Does it convert currency or land units like marla and kanal?

No. Exchange rates change daily, so use the Currency Calculator for money. For marla and kanal use the Area Calculator, since a marla is 225, 250 or 272.25 square feet depending on the city and housing scheme.

Why can my answer differ slightly from another converter?

Some converters use rounded factors, such as 0.453592 for the pound. This one uses the exact legal definitions, so answers can differ in the sixth or seventh digit.

How long is a month or a year here?

Months and years have different lengths, so the average is used: a year of 365.25 days (counting leap years) and a month of one twelfth of that, 30.4375 days. For exact calendar dates use a date calculator.
