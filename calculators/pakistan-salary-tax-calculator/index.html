What this calculator works out

Income tax on a salary in Pakistan is worked out on the whole year's taxable salary, then collected a little at a time: your employer deducts it from each payment and pays it to the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR). This calculator follows the same path. It takes your taxable salary per month or per year, finds the slab that applies in tax year 2027, and shows the yearly tax, the monthly deduction and what you keep.

Tax year 2027 runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, the same period people call 2026-27 or the 2026-27 budget year. The rates come from the FBR's withholding tax rate card for tax year 2027, which the FBR updated to 30 June 2026 to match the Finance Act 2026.

How to use it

  • Taxable salary: type the salary on which tax is charged. For most employees this is the gross pay on the payslip less any part the law exempts. If you are not sure, your employer's payroll team or your annual tax certificate shows the taxable figure.
  • Per: choose month if you typed a monthly figure, or year for a yearly one. A monthly figure is multiplied by 12.
  • Read the result: the first line is the tax per month (or per year), followed by the other figures, a chart of tax against take-home pay, the worked steps and the full slab table.

The salary tax slabs for 2026-27

Pakistan taxes salary in slabs. Each slab has a fixed amount, which is the full tax on all the slabs below it, plus a percentage of the part of your salary inside that slab. Only that part is charged the higher rate, so moving into a new slab never makes your take-home pay go down.

Worked examples

  • Rs 50,000 a month is Rs 600,000 a year: no income tax.
  • Rs 100,000 a month is Rs 1,200,000 a year: 1% of Rs 600,000 = Rs 6,000 a year, or Rs 500 a month.
  • Rs 150,000 a month is Rs 1,800,000 a year: Rs 6,000 + 11% of Rs 600,000 = Rs 72,000 a year, or Rs 6,000 a month.
  • Rs 300,000 a month is Rs 3,600,000 a year: Rs 316,000 + 25% of Rs 400,000 = Rs 416,000 a year, or about Rs 34,667 a month.
  • Rs 500,000 a month is Rs 6,000,000 a year: Rs 976,000 + 32% of Rs 400,000 = Rs 1,104,000 a year, or Rs 92,000 a month.
  • Rs 8,000,000 a year: Rs 1,424,000 + 35% of Rs 1,000,000 = Rs 1,774,000, an average rate of about 22.2%.

Average rate and the rate on your next rupee

Two percentages describe your tax. The rate on your next rupee is the rate of your slab: it tells you how much of a raise goes to tax. The average, or effective, rate is the whole year's tax divided by the whole year's taxable salary. Because the lower parts of your salary are taxed at lower rates, the average rate is always below the slab rate.

For example, at Rs 300,000 a month the slab rate is 25%, but the tax of Rs 416,000 on Rs 3,600,000 is an average rate of about 11.6%. A raise of Rs 10,000 a month would add Rs 2,500 a month in tax at that level, and you would keep the other Rs 7,500.

When these rates apply

These are the rates for salaried taxpayers: people whose salary is more than 75% of their taxable income. If a large part of your income comes from business, rent or other sources, a different table applies to you, and this calculator will understate or overstate your tax.

The rate card for tax year 2027 shows no surcharge on salary income. Pension, bonuses, arrears and income from other sources have rules of their own, so check them separately if they apply to you.

How your employer deducts the tax

Under section 149 of the Income Tax Ordinance, your employer estimates your taxable salary for the whole tax year, works out the tax on it, and deducts it in equal parts from each salary payment. If your pay changes during the year, for example after a raise or a bonus, the employer works it out again for the rest of the year, so the monthly deduction can change.

At the end of the year the deductions should add up to your tax for the year. Your employer gives you a certificate of the tax deducted, and you use it when you file your income tax return on the FBR's IRIS portal.

Things this calculator does not include

  • Exempt allowances and other deductions: type your taxable salary, after any part the law exempts.
  • Tax credits, for example for donations or certain investments, which can lower the tax on your return.
  • Income other than salary, and the separate rules for pension, arrears and some kinds of bonus.
  • Withholding taxes on other things you pay for, such as some bank transactions or utility bills, which are separate from income tax on salary.

Check before you rely on it

Tax rules change with each Finance Act, and your own payslip may include items this calculator does not see. Use the figure here to plan your budget or to check a payslip, and ask your employer's payroll team or a tax adviser when the numbers matter, for example before you sign a new job offer. For other salary sums, the salary calculator turns a monthly figure into weekly, daily or hourly pay, and the budget calculator helps you plan what you keep.

How it works

Type your taxable salary and choose whether it is per month or per year. The calculator turns a monthly salary into a yearly one, finds your slab in the tax year 2027 table, works out the yearly income tax, and splits it into twelve monthly deductions. It also shows your salary after tax, your average tax rate and the rate on your next rupee, with the steps of the sum.

The formula

  • Yearly taxable salary = monthly taxable salary × 12
  • Income tax per year = fixed tax of your slab + slab rate × (yearly taxable salary − the slab's starting amount)
  • Income tax per month = income tax per year ÷ 12; salary after tax = salary − income tax

A worked example

A salary of Rs 150,000 a month is Rs 1,800,000 a year. That falls in the slab above Rs 1,200,000, so the tax is Rs 6,000 + 11% of Rs 600,000 = Rs 72,000 a year, or Rs 6,000 a month. Take-home pay is Rs 144,000 a month.

Questions

How much tax is deducted from a salary of Rs 100,000 a month in 2026-27?

Rs 500 a month. Rs 100,000 a month is Rs 1,200,000 a year, and the tax is 1% of the Rs 600,000 above the tax-free Rs 600,000, which is Rs 6,000 a year.

How much tax is deducted from a salary of Rs 150,000 a month?

Rs 6,000 a month. The yearly salary of Rs 1,800,000 is taxed Rs 6,000 + 11% of the Rs 600,000 above Rs 1,200,000, which is Rs 72,000 a year.

Up to what salary is there no income tax?

A taxable salary up to Rs 600,000 a year, which is Rs 50,000 a month, has no income tax in tax year 2027.

What is the highest rate on salary in 2026-27?

35%, on the part of a yearly taxable salary above Rs 7,000,000. The tax at that level is Rs 1,424,000 plus 35% of the amount above Rs 7,000,000.

Is there a surcharge on salary income in tax year 2027?

The FBR's rate card for tax year 2027 shows no surcharge on salary income. It lists a surcharge only for some other income, such as large pensions.

Which period is tax year 2027?

1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, the 2026-27 budget year. The rates come from the Finance Act 2026.

Why is my average tax rate lower than my slab rate?

Only the part of your salary inside your slab is taxed at the slab rate. The parts below it are taxed at the lower rates, so the tax divided by the whole salary is always lower.

Does this include my allowances?

Type your taxable salary: your pay after any part the law exempts. Your payslip or your employer's tax certificate shows the taxable figure.
