What the sales tax calculator does

Sales tax is a tax on buying goods and some services, charged as a percentage of the price and collected by the seller, who pays it over to the government. This sales tax calculator works three ways. It adds sales tax to a price, takes the tax back out of a total that already includes it, or finds the rate from the price before and after tax. Each answer shows the tax amount, both prices, the rate and the steps of the sum.

For US shoppers it holds the base sales tax rate of every state, as in effect on 1 January 2026, and lets you add your city or county rate on top. The same maths works for any tax added to a price, so you can also use it for Pakistan's 18% sales tax, Canada's GST and HST, or VAT, by typing your own rate and picking your currency.

How to use it

  • Add tax: enter the price before tax, then either keep Type my own rate and enter the sales tax rate, or pick a state from the Tax rate menu and enter the local (city and county) rate. The result is the price with tax.
  • Take tax out: enter the price with tax from a receipt or price tag, then the rate in the same way. The result is the price before tax and the tax that was inside the total.
  • Find the rate: enter the price before tax and the price with tax. The result is the rate that was charged, to three decimals.
  • Currency: pick the symbol at the top (dollars, rupees, pounds, euros and more); it changes only how amounts are shown.

Rates can have up to three decimals, such as 8.875% or 4.225%. Under the result, a collapsed table lists every state's base rate with notes on special cases.

The formulas, and why you divide to remove tax

Tax is the price times the rate: at 7%, a $60 item carries $60 × 0.07 = $4.20 of tax, so you pay $64.20. Finding the rate is the reverse: the tax divided by the price before tax, times 100.

Working backwards from a total is where most mistakes happen. The tax was worked out on the lower, pre-tax price, so you cannot take 7% off $64.20; that gives $59.71, not $60. Divide the total by 1 plus the rate instead: $64.20 ÷ 1.07 = $60.00. The calculator's Take tax out tab does exactly that.

In a spreadsheet, with the price in A2 and the rate in percent in B2: the tax is =A2*B2/100, the total is =A2*(1+B2/100), and the price before tax from a total in C2 is =C2/(1+B2/100). Round the final tax to the cent only at the end, so small rounding errors do not add up.

US sales tax rates by state in 2026

There is no federal sales tax in the United States. Forty-five states and the District of Columbia charge a statewide sales tax, and in most of them cities, counties and special districts add their own. Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon have no statewide sales tax, though some Alaskan towns charge one.

Worked examples

  • Add tax in Texas: a $1,250 purchase in a city with the full 2% local rate is taxed at 8.25%. Tax is $103.13 and the price with tax is $1,353.13.
  • Find the rate from a receipt: an item priced $48.00 that cost $51.90 at the till carried $3.90 of tax, and $3.90 ÷ $48.00 × 100 = 8.125%.
  • Take tax out in Pakistan: a bill of Rs 11,800 that includes 18% sales tax is Rs 11,800 ÷ 1.18 = Rs 10,000 before tax, so Rs 1,800 is sales tax.
  • Canada: Ontario charges 13% HST, so C$80 becomes C$90.40. Quebec charges 5% GST plus 9.975% provincial tax, 14.975% together, so C$80 becomes C$91.98.

Sales tax on a car

Vehicle purchases are taxed too, often with their own rules. In Texas, motor vehicle sales tax is 6.25% of the sales price minus any trade-in allowance, and a Texas dealer collects it from the buyer, according to the Texas Comptroller.

So a $32,000 car with an $8,000 trade-in is taxed on $24,000: enter $24,000 and 6.25% in Add tax to get $1,500 of tax. Other states, such as Missouri and Arkansas, set their own vehicle rates, local add-ons and trade-in rules, so check your state's motor vehicle office before you budget.

To see the tax inside a monthly car payment, use the Auto Loan Calculator, which applies state sales tax rules to the loan.

Sales tax in Pakistan

In Pakistan, sales tax on goods is charged under the Sales Tax Act, 1990, collected by the Federal Board of Revenue. The standard rate in the Act, as amended up to 30 June 2026, is 18% of the value of taxable supplies made by a registered person and of goods imported into Pakistan.

Two rules change what a buyer pays. When a registered seller supplies a buyer who is not registered or not an active taxpayer, a further tax of 4% of the value is added on top of the 18%. Goods listed in the Third Schedule are taxed at 18% of their printed retail price, so the sales tax is already inside the price on the pack.

Sales tax on services is a provincial tax, collected by bodies such as the Punjab Revenue Authority and the Sindh Revenue Board under their own laws and rates. Check the provincial authority for the rate on a restaurant bill, phone package or other service.

  • Tax on a price before tax: price × 18 ÷ 100. Rs 25,000 + 18% = Rs 25,000 + Rs 4,500 = Rs 29,500.
  • Tax inside a total: total − total ÷ 1.18. Rs 29,500 ÷ 1.18 = Rs 25,000, so the tax is Rs 4,500.
  • With further tax for an unregistered buyer: price × 22 ÷ 100, so Rs 25,000 carries Rs 5,500.

Deducting sales tax on a US tax return

If you itemize deductions on Schedule A, you can deduct either your state and local income taxes or your state and local general sales taxes, not both. In states with no income tax, such as Texas or Florida, the sales tax choice is the one that helps.

You can add up actual receipts or use the IRS optional sales tax tables, and the IRS Sales Tax Deduction Calculator does the table sum for you. All state and local taxes together are capped at $40,000 for 2025 ($20,000 if married filing separately), $40,400 for 2026, and less at very high incomes. To see whether itemizing beats the standard deduction, try the Income Tax Calculator.

  • Use the full rate where you buy, state plus local, not just the state rate.
  • To remove tax, always divide by 1 plus the rate; never subtract the rate from the total.

How it works

Choose what you know. Add tax: type the price and the rate to get the tax and the total. Take tax out: type a total that already includes tax and the rate to get the price before tax. Find the rate: type both prices. For the rate you can type your own or pick a US state, which fills in the state's base rate, and then add your city or county rate on top. Results update as you type.

The formula

  • Tax = price before tax × rate ÷ 100; price with tax = price before tax + tax
  • Price before tax = price with tax ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100)
  • Rate = (price with tax − price before tax) ÷ price before tax × 100

A worked example

A $1,250 laptop bought in a Texas city that charges the full 2% local rate: 6.25% state + 2% local = 8.25%. Tax = $1,250 × 8.25 ÷ 100 = $103.13, so the price with tax is $1,353.13.

Questions

How do I calculate sales tax?

Multiply the price by the rate and divide by 100, then add the result to the price. At 6.5%, a $40 item has $40 × 6.5 ÷ 100 = $2.60 of tax, so the total is $42.60. Use the full local rate, not only the state rate.

How do I calculate sales tax backwards from a total?

Divide the total by 1 plus the rate. At 8%, a $54 total is $54 ÷ 1.08 = $50 before tax. Taking 8% off $54 instead would give $49.68, which is wrong, because the tax was worked out on the lower price.

How do I find the sales tax percentage from a total?

Subtract the price before tax from the total to get the tax, divide the tax by the price before tax and multiply by 100. A $20 item that cost $21.45 carried $1.45 of tax: $1.45 ÷ $20 × 100 = 7.25%.

What is the sales tax formula?

Tax = price × rate ÷ 100, and price with tax = price × (1 + rate ÷ 100). For example, $150 at 9% gives $13.50 of tax and a total of $163.50. The reverse is price = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100).

Why is the rate at the shop higher than the state rate?

Most states let cities, counties and special districts add their own sales tax. The state rate in the list is only the base; add your local rate in the box below it. Your state's tax department has a lookup by address for the exact total.

Which states have no sales tax?

Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon have no general sales tax, and Alaska has no state sales tax, though many Alaskan towns charge their own.

Does it work by ZIP code?

No, it does not look up addresses. Pick your state for the base rate and type the local rate for your city or county, which your state's tax department lists by address or ZIP code.

What is the sales tax rate in Pakistan in 2026?

The standard rate on goods under the Sales Tax Act, 1990 is 18%, as amended up to 30 June 2026. A further 4% applies when the buyer is not registered or not an active taxpayer, and services are taxed by the provinces at their own rates.

How do I calculate sales tax in Pakistan?

For a price before tax, multiply by 18 and divide by 100: Rs 10,000 carries Rs 1,800, so the total is Rs 11,800. For a total that includes tax, divide by 1.18 to get the price before tax. Pick Rs at the top and type 18 as your rate.

Can I use it for VAT, GST or Canadian HST?

Yes, the maths is the same for any tax added on top of a price. Type your own rate, for example 13% for Ontario HST or 14.975% for Quebec's GST and provincial tax together, and pick your currency symbol at the top.
