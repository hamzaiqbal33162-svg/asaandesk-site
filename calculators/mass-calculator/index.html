What the mass calculator does

This mass calculator finds the mass of a material from its density and its volume, using the formula m = ρ × V. Type the density and volume in whatever units you have, or pick a common material such as water, steel, copper or gold, and the mass appears with the working shown.

The answer comes in the mass unit of the density you chose, and a table gives the same mass in kilograms, grams, milligrams, metric tons, pounds, ounces, short and long tons, carats, grains and atomic mass units.

How to use the mass calculator

  • Material: pick one of the listed materials to use its typical density, or keep "Type my own density".
  • Density (ρ): type the density and choose its unit, from kg/m³ and g/cm³ to lb/ft³, lb/gal, g/L or psi per 1,000 ft.
  • Volume (V): type the volume and choose its unit: cubic meters, feet, yards or inches, liters, milliliters, US or UK gallons, pints and more.
  • Read the mass, the steps that convert the volume into the density's unit, and the table of other units.

Mass formulas for physics and chemistry

Mass can be worked out in several ways, depending on what you know. The calculator uses the first one; the others need a different tool or a simple division.

The concentration case works here directly: choose g/L as the density unit, type the concentration and the volume, and the mass of what is dissolved comes out in grams. The molar mass for the moles case comes from the molecular weight calculator.

Two chemistry terms sound alike but mean other things. The mass number of an atom is the count of its protons and neutrons (12 for carbon-12), a whole number with no unit. Mass percent is the mass of one part ÷ the total mass × 100; the molecular weight calculator shows it for each element of a compound.

Mass and weight are not the same

Mass is the amount of matter in an object, measured in kilograms. It does not change with place. Weight is the force of gravity on that mass, W = m × g, measured in newtons. With standard gravity of 9.80665 m/s², a 70 kg person weighs 686.47 N on Earth. On the Moon, where surface gravity is about 1.62 m/s², the same 70 kg weighs only about 113.4 N.

Everyday scales show kilograms because they assume Earth's gravity, which is why people use "weight" for mass in daily life. To get the mass from a weight in newtons, divide by g; to turn a mass into a weight in newtons, multiply by g.

Since the 2018 revision of the SI, the kilogram itself is defined by fixing the Planck constant at exactly 6.626 070 15 × 10⁻³⁴ J s, a constant of nature that does not change.

Worked examples in metric and US units

  • Water: 1 US gallon of water at 20 °C (998.21 kg/m³) has a mass of 3.778635897 kg, or 8.33 lb.
  • Gold: 10 cm³ of gold at 19,282 kg/m³ is 0.19282 kg, about 192.8 g or 0.425 lb, more than twice the mass of the same volume of steel.
  • Concrete-like fill in US units: 2 yd³ of a material at 150 lb/ft³ becomes 54 ft³ × 150 lb/ft³ = 8,100 lb, which the table shows as 4.05 US short tons or 3,674.098197 kg.
  • Salt solution: 2.5 L at 9 g/L holds 22.5 g of dissolved salt.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Match the units before you multiply. 7,850 kg/m³ × 1,884.96 cm³ is not 14.8 million kg: the cubic centimeters must become cubic meters first, which the calculator does for you.
  • Density changes with temperature, especially for liquids and gases. Water is 999.97 kg/m³ at 4 °C and 998.21 kg/m³ at 20 °C, so pick the value for your conditions.
  • Listed densities are typical values. Alloys, wood, soil and concrete vary from batch to batch, so use a measured density when the mass really matters.
  • For a hollow or mixed object, work out each solid part separately and add the masses, or measure the mass on a scale and the volume by water displacement.
  • Mass is not weight in newtons. If a question gives a force, divide by g to get the mass.

How it works

Enter the density of the material and the volume you have, each with its unit, or pick a common material to fill in the density. Mass is simply density times volume. The calculator turns the volume into the volume unit of the density (cubic feet for lb/ft³, liters for g/L ...), multiplies, and gives the mass in the density's own mass unit. Below it you get the same mass in kilograms, grams, pounds, ounces, tons, carats, grains and atomic mass units. Mass is how much matter there is; it is the same on the Moon as on Earth, while weight (the pull of gravity on it) is not.

The formula

  • m = ρ × V
  • Volume in the density's unit first: for 62.4 lb/ft³ and 10 ft³, m = 10 × 62.4 = 624 lb
  • Exact factors: 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, 1 grain = 64.79891 mg, 1 carat = 200 mg, 1 US short ton = 2,000 lb, 1 UK long ton = 2,240 lb
  • 1 slug = 1 lbf·s²/ft ≈ 14.5939 kg; 1 psi per 1,000 ft of fluid ≈ 2.30666 kg/m³ (standard gravity 9.80665 m/s²)
  • 1 atomic mass unit (dalton) = 1.66053906892 × 10⁻²⁷ kg (CODATA 2022)

A worked example

A tank holds 3 liters of water at 1 g/cm³. Convert the volume to cubic centimeters: 3 L = 3,000 cm³. Then m = 3,000 cm³ × 1 g/cm³ = 3,000 g, which is 3 kg or about 6.61 lb.

Questions

How do I calculate mass from density and volume?

Multiply them: m = ρ × V, with the volume in the same unit as the density's volume part. 1,884.96 cm³ of steel at 7,850 kg/m³ is 0.00188496 m³ × 7,850 kg/m³ = 14.8 kg. The calculator converts the units for you and shows each step.

How do I calculate mass from weight?

Divide the weight in newtons by the acceleration of gravity: m = W ÷ g. With standard gravity of 9.80665 m/s², a weight of 686.47 N is a mass of 70 kg. A scale that reads in kilograms already shows mass.

What is the difference between mass and weight?

Mass is the amount of matter in an object and is measured in kilograms or pounds of mass. Weight is the force of gravity on that mass. On Earth people use the two words for the same thing, but an astronaut keeps the same mass in space while weighing almost nothing.

What is the mass formula in physics?

The most common ones are m = ρ × V (density times volume), m = W ÷ g (weight over gravity) and m = F ÷ a (force over acceleration, from Newton's second law). The SI unit of mass is the kilogram.

How do I calculate mass in chemistry?

Multiply the amount in moles by the molar mass: m = n × M. Sodium chloride has a molar mass of 58.44 g/mol, so 0.5 mol of it is 29.22 g. For a solution, multiply the concentration in g/L by the volume in liters.

How do I calculate the mass of a steel bar or cylinder?

Find the volume first: π × radius² × length for a round bar. Then pick Structural steel (7,850 kg/m³) and type that volume. A 20 mm bar 6 m long is 1,884.96 cm³, which is about 14.8 kg.

How do I find the density of my material?

Pick it from the material list if it is there, look it up in a data sheet, or measure it: weigh a sample and divide by its volume. For an odd shape, the volume is the amount of water it pushes aside when dropped into a measuring jug.

Why is my answer in pounds when I typed liters?

The answer comes in the mass unit of the density you chose (lb for lb/ft³, g for g/L). The table under it gives the same mass in kilograms, pounds and nine other units.

What is the mass of the Earth?

NASA gives Earth's mass as about 5.9722 × 10²⁴ kg, with a mean density of 5,513 kg/m³. The Moon is about 0.07346 × 10²⁴ kg, a little over 1% of Earth's mass.

What is psi per 1,000 feet?

It is used in drilling and well work: how fast the pressure in a column of fluid rises with depth. Fresh water rises about 433 psi every 1,000 feet, which matches a density of about 999 kg/m³.
