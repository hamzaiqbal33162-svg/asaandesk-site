How it works

Enter the home price, your down payment as a % of the price, the term and the yearly rate. The calculator finds the monthly principal and interest payment and follows the loan month by month, so you can see how much of each payment is interest and how the balance falls, by year or by month. With home costs switched on, property tax (a % of the price), insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA and other yearly costs are added one twelfth a month, and the cost table shows them for one month and over the whole loan. Mortgage insurance stops once you owe 80% of the price.

The formula

  • Loan L = price × (1 − down % ÷ 100)
  • Monthly rate r = yearly rate ÷ 100 ÷ 12; payments n = years × 12
  • Monthly payment M = L × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n); at 0% interest M = L ÷ n
  • Each month: interest = balance × r; principal = M − interest; new balance = balance − principal
  • Each yearly cost is paid as one twelfth a month; property tax per year = price × tax % ÷ 100
  • PMI is paid with each payment that leaves the balance above 80% of the price

A worked example

A 500,000 home with 20% down is a 400,000 loan. At 7.282% for 30 years, r = 0.07282 ÷ 12 and n = 360, so M = 2,737.39 a month and 585,461.26 of interest over the loan. Property tax of 1.2% (500 a month), insurance of 2,500 a year (208.33) and 5,000 a year of other costs (416.67) bring the monthly total to 3,862.39.

Questions

Why does the balance fall so slowly at first?

Interest is charged on the balance you still owe, and at the start that balance is largest. On a 30-year loan most of each early payment is interest; the principal part grows every month, so the balance falls faster in later years.

When does mortgage insurance stop?

Here PMI is counted until the balance after a payment drops to 80% of the home price, when most lenders let you ask to cancel it. US rules also end it automatically at 78% of the original value if your payments are up to date.

Is this the same as the mortgage calculator?

It is a simpler page that puts the schedule first. Use the mortgage calculator for a down payment in money, cost rises each year, extra payments or paying every two weeks.

Are property tax and insurance part of my mortgage payment?

The headline number is principal and interest only, the part your lender sets. Many lenders also collect tax and insurance with each payment; the second number adds them so you can see what leaves your account each month.
