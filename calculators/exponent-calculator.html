How it works

An exponent says how many times to multiply a number by itself: 2⁵ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32. Choose what you want to find and fill in the other two values. You can type whole numbers, decimals, fractions such as 1/3, or the letter e. A negative exponent means 1 divided by the power, and a fraction exponent takes a root: 8^(2/3) is the cube root of 8, squared, which is 4. Whole powers are worked out exactly, so even 2^1000 shows every digit.

The formula

  • x^n = x × x × … × x (n copies) for a whole n
  • x^(−n) = 1 ÷ x^n
  • x^(p/q) = (q-th root of x)^p; the cube root (or any odd root) of a negative number is negative
  • Any other exponent: x^n = e^(n × ln x), for x > 0
  • Solve for the base: x = y^(1/n); solve for the exponent: n = ln(y) ÷ ln(x)

A worked example

1.1^−2.5: the exponent −2.5 is −5/2, so take the square root of 1.1 (1.0488088481702) and raise it to the power −5. That is 1 ÷ 1.0488088481702⁵ = 1 ÷ 1.2690587062858 = 0.78798561094677.

Questions

What does a negative exponent mean?

It means 1 divided by the positive power. So 2⁻³ = 1 ÷ 2³ = 1 ÷ 8 = 0.125. The bigger the negative exponent, the closer the answer gets to 0.

What does a fraction exponent mean?

The bottom of the fraction is a root and the top is a power. 27^(1/3) is the cube root of 27, which is 3, and 27^(2/3) is that cube root squared, 9. Decimals work the same way: 2.5 is 5/2.

Why is (−8)^(1/3) a real number but (−4)^(1/2) is not?

An odd root of a negative number is real: −2 × −2 × −2 = −8, so the cube root of −8 is −2. An even root of a negative number cannot be real, because any real number squared is positive. For those, the calculator shows the complex answer, such as 2i.

What is 0 to the power 0?

It is taken as 1, which keeps the power rules and many formulas simple. Some books call it undefined, and the result shows a note about that.

How big can the numbers be?

Whole powers are exact with any number of digits, and the result shows every digit up to 400 digits (and the digit count beyond that). Other powers keep 14 significant digits and switch to the × 10ⁿ form for very big or very small results.
