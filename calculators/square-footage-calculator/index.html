How it works

Pick the shape and type its sizes; each box has its own unit menu, so you can mix feet, yards, inches, meters and more. The area appears as you type in square feet (or square meters in the metric view), with square yards, acres and a table of other units. Use Quantity for several identical shapes, Several rooms to add up to 12 rooms, and Price to see the cost of flooring, turf or paving at a price per square foot, square yard or square meter.

The formula

  • Rectangle = length × width; parallelogram = base × height; triangle = base × height ÷ 2
  • Border inside a rectangle = L × W − (L − 2b) × (W − 2b)
  • Circle = π × diameter² ÷ 4; ring = π ÷ 4 × (D² − (D − 2b)²)
  • Triangle from three sides (Heron): √(s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)) with s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2
  • Trapezoid = (base 1 + base 2) ÷ 2 × height; sector = angle ÷ 360° × π × r² (or r² × angle ÷ 2 in radians)
  • Total = area × quantity; 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m²; 1 acre = 43,560 ft²; cost = area in the price unit × price

A worked example

A room 30 ft by 20 ft is 600 ft², which is 66.67 yd², 55.74 m² or 0.0138 acres. Flooring at $5 per square foot costs $3,000.

Questions

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Split it into two rectangles, open Several rooms and enter each rectangle as its own row. The total is the square footage of the whole room. The same works for any room made of rectangles.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

Multiply square feet by 0.09290304, or divide by about 10.764. The calculator shows both at once: switch to the metric view to see square meters first.

What is the rectangle border shape for?

It gives the area of a strip of a given width running inside the edge of a rectangle, such as a path around a lawn, a tile border around a floor or a frame. The middle part is left out.

How many square feet are in an acre?

An acre is exactly 43,560 square feet, about 4,047 square meters. A square plot of one acre is about 208.7 feet on each side.

Should I buy extra flooring?

Yes. Cutting around walls and doorways wastes material, so most installers add 5 to 10 percent, or 15 percent for diagonal or patterned layouts. For tiles, the Tile Calculator works out the pieces and boxes directly.
