What square footage is

Square footage is the area of a flat surface measured in square feet (sq ft or ft²): the number of 1 ft × 1 ft squares that would cover it. A square footage calculator finds that number for a room, a floor, a wall, a lawn or a plot, so you can compare home sizes, order flooring or paint, or price a job quoted per square foot.

For a rectangle, square footage is simply length × width in feet. A room 12 ft by 10 ft is 120 sq ft. Because area multiplies two lengths, the units are squared: 1 square yard is 3 ft × 3 ft = 9 sq ft, and 1 square foot is 12 in × 12 in = 144 square inches. One square foot is exactly 0.09290304 square meters, so 1 m² is about 10.764 sq ft.

Real rooms and plots are often not plain rectangles. This calculator handles nine shapes (rectangle, border strip, circle, ring, triangle from three sides or from base and height, trapezoid, sector and parallelogram) and adds up as many as 12 separate rooms.

How to use the square footage calculator

  • Choose the shape: Rectangle, Border, Circle, Ring, Triangle (3 sides), Triangle (base, height), Trapezoid, Sector, Parallelogram, or Several rooms.
  • Type the sizes. Every box has its own unit menu: feet, yards, inches, miles, meters, kilometers or centimeters. You can also type feet and inches together, such as 12' 6" or 12ft 6in.
  • For several identical shapes, such as four walls of the same size or ten paving slabs, set Quantity.
  • For a whole flat or house, choose Several rooms and type the length and width of each room; rooms 4 to 12 are in the extra group below the first three.
  • To price the job, type a Price and choose whether it is per ft², per m², per yd², per in² or per cm². The cost appears in your chosen currency.
  • Switch between US and metric to choose whether square feet or square meters lead the result.

How to calculate square footage of a room, an L-shaped space and a house

One room. Measure the longest length and the widest width at floor level and multiply. A bedroom 12 ft 6 in by 11 ft 3 in is 12.5 × 11.25 = 140.63 sq ft (13.06 m²). Turn inches into feet by dividing by 12 first: 6 in is 0.5 ft and 3 in is 0.25 ft; or let the calculator take the feet and inches as typed.

L-shaped room. Split it into two rectangles that do not overlap. A living room made of an 18 ft × 14 ft part and a 10 ft × 8 ft part is 252 + 80 = 332 sq ft; enter the two parts as Room 1 and Room 2 under Several rooms.

Whole flat or house. Add every room. Three rooms of 12 ft 6 in × 11 ft 3 in, 10 ft × 9 ft 6 in and 8 ft × 6 ft come to 283.63 sq ft. Listings and builders do not always count the same spaces (walls, stairs, garages, balconies or unfinished basements), so ask what a quoted house size includes before you compare prices per square foot.

Worked examples: flooring, walls, a round patio and a garden

Flooring cost. That 140.63 sq ft bedroom at $4.25 per square foot costs $597.66 in material. Many installers add 5 to 10 percent for cutting, more for diagonal or patterned layouts, so order a little more than the bare area.

Walls for paint or wallpaper. Four walls 14 ft long and 9 ft high are 14 × 9 = 126 sq ft each; with Quantity 4 the total is 504 sq ft. Take away doors and windows by working out their areas separately, then divide by the coverage printed on the paint tin to find how much to buy, and multiply by the number of coats.

Round patio. A circle 12 ft across is π × 12² ÷ 4 = 113.1 sq ft.

Irregular garden. For a triangular bed with sides of 20, 25 and 30 ft, Triangle (3 sides) uses Heron's formula and gives 248.04 sq ft. Most irregular shapes can be split into triangles and rectangles like this and added up.

Path around a lawn. A 3 ft path running inside the edge of a 40 ft × 30 ft garden is a Border: 40 × 30 − 34 × 24 = 384 sq ft of paving.

Square feet for plots and homes in Pakistan

In Pakistan, flats, shops and the covered area of houses are usually measured and sold in square feet, while plots are also described in marla and kanal. A plot 25 ft by 45 ft is 1,125 sq ft (125 square yards or 104.52 m²). If a contractor quotes a rate per square foot, type it as the price: at an example rate of Rs 180 per sq ft, 1,125 sq ft costs Rs 202,500.

The size of a marla is not the same in every scheme and city, so for marla and kanal use the Area Calculator linked below, which shows the area in those units and explains the conversion it uses. Always check the marla size written in your allotment letter or sale deed.

Square footage formulas for every shape

  • Rectangle or square: length × width.
  • Triangle: base × height ÷ 2, with the height measured square to the base; or Heron's formula from the three sides.
  • Circle: π × radius², which is π × diameter² ÷ 4.
  • Ring (a round path around a pond): π ÷ 4 × (outer diameter² − inner diameter²).
  • Trapezoid: (base 1 + base 2) ÷ 2 × height, where the bases are the two parallel sides.
  • Sector (a slice of a circle): angle ÷ 360° × π × radius².
  • Parallelogram: base × height, with the height measured square to the base, not along the slanted side.
  • From inches: square inches ÷ 144 = square feet. A countertop 96 in × 25.5 in is 2,448 in², or 17 sq ft.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Do not add feet and inches as decimals: 10 ft 6 in is 10.5 ft, not 10.6 ft.
  • Do not mix units in your head. 30 ft × 20 yd is 30 × 60 = 1,800 sq ft, not 600; give each box its own unit instead.
  • Measure each wall or floor at its widest point, and split odd shapes into simple ones rather than guessing.
  • For a sloped roof, the surface is larger than the floor plan below it; use the Roofing Calculator, which allows for the pitch.
  • For tiles, the Tile Calculator counts whole tiles and boxes, including the grout gaps and waste.

How it works

Pick the shape and type its sizes; each box has its own unit menu, so you can mix feet, yards, inches, meters and more. The area appears as you type in square feet (or square meters in the metric view), with square yards, acres and a table of other units. Use Quantity for several identical shapes, Several rooms to add up to 12 rooms, and Price to see the cost of flooring, turf or paving at a price per square foot, square yard or square meter.

The formula

  • Rectangle = length × width; parallelogram = base × height; triangle = base × height ÷ 2
  • Border inside a rectangle = L × W − (L − 2b) × (W − 2b)
  • Circle = π × diameter² ÷ 4; ring = π ÷ 4 × (D² − (D − 2b)²)
  • Triangle from three sides (Heron): √(s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)) with s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2
  • Trapezoid = (base 1 + base 2) ÷ 2 × height; sector = angle ÷ 360° × π × r² (or r² × angle ÷ 2 in radians)
  • Total = area × quantity; 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m²; 1 acre = 43,560 ft²; cost = area in the price unit × price

A worked example

A bedroom 12 ft 6 in long and 11 ft 3 in wide is 12.5 × 11.25 = 140.63 ft², which is 15.63 yd² or 13.06 m². Flooring at $4.25 per square foot costs $597.66 before any extra for cutting.

Questions

How do I calculate square footage?

For a rectangle, multiply the length by the width, both in feet. For other shapes use their area formula, or split the space into rectangles and triangles and add them up. A room 12 ft by 10 ft is 120 sq ft.

How do I calculate square footage from inches?

Multiply the length and width in inches and divide by 144, because a square foot is 12 × 12 = 144 square inches. 96 in × 25.5 in is 2,448 in², which is 17 sq ft.

How do I calculate square footage with feet and inches?

Turn the inches into a fraction of a foot (divide by 12), then multiply. 12 ft 6 in × 11 ft 3 in is 12.5 × 11.25 = 140.63 sq ft. The calculator also accepts 12' 6" typed directly.

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Split it into two rectangles, open Several rooms and enter each rectangle as its own row. The total is the square footage of the whole room. The same works for any room made of rectangles.

How do I calculate square footage of a wall?

Multiply the wall's length by its height, then subtract windows and doors. A wall 14 ft long and 9 ft high is 126 sq ft; use Quantity for several identical walls.

How do I calculate the square footage of a circle?

Square the diameter, multiply by π and divide by 4. A circle 12 ft across is 113.1 sq ft.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

Multiply square feet by 0.09290304, or divide by about 10.764. The calculator shows both at once: switch to the metric view to see square meters first.

How many square feet are in an acre?

An acre is exactly 43,560 square feet, about 4,047 square meters. A square plot of one acre is about 208.7 feet on each side.

What is the rectangle border shape for?

It gives the area of a strip of a given width running inside the edge of a rectangle, such as a path around a lawn, a tile border around a floor or a frame. The middle part is left out.

Should I buy extra flooring?

Yes. Cutting around walls and doorways wastes material, so most installers add 5 to 10 percent, or 15 percent for diagonal or patterned layouts. For tiles, the Tile Calculator works out the pieces and boxes directly.
