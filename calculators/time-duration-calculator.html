What the time duration calculator does

This time duration calculator finds the exact time between two clock times, down to the second, or between two dates with clock times. It answers questions like how long a shift, an exam, a flight or a recording lasted, and it handles spans that run past midnight.

The result is shown as hours, minutes and seconds, and also as total hours, total minutes and total seconds (and total days on the dates tab), so you can copy whichever form a time sheet, a report or a spreadsheet needs.

How to use it

  • Pick a tab: Between two times for a span within a day or overnight, or Between two dates and times for anything longer.
  • Start time: type the hour, minute and second, then choose AM, PM or 24-hour on the Clock switch. An hour from 13 to 23, or 0, is read as 24-hour time on its own.
  • End time: the same. On the dates tab, also pick the Start date and End date.
  • Read the result. On the first tab an end time earlier than the start is counted on the next day, and a note says so. On the dates tab an end before the start is an error, so check the dates.

How to calculate time duration by hand

The cleanest method turns both times into seconds after midnight. Use the 24-hour clock first (add 12 to PM hours from 1 to 11; 12 AM is 0). Then seconds = hours × 3,600 + minutes × 60 + seconds. For 7:48:35 AM that is 25,200 + 2,880 + 35 = 28,115; for 4:12:10 PM, which is 16:12:10, it is 57,600 + 720 + 10 = 58,330. The difference, 30,215 seconds, splits back into 8 hours (28,800), 23 minutes (1,380) and 35 seconds.

The column method works on paper too: subtract seconds, then minutes, then hours, borrowing when the top number is smaller. 16:12:10 − 7:48:35: 10 seconds is less than 35, so borrow a minute (70 − 35 = 35 seconds, 11 minutes left); 11 minutes is less than 48, so borrow an hour (71 − 48 = 23 minutes, 15 hours left); 15 − 7 = 8 hours. The answer is again 8:23:35.

If the result is negative, the span crossed midnight: add 24 hours (86,400 seconds). 10:40:00 PM to 2:15:30 AM gives −73,470 seconds, and −73,470 + 86,400 = 12,930 seconds, or 3 hours, 35 minutes, 30 seconds, which matches the calculator.

Time duration formula in Excel and Google Sheets

Spreadsheets keep a time as a fraction of a day, so the time duration formula is a plain subtraction. With the start time in A2 and the end time in B2, =B2-A2 gives the duration; format the cell as h:mm:ss, or as [h]:mm:ss when it can be 24 hours or more.

To get one number instead: =(B2-A2)*24 for hours, =(B2-A2)*1440 for minutes and =(B2-A2)*86400 for seconds. For a span that passes midnight use =MOD(B2-A2,1) so the result is never negative. When the cells hold a date and a time together, =B2-A2 gives the duration in days, and the same multipliers convert it. Google Sheets uses the same formulas.

Common durations and time units

Durations convert with fixed factors: 1 minute = 60 seconds, 1 hour = 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds and 1 day = 24 hours = 1,440 minutes = 86,400 seconds. The second itself is defined by atomic clocks, which count 9,192,631,770 oscillations of the microwave radiation of caesium atoms.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Check AM and PM. 12 AM is midnight and 12 PM is noon, so 12:00 AM to 12:00 PM is 12 hours.
  • Use the dates tab for anything over 24 hours; the first tab always assumes the end is within a day of the start.
  • Daylight saving time is not counted. On the night clocks go forward or back, the real duration is an hour shorter or longer than the clock difference.
  • Totals are rounded for display (hours to 3 decimals, minutes to 2); the hours, minutes and seconds line is exact.
  • To find an end time from a start time and a duration instead, add the duration with the time calculator.
  • In physics the time difference is Δt = t₂ − t₁, the later reading minus the earlier one, which is exactly what this calculator works out.

How it works

Fill in the hour, minute and second of the start and end, and choose AM, PM or the 24-hour clock for each. The answer shows the time between them in hours, minutes and seconds, and also as total hours, total minutes and total seconds. If the end time is earlier in the day than the start, the end is taken as the next day, so 11:45 PM to 1:05 AM is 1 hour 19 minutes 50 seconds. To measure across days, open the two dates tab and give each time a date.

The formula

  • Seconds after midnight = hour × 3,600 + minute × 60 + second (12 AM is hour 0, 1-11 PM add 12 hours)
  • Same day: duration = end − start; when that is negative, add 24 hours (86,400 seconds)
  • Two dates: duration = (end date and time) − (start date and time)
  • Total hours = seconds ÷ 3,600, total minutes = seconds ÷ 60, total days = seconds ÷ 86,400

A worked example

From 11:45:20 PM to 1:05:10 AM: 11:45:20 PM is 85,520 seconds after midnight and 1:05:10 AM is 3,910 seconds. 3,910 − 85,520 is negative, so add 86,400: 4,790 seconds. That is 1 hour, 19 minutes, 50 seconds, or about 1.331 hours or 79.83 minutes.

Questions

How do I calculate time duration?

Take the start time from the end time. It is easiest in seconds: turn both times into seconds after midnight, subtract, and turn the answer back into hours, minutes and seconds. If the answer is negative, add 24 hours, because the end is on the next day.

How do I calculate time duration in hours and minutes?

Subtract the start from the end, borrowing 60 minutes from the hours when the end has fewer minutes. 7:48 AM to 4:12 PM is 16:12 − 7:48 = 8 hours 24 minutes. The calculator shows the exact hours, minutes and seconds and the total in hours or minutes.

How do I calculate time duration in Excel?

Use =B2-A2 with the start in A2 and the end in B2, formatted as h:mm:ss. Multiply by 24, 1,440 or 86,400 for hours, minutes or seconds, and use =MOD(B2-A2,1) when the span passes midnight.

How do I calculate time duration in Google Sheets?

The same way as in Excel: =B2-A2 for the duration, =(B2-A2)*24 for decimal hours, and =MOD(B2-A2,1) for spans past midnight. Format the result as a duration to show hours, minutes and seconds.

How do I find the duration between two dates and times?

Open the Between two dates and times tab and enter both dates and times. From 6:00 PM on November 22, 2026 to 9:30:30 AM on November 25, 2026 is 2 days, 15 hours, 30 minutes, 30 seconds.

What if the end time is after midnight?

Just type it as it shows on the clock. When the end time is earlier in the day than the start time, the calculator counts the end on the next day, which is what you want for a night shift or a late film.

How do I enter a 24-hour time like 18:30?

Type 18 in the hour box and 30 in the minute box. Any hour from 13 to 23 (or 0) is read as the 24-hour clock on its own, or pick 24-hour on the switch to make it clear.

Can it subtract a lunch break?

Not directly; it measures the whole span. Take the break away from the answer yourself, or use the time card calculator, which has a break box for each day.

Why are the totals rounded?

Total hours are shown to 3 decimals and total minutes to 2, which is enough to read and to copy into a time sheet. The hours, minutes and seconds line above them is always exact.

Does it allow for daylight saving time?

No. It counts clock time. On the night your clocks go forward or back, the real time that passes is an hour shorter or longer than shown.
