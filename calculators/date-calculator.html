What the date calculator does

This date calculator answers two everyday questions. The first tab finds the time between two dates, as years, months and days, as weeks and as calendar days, and also counts the weekend days, holidays and business days in between. The second tab adds or subtracts years, months, weeks and days, or business days, to give you a new date.

People use it to count the days between dates for a notice period, a visa, a loan or a project; to find a deadline such as 90 days from today; to work out a retirement or contract end date; and to count working days for leave or payroll. Every result comes with a month calendar, so you can see the dates and any holidays at a glance.

How to use the calculator

Pick a tab, fill in the boxes and the answer updates as you type. The settings under Weekends and holidays apply to both tabs.

  • Time between dates: enter a Start date and an End date. The start date is counted and the end date is not; tick "Count the end date too" when both days should count, such as the days of a festival. If the end date is earlier, the two dates are swapped and a note says so.
  • Add or subtract: enter a Start date, choose Add or Subtract, and fill in any mix of Years, Months, Weeks and Days. Empty boxes count as zero.
  • Business days: on the Add or subtract tab, tick "Count business days only". The Days box then holds working days, and weekends and holidays are stepped over.
  • Weekend: choose Saturday and Sunday, Friday and Saturday, or Sunday only, for a six-day working week.
  • Business days skip: choose Weekends and holidays, or Weekends only to ignore holidays.
  • A holiday on a weekend: either it gives a day off on the nearest working day, the US federal rule, or it gives no extra day off.
  • Holidays: the 11 US federal holidays are ticked at first, and three common company days off can be added. Untick any that do not apply, and type your own in "Your own holidays": a date, then a name, separated by semicolons, up to 40 of them. A date without a year repeats every year.

Should the end date count?

The most common reason two date calculators disagree by one day is the end date. Counting from Monday to Friday gives 4 days if you count the nights or the gaps between the days, and 5 days if you count every day you are at work. Neither is wrong; they answer different questions.

This calculator leaves the end date out by default, which suits stays, loans, notice periods and "how many days until" questions. Tick "Count the end date too" for events, leave applications and anything where both the first and last day are used. Excel's NETWORKDAYS function counts both ends: Microsoft's own example gives 110 workdays from October 1, 2012 to March 1, 2013, which matches this calculator only with the end date ticked.

Worked examples

Adding business days in the US. Ten business days after Friday, November 20, 2026 is Monday, December 7, 2026 with the default holidays, because Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26 is skipped as well as two weekends. Switch "Business days skip" to Weekends only and the answer becomes Friday, December 4.

Working days in Pakistan. An office in Pakistan with a six-day week wants its working days in February 2027. Choose Sunday only as the weekend, untick Presidents' Day, type "Feb 5 Kashmir Day" under your own holidays, and count from February 1 to February 28 with the end date ticked. The month has 28 days, 4 Sundays and 1 holiday, which leaves 23 working days.

A retirement or superannuation date. Open the Add or subtract tab, enter the date of birth as the start date and add the retirement age in years that your service rules set. For a retirement age of 60, someone born on March 14, 1967 reaches it on Sunday, March 14, 2027. Service rules say whether the last working day is the birthday itself or the day before, so check yours.

Adding a month to the end of a month. One month after January 31, 2027 is February 28, 2027, because February has no 31st. Years and months are added first and the day is kept inside the shorter month; weeks and days are added after that.

Weekends, holidays and business days

Business days are the calendar days minus the weekend days minus the holidays that fall on a working day. The calculator never takes the same day off twice. With the US rule, a holiday on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before and one on a Sunday on the Monday after, as the US Office of Personnel Management does for federal staff. In 2027 that moves Juneteenth to Friday, June 18, Independence Day to Monday, July 5, and Christmas to Friday, December 24. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so Friday, December 31, 2027 is a day off too.

In Pakistan, the Cabinet Division announces each year's public holidays in a circular. The 2026 list, issued on January 19, 2026, included Kashmir Day on February 5, Pakistan Day on March 23 and Independence Day on August 14. Islamic holidays such as Eid are subject to the sighting of the moon, so their final dates come in later notifications: for Eid ul Fitr 2026, offices were closed on March 20 and 21. Type the dates that apply to you under your own holidays, with the year for moon-dependent days.

This calculator works on the Gregorian calendar only. It does not convert dates to or from the Islamic (Hijri) calendar, so enter Gregorian dates for Eid and other religious holidays once they are announced.

How the Gregorian calendar came to be

The calendar on your phone is the Gregorian calendar, and its rules decide how many days every month and year has. It replaced the Julian calendar, which added a leap day every fourth year without exception. That made the average Julian year 365.25 days, slightly too long, so the calendar slowly drifted against the seasons.

By the 16th century the spring equinox, which the Council of Nicaea had placed on March 21, had moved by about ten days. In the bull Inter gravissimas of 1582, Pope Gregory XIII ordered ten days to be dropped: the day after Thursday, October 4, 1582 became Friday, October 15. To stop the drift coming back, century years stopped being leap years unless they divide by 400, which removes three leap days every 400 years. The bull even told judges to add the ten missing days to payment due dates, so nobody lost money on the change.

Countries switched at different times. Great Britain and its colonies changed under the Calendar (New Style) Act 1750: the day after September 2, 1752 was September 14, so 11 days were left out, and the year started on January 1 from then on. The Act also made sure that rents, leases and the age of 21 still fell on the same real days.

With 97 leap days in every 400 years, the Gregorian cycle is exactly 146,097 days, which is 20,871 whole weeks. That is why the calendar repeats with the same weekdays every 400 years, and why this calculator can work out any date from the year 1 to 9999 with whole-day arithmetic.

Date differences in Excel and Google Sheets

In a spreadsheet, subtracting one date cell from another gives the calendar days between them, the same as this calculator with the end date not counted. For working days, NETWORKDAYS(start, end, holidays) leaves out Saturdays, Sundays and the holiday dates you list, and counts both the start and the end date. NETWORKDAYS.INTL lets you choose a different weekend.

To add days, add a number to a date cell, for example =A1+90. For months, Excel's EDATE moves a date by whole months. If a spreadsheet and this page disagree by one day, check whether the end date is being counted.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Decide first whether the end date counts, then tick or untick the box to match.
  • For a deadline counted in business days, check the holiday list for the right country and year before you trust the date.
  • Adding 1 month to the 29th, 30th or 31st can land on the last day of a shorter month, so 1 month after January 31 is February 28 or 29.
  • Adding 12 months and adding 365 days are not always the same, because a leap day may fall inside the span.
  • Moon-dependent holidays change every year. Enter them with the year, such as "2027-03-10 Eid holiday", once the official notice is out.

How it works

The first tab measures the time between two dates: as years, months and days, as total months, weeks and calendar days, and as the weekend days, holidays and business days inside it. The start date is counted and the end date is not, unless you tick the box to count it too. The second tab moves a date forward or back by years, months, weeks and days, or by business days that skip weekends and holidays. Under Weekends and holidays you can choose the weekend, tick the holidays that apply and type your own.

The formula

  • Calendar days = end date − start date (+ 1 when the end date is counted too)
  • Years, months and days: whole calendar months from the start (one fewer when the start day of the month is later than the end day), then the days left
  • Business days = calendar days − weekend days − holidays that fall on working days
  • A holiday on a weekend gives its day off on the nearest working day (Saturday to Friday, Sunday to Monday), or no extra day off; either way it is never subtracted twice
  • Adding: years and months first (a missing day such as Feb 30 becomes the last day of the month), then weeks × 7 + days
  • Adding business days: the start counts as business day 1 when it is one; the new date is the next business day after the last one counted

A worked example

Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2026 is 11 months 30 days, or 364 calendar days. It holds 104 weekend days and, with the US federal holidays, 11 days off (Independence Day falls on Saturday, July 4, so Friday, July 3 is the day off), which leaves 364 − 104 − 11 = 249 business days.

Questions

How do I calculate the days between two dates?

Enter the two dates on the Time between dates tab. You get the gap as years, months and days, as weeks and as calendar days. The first day is counted and the last one is not, unless you tick "Count the end date too".

Is the end date included in the count?

Not by default: from Monday to Friday is 4 days, the way a stay or a loan period is usually counted. Tick "Count the end date too" when both days should count, for example for the days of an event; that adds 1 day.

How do I add days to a date?

Open the Add or subtract tab, enter the start date, choose Add and type the number of days, weeks, months or years. The new date is shown with its weekday and on a calendar. Choose Subtract to go back in time instead.

Which holidays are used for business days?

The 11 US federal holidays are ticked at first, and three common company days off (the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve) can be ticked too. Untick the ones that do not apply, or type your own, such as "Aug 14 Independence Day; 2027-03-10 Eid holiday". A date without a year counts every year.

Can I count working days in Pakistan?

Yes. Untick the US holidays that do not apply, type Pakistan's holidays under your own holidays, and pick Sunday only as the weekend if your office works six days a week. Islamic holidays depend on the moon, so add them with the year once they are announced.

What happens when a holiday falls on a weekend?

It is never taken off twice. By default the day off moves to the nearest working day, as US federal offices do: a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday and a Sunday one on the Monday. You can choose "Gives no extra day off" instead, then the weekend day is simply a day off already.

How are business days added to a date?

The start date counts as business day 1 when it is a business day, and the answer is the next business day after the last one counted. So 10 business days after Friday, Nov 20, 2026 is Monday, Dec 7 with the default US holidays (Thanksgiving is skipped), or Friday, Dec 4 if you count weekends only.

How do I calculate my retirement date from my date of birth?

Use the Add or subtract tab: enter your date of birth and add the retirement age in years set by your service rules. For a retirement age of 60, a birth date of March 14, 1967 gives March 14, 2027. Your rules decide whether you retire on that day or the day before.

What is one month after January 31?

February has no 31st, so the answer is the last day of February: Feb 28 in 2026 or Feb 29 in a leap year. Years and months are added together first, then weeks and days.

How many working days are there in 2027?

With a Saturday and Sunday weekend and the US federal holidays, January 1 to December 31, 2027 holds 365 days, 104 weekend days and 12 holiday days off, which leaves 249 business days. With your own holidays the number will differ, so enter them to see yours.

Does this calculator convert Islamic (Hijri) dates?

No. It works on the Gregorian calendar only. Enter the Gregorian date of an Islamic holiday once it has been announced, and the calculator counts it like any other day off.

Why did October 1582 lose ten days?

The old Julian calendar ran about ten days behind the seasons by then. Pope Gregory XIII's reform dropped October 5 to 14, 1582 and changed the leap year rule so century years are leap years only when they divide by 400.
