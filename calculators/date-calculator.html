How it works

The first tab measures the time between two dates: as years, months and days, as total months, weeks and calendar days, and as the weekend days, holidays and business days inside it. The start date is counted and the end date is not, unless you tick the box to count it too. The second tab moves a date forward or back by years, months, weeks and days, or by business days that skip weekends and holidays. Under Weekends and holidays you can choose the weekend, tick the holidays that apply and type your own.

The formula

  • Calendar days = end date − start date (+ 1 when the end date is counted too)
  • Years, months and days: whole calendar months from the start (one fewer when the start day of the month is later than the end day), then the days left
  • Business days = calendar days − weekend days − holidays that fall on working days
  • A holiday on a weekend gives its day off on the nearest working day (Saturday to Friday, Sunday to Monday), or no extra day off; either way it is never subtracted twice
  • Adding: years and months first (a missing day such as Feb 30 becomes the last day of the month), then weeks × 7 + days
  • Adding business days: the start counts as business day 1 when it is one; the new date is the next business day after the last one counted

A worked example

Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2026 is 11 months 30 days, or 364 calendar days. It holds 104 weekend days and, with the US federal holidays, 11 days off (Independence Day falls on Saturday, July 4, so Friday, July 3 is the day off), which leaves 364 − 104 − 11 = 249 business days.

Questions

Is the end date included in the count?

Not by default: from Monday to Friday is 4 days, the way a stay or a loan period is usually counted. Tick "Count the end date too" when both days should count, for example for the days of an event; that adds 1 day.

Which holidays are used for business days?

The 11 US federal holidays are ticked at first, and three common company days off (the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve) can be ticked too. Untick the ones that do not apply, or type your own, such as "Aug 14 Independence Day; 2027-03-10 Eid holiday". A date without a year counts every year.

What happens when a holiday falls on a weekend?

It is never taken off twice. By default the day off moves to the nearest working day, as US federal offices do: a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday and a Sunday one on the Monday. You can choose "Gives no extra day off" instead, then the weekend day is simply a day off already.

How are business days added to a date?

The start date counts as business day 1 when it is a business day, and the answer is the next business day after the last one counted. So 10 business days after Friday, Nov 20, 2026 is Friday, Dec 4, and the dates between the two always hold exactly the number you asked for.

What is one month after January 31?

February has no 31st, so the answer is the last day of February: Feb 28 in 2026 or Feb 29 in a leap year. Years and months are added together first, then weeks and days.
