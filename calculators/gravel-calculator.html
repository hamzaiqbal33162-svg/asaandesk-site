How it works

Choose the shape of the area (a rectangle, a circle or an area you already know), type its size and the depth of the layer, and pick the material. The volume and weight appear as you type: in the US view tons and cubic yards lead (how gravel is usually sold there), in the metric view metric tons and cubic meters. Every box has its own unit menu. Under Price, bags and extra you can enter your supplier's price per volume, per weight or per bag, and add a margin for the settling that happens when gravel is spread and compacted.

The formula

  • Area: rectangle = length × width; circle = π × (diameter ÷ 2)²
  • Volume = area × depth × (1 + extra %)
  • Weight = volume × density (gravel 1,680 kg/m³, gravel with sand 1,920, dry sand 1,500, wet sand 1,900)
  • 1 short ton = 2,000 lb = 907.18474 kg; 1 metric ton = 1,000 kg; 1 yd³ = 27 ft³ = 0.764554857984 m³
  • Bags = volume or weight ÷ bag size, rounded up; cost = amount in the price unit × price

A worked example

A rectangle 20 ft × 15 ft covered 4 in deep with gravel and sand: 300 ft² × 1/3 ft = 100 ft³ (3.7 yd³, 2.83 m³). At 1,920 kg/m³ that is about 5,437 kg, 11,986 lb or 5.99 short tons, so at $30 per short ton the gravel costs $179.79.

Questions

How deep should a gravel layer be?

Decorative paths and beds are often 2 to 3 inches (5 to 8 cm) deep. A driveway usually has a base of larger crushed stone 4 to 8 inches (10 to 20 cm) deep with a top layer of finer gravel 2 to 4 inches (5 to 10 cm) deep. Work out each layer separately if they use different stone.

Why does the weight depend on the material?

Loose gravel has air between the stones, so a cubic meter weighs about 1,680 kg, while gravel mixed with sand fills those gaps and weighs about 1,920 kg. Wet sand is heavier than dry sand. If your supplier gives a density, choose Type my own density and enter it.

Should I order extra gravel?

Usually yes. Gravel settles and packs down when it is spread and compacted, and edges are rarely exact, so many people add 10 to 15 percent. Put the margin in the Extra box and every total includes it.

What is the difference between a short ton and a metric ton?

A short ton, used in the United States, is 2,000 pounds (907 kg). A metric ton, or tonne, is 1,000 kg (about 2,205 lb). Check which one your supplier means before comparing prices.
