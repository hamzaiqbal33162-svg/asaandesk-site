How it works

Type how far you will drive, how much fuel your car uses and what the fuel costs. Fuel economy can be in miles per US or UK gallon, litres per 100 km, kilometres per litre or litres per mile, and the price can be per litre or per gallon: the calculator turns everything into litres and kilometres first, so mixed units are fine. It then multiplies the fuel needed by the price. Under More options you can double the distance for a round trip and split the cost between the people in the car. A table shows the same trip at other fuel economy figures, so you can see what a thirstier or a more frugal car would cost.

The formula

  • Fuel (US gallons) = miles ÷ mpg
  • Fuel (litres) = km × L/100 km ÷ 100, or km ÷ (km/L)
  • Cost = fuel × price (gallons are turned into litres when the price is per litre: 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L)
  • 1 mile = 1.609344 km; 1 UK gallon = 4.54609 L
  • Cost per person = cost ÷ number of people

A worked example

A 300-mile trip in a car that does 25 mpg needs 300 ÷ 25 = 12 US gallons. At $3.50 a gallon that is 12 × 3.50 = $42.00. The same car is 9.41 L/100 km, so with fuel at Rs 280 a litre the 482.8 km trip uses 45.42 litres and costs about Rs 12,719.

Questions

Where do I find my car's fuel economy?

The best figure is your own: fill the tank, note the distance until the next fill and divide (the gas mileage calculator does this for you). Otherwise use the figure in the owner's manual or an official rating, and expect real driving to use a little more.

Can I mix units, such as mpg with a price per litre?

Yes. Every figure is turned into litres and kilometres before the sums, so an mpg car with a price per litre, or a distance in miles with L/100 km, all work.

Why is the real cost often higher?

Fuel economy changes with speed, traffic, hills, cold weather, roof boxes, heavy loads and air conditioning. For a safe budget add 10 to 15% to the result.

How do I share the cost of a road trip?

Open More options, tick round trip if you are coming back, and set the number of people. The calculator shows each person's share of the fuel. Tolls and parking are not included.
