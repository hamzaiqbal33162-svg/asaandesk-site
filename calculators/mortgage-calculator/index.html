How it works

Type the home price, your down payment, the loan term and the yearly interest rate. The calculator finds the one monthly payment that clears the loan by the end of the term, then follows the loan month by month: each month's interest is the balance times the monthly rate, and the rest of the payment lowers the balance. Property tax, insurance, PMI, HOA and other costs are added on top of that payment. Extra payments go straight to the balance, so the loan ends sooner and you pay less interest.

The formula

  • Loan L = home price − down payment
  • Monthly rate r = yearly rate ÷ 100 ÷ 12; number of payments n = years × 12
  • Monthly payment M = L × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n); at 0% interest, M = L ÷ n
  • Each month: interest = balance × r; principal = M − interest + any extra; new balance = balance − principal
  • Yearly costs in % are a share of the home price (PMI: of the loan), paid as one twelfth a month
  • PMI is paid while the balance after a payment is above 80% of the home price

A worked example

A $400,000 home with 20% down is a $320,000 loan. At 6.5% for 30 years, r = 0.065 ÷ 12 ≈ 0.0054167 and n = 360, so M = 320,000 × 0.0054167 ÷ (1 − 1.0054167^−360) = $2,022.62 a month. The 360 payments add up to $728,142.36, so $408,142.36 of it is interest.

Questions

Does the monthly payment include property tax and insurance?

The big number is principal and interest only, the part your lender sets. With taxes and costs switched on, the second number adds one twelfth of each yearly cost, which is closer to what leaves your account each month if the lender collects tax and insurance with the payment.

When does mortgage insurance (PMI) stop?

Here PMI is counted until the balance after a payment drops to 80% of the home price, when most lenders let you ask to cancel it. US rules also end it automatically at 78% of the original value if your payments are up to date.

How much do extra payments save?

Every extra dollar lowers the balance right away, so less interest builds up in every month that follows. Open Extra payments, add an amount every month, every year or once, and the results show the new payoff date and the interest you save.

Why does paying every two weeks finish the loan sooner?

Half the monthly payment every two weeks is 26 half payments a year, the same as 13 monthly payments instead of 12. The extra payment each year goes to the balance, which usually takes several years off a 30-year loan.

Can I use it for a loan in rupees, pounds or another currency?

Yes. Pick your currency symbol at the top of the calculator. The maths is the same for any currency; the symbol only changes how the amounts are shown.
