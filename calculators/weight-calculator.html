What the weight calculator converts

This weight calculator converts an amount between 22 units of weight: kilograms, grams, milligrams and tonnes; pounds, ounces, grains, stone, quarters and long and short tons; carats and atomic mass units; and the South Asian tola, masha, ratti, ser and maund. Type an amount, pick the two units, and the answer appears with the same amount in every other unit.

It uses the exact legal definitions, such as 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg and 1 tola = 180 grains, and keeps up to 14 significant digits, so a gold weight in tola or a parcel weight in pounds comes out without rounding errors.

How to use it

  • Amount: type the number you want to convert, such as 72.5.
  • From: pick the unit the amount is in, for example kilograms.
  • To: pick the unit you want, for example pounds.
  • Read the result, the two one-unit lines (1 kilogram in pounds and 1 pound in kilograms), the amount in kilograms, and the table with every unit.
  • Use the Copy button to copy the full-precision answer.

For a quick mental estimate of kilograms to pounds, double the kilograms and add 10%: 72.5 × 2 = 145, plus 14.5 makes 159.5, close to the exact 159.84. To go from pounds to kilograms, halve the pounds and take off 10% of the result.

Tola, masha, ratti, ser and maund in Pakistan

The tola is the traditional South Asian unit for gold and silver. It is defined as 180 grains, which is 11.6638038 g; a tola is 12 masha and a masha 8 ratti, so a ratti is about 0.1215 g. If a gold rate is quoted per tola, convert the grams on your receipt to tola here: 50 g is 4.2867662091504 tola, and 2.5 tola is 29.1595095 g.

For grain, flour and other produce, the market ser is 1 kg and the market maund 40 kg, so 5 maund is 200 kg. Older records may use the old ser of 80 tola (933.1 g) and the old maund of 40 old ser (37.32 kg), so 5 old maund is only 186.62 kg. Both are in the lists, so check which one a bill or document means.

How to calculate weight in kg and the weight formula in physics

To turn any unit into kilograms, multiply by the number of kilograms in one of that unit: pounds × 0.45359237, ounces × 0.028349523125, stone × 6.35029318, tola × 0.0116638038. To go the other way, divide by the same number.

In physics, weight is a force, not a mass: W = m × g, measured in newtons, where g is the acceleration due to gravity. With standard gravity of 9.80665 m/s², a 72.5 kg person weighs 710.98 N on Earth. On the Moon, where surface gravity is about 1.62 m/s², the same 72.5 kg weighs only 117.45 N, while the mass stays 72.5 kg. Everyday scales show kilograms or pounds because they assume Earth's gravity.

For an object of known size, weight starts from density: mass = density × volume, and then W = m × g. The mass calculator does the first step for any material.

Steel bar, metal and body weight

The weight of a steel bar, plate or pipe depends on its volume and density, so use the mass calculator with Structural steel (7,850 kg/m³). A 12 mm round bar 12 m long has a volume of π × 0.6² × 1,200 = 1,357.17 cm³, which the mass calculator turns into 10.65 kg. The builders' shortcut D² ÷ 162 kg per meter (D in mm) gives 144 ÷ 162 × 12 = 10.67 kg, close to the same.

For a healthy body weight by height, age or BMI, this unit converter is the wrong tool: use the BMI calculator or the ideal weight calculator, and then convert the result to stone, pounds or kilograms here.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Ton is not one unit. A tonne (metric ton) is 1,000 kg, a US short ton 2,000 lb (907.18 kg) and a UK long ton 2,240 lb (1,016.05 kg).
  • The UK stone is 14 lb; the rarely used US stone is 12.5 lb. For body weight in Britain and Ireland, pick stone (UK).
  • Troy ounces, used for precious metals, are a different unit from the ounce here (the avoirdupois ounce of 1/16 lb), so do not mix them.
  • Check which maund or ser a document means; the old and market units differ by about 7%.
  • Weight and mass are used as the same thing in everyday life; only in physics is weight a force in newtons.

How it works

Type an amount and pick the unit it is in and the unit you want. The amount is turned into kilograms with the exact size of the first unit, then divided by the size of the second. The table under the result shows the same amount in every unit at once. Results keep up to 14 significant digits, so small differences are not rounded away.

The formula

  • Result = amount × (kilograms in 1 'from' unit) ÷ (kilograms in 1 'to' unit)
  • 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg exactly; 1 ounce = 1/16 pound; 1 grain = 64.79891 mg; 1 carat = 200 mg
  • 1 stone (UK) = 14 lb; 1 stone (US) = 12.5 lb; 1 quarter (UK) = 28 lb; 1 quarter (US) = 25 lb; long ton = 2,240 lb; short ton = 2,000 lb; tonne = 1,000 kg
  • 1 tola = 180 grains = 11.6638038 g = 12 masha = 96 ratti
  • Market ser = 1 kg and maund = 40 kg; old ser = 80 tola (933.1 g) and old maund = 40 old ser (37.32 kg)
  • 1 atomic mass unit (dalton) = 1.66053906892 × 10^-27 kg (CODATA 2022)

A worked example

70 kg in pounds: 70 × 1 ÷ 0.45359237 = 154.32358352941 lb. And 10 tola of gold in grams: 10 × 11.6638038 = 116.638038 g.

Questions

How do I convert kg to lbs?

Divide the kilograms by 0.45359237, or multiply by 2.2046226218488. 72.5 kg is 159.84 lb. Pick kilograms in From and pounds in To and the calculator gives the exact figure.

How do I calculate weight in kg?

Multiply the amount by the kilograms in one of its unit: pounds × 0.45359237, stone × 6.35029318, ounces × 0.028349523125. So 180 lb is 81.65 kg and 12 stone is 76.2 kg.

How many grams are in a tola?

One tola is 11.6638 grams (180 grains), so 10 grams is about 0.857 tola. A tola is 12 masha, so a masha is about 0.972 g, and a masha is 8 ratti of about 0.1215 g each.

How many kilograms is one maund?

In markets today a maund is 40 kg, made of 40 ser of 1 kg each. The older maund was 40 ser of 80 tola each, which is 37.32 kg. Both are in the lists, so check which one your bill or market uses.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

1 kg is 2.2046226218488 pounds, because a pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kg. For a quick estimate, double the kilograms and add 10% (70 kg is about 154 lb).

What is the weight formula in physics?

Weight is the force of gravity on a mass: W = m × g. With standard gravity of 9.80665 m/s², a 72.5 kg mass weighs 710.98 newtons. The mass in kilograms does not change from place to place, but the weight does.

How do I calculate the weight of a steel bar?

Find the bar's volume (π × radius² × length) and multiply by steel's density of about 7,850 kg/m³, or use the mass calculator. A 12 mm bar 12 m long is about 10.65 kg; the shortcut D² ÷ 162 per meter gives nearly the same.

What is a stone?

A UK stone is 14 pounds (6.35 kg), still used in Britain and Ireland for body weight: 11 stone is 154 lb or about 70 kg. The less common US stone is 12.5 pounds.

Is a ton the same as a tonne?

No. A tonne (metric ton) is 1,000 kg. A US short ton is 2,000 lb (907.2 kg) and a UK long ton is 2,240 lb (1,016 kg). Choose the one you mean from the list.

Can it tell me my ideal weight by height and age?

No, it only converts between units. For a healthy weight range use the BMI calculator or the ideal weight calculator, then convert the answer here if you need it in stone or pounds.
