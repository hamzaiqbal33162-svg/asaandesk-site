What area is, and how it differs from perimeter

An area calculator gives the size of a flat surface: how much floor a carpet covers, how much wall a tin of paint must reach, or how big a plot of land is. Area is counted in square units. A square that is 1 meter on each side has an area of 1 square meter (1 m²), so a floor 4 m long and 3 m wide holds 4 × 3 = 12 of those squares and has an area of 12 m².

In math, area is the amount of two-dimensional space inside a closed outline. Length runs along a line and is measured in meters or feet, area covers a surface and is measured in square meters or square feet, and volume fills a space in cubic units. Because area multiplies two lengths, doubling every side of a shape makes its area four times larger, and tripling every side makes it nine times larger.

This page covers the seven shapes behind most homework, building and land questions: rectangle (including the square), triangle, trapezoid, circle, sector, ellipse and parallelogram. Each answer comes with the formula and your numbers in it, a labelled drawing, and the same area in acres, hectares, square feet, square meters, marla and kanal.

Perimeter is the distance around a shape and is measured in plain units such as meters or feet. Area is the surface inside the outline and is measured in square units. You need the perimeter for a fence, a boundary wall or skirting board, and the area for flooring, paint, tiles, grass seed or the price of land.

How to use this area calculator, step by step

  • Pick the shape from the tabs: Rectangle, Triangle, Trapezoid, Circle, Sector, Ellipse or Parallelogram. For a square, use Rectangle and type the same number as the length and the width.
  • Choose the Unit you measured in: feet, inches, yards, miles, meters, kilometers, centimeters or millimeters. Every length box then uses that one unit.
  • Type the lengths. The boxes start with sample values (30 by 20 for a rectangle, sides of 30, 45 and 50 for a triangle) so a result shows before you type anything.
  • Triangle needs all three sides (Side a, Side b, Side c). Any two of them added together must be longer than the third, otherwise no triangle exists and the page says so.
  • Trapezoid needs the Top base (b₁), the Bottom base (b₂) and the Height (h), the straight distance between the two parallel sides.
  • Sector needs the Radius (r), the Central angle (θ) and the Angle unit, degrees or radians.
  • Ellipse needs the Semi-major axis (a), half of the longest width, and the Semi-minor axis (b), half of the shortest width.
  • Parallelogram needs the Base (b) and the Height (h) measured at a right angle to the base, not along the slanted side.
  • Mixed units: open Units and tick Use a different unit for each length. Each box gets its own unit menu, and the area is worked out once in every unit you used.

Worked examples for every shape

Room in square feet. A bedroom is 14 ft long and 12 ft wide. Area = 14 × 12 = 168 ft². In square meters that is 168 × 0.09290304 = 15.61 m². If a carpet is priced at PKR 450 per square foot, the carpet alone comes to 168 × 450 = PKR 75,600; the price is only an example, so use your own quote.

Square. A square courtyard has 5 m sides, so its area is 5 × 5 = 25 m². A square twice as wide, 10 m on a side, is 100 m², four times bigger, which shows how area grows with the square of the length.

Triangle from three sides. A triangular garden has sides of 13 m, 14 m and 15 m. First the semiperimeter: s = (13 + 14 + 15) ÷ 2 = 21. Then Heron's formula: A = √(21 × 8 × 7 × 6) = √7,056 = 84 m². You can check it another way: the height onto the 14 m side is 12 m, and ½ × 14 × 12 = 84 m².

Triangle from base and height. A sail has a base of 3 m and a height of 4.5 m measured straight up from the base. Area = ½ × 3 × 4.5 = 6.75 m². The slanted edges do not enter this formula at all; only the base and the perpendicular height do.

Trapezium. A garden bed has parallel sides of 8 m and 12 m that are 5 m apart. Area = (8 + 12) × 5 ÷ 2 = 50 m². This is the same as a rectangle as wide as the average of the two bases (10 m) and as tall as the height (5 m). Pakistani and British school books call this shape a trapezium; American books call it a trapezoid.

Circle from its diameter. A round table top is 1.4 m across, so its radius is 0.7 m. Area = π × 0.7² = 0.49π ≈ 1.539 m². With π taken as 22/7, as many school books do, the answer is 22/7 × 0.49 = 1.54 m². Using the diameter in place of the radius would give four times the true area, 6.16 m².

Sector. One slice of a 30 cm pizza cut into six equal pieces is a sector with r = 15 cm and θ = 60°. Area = 60 ÷ 360 × π × 15² = 37.5π ≈ 117.81 cm². In radians the angle is π/3, and θr² ÷ 2 = (π/3) × 225 ÷ 2 gives the same 37.5π.

Ellipse. An oval rug is 2 m long and 1.2 m wide, so its semi-axes are 1 m and 0.6 m. Area = π × 1 × 0.6 = 0.6π ≈ 1.885 m². Typing the full length and width by mistake gives 2.4π ≈ 7.54 m², four times too much.

Parallelogram. A parallelogram has a base of 10 cm, slanted sides of 7 cm and a perpendicular height of 6 cm. Area = 10 × 6 = 60 cm². Multiplying the base by the slanted side (10 × 7 = 70 cm²) is one of the most frequent errors in homework.

Area units and how to convert them

Because area is a length times a length, a length conversion factor gets squared. One meter is 100 cm, so 1 m² is 100 × 100 = 10,000 cm², not 100 cm². One yard is 3 ft, so 1 square yard is 9 square feet. The table lists the conversions people need most often.

The foot has been exactly 0.3048 m since 1959, when the international foot was adopted, and in the United States the older survey foot was retired at the end of 2022 (NIST). That is why every factor above is exact. Countries and provinces are measured in km²: the 2023 census tables of the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics give Pakistan's area as 796,096 km², which is about 79.6 million hectares.

Area formulas in physics use the same shapes, with the area in m². Pressure, for example, is force divided by area (P = F ÷ A, and 1 pascal is 1 newton per square meter), so a person weighing 600 N who stands on 0.04 m² of shoe sole presses on the floor with 600 ÷ 0.04 = 15,000 Pa. The same weight on a single 0.01 m² heel gives four times the pressure.

Land area in Pakistan: marla, kanal, square feet and square yards

Plots and houses in Pakistan are bought and sold in marla and kanal. A kanal is always 20 marla, but a marla is not the same size everywhere. Many housing societies and property listings use 225 square feet (25 square yards), some schemes use 250 square feet, and land revenue records use the older 272.25 square feet. The same 10 marla plot can therefore mean 2,250, 2,500 or 2,722.5 square feet.

With the 272.25 ft² marla, 160 marla (8 kanal) make exactly 43,560 ft², which is one acre. A plot 25 ft wide and 45 ft deep covers 1,125 ft²: that is 5 marla at 225 ft², 4.5 marla at 250 ft² and about 4.13 marla at 272.25 ft². In Karachi, plots are often quoted in square yards as well as square feet. A 120 square yard plot is 120 × 9 = 1,080 ft², or about 100.34 m². Before comparing prices per marla or per yard, check which size the allotment letter, sale deed or society rules use.

Irregular shapes and land with four different sides

Most real rooms and plots are not perfect rectangles. The dependable method is to split the shape into pieces this calculator handles, find the area of each piece and add them up, or subtract a cut-out such as a staircase or a pillar. An L-shaped room, for example, splits into a 5 m × 4 m rectangle and a 3 m × 2 m rectangle: 20 + 6 = 26 m².

For a plot with four different sides, measure one diagonal too. The diagonal cuts the plot into two triangles, and the Triangle tab gives each one from its three sides. Take a plot whose sides, in order, are 60 ft, 45 ft, 58 ft and 40 ft, with a 75 ft diagonal from the first corner to the third.

Four sides on their own are not enough. A plot with four 50 ft sides could be a 2,500 ft² square, or a squashed diamond that holds only about 1,961.5 ft² when its long diagonal is 90 ft. The old shortcut of multiplying the averages of opposite sides, (60 + 58) ÷ 2 × (45 + 40) ÷ 2 = 2,507.5 ft² for the plot above, can never come out smaller than the true area and is exact only for a rectangle, so it tends to overstate land.

When you know the corners as coordinates, for example from a GPS app, the shoelace formula gives the area: A = ½ × |x₁y₂ − x₂y₁ + x₂y₃ − x₃y₂ + ... + xₙy₁ − x₁yₙ|. Corners at (0, 0), (30, 0), (36, 20) and (4, 24) in meters give ½ × (0 + 600 + 784 + 0) = 692 m².

On a map, Google Earth's Measure tool lets you click the corners of a plot and close the shape; on a computer it shows both the area and the perimeter. Google describes these as estimates that ignore slopes and are no substitute for a survey, so for boundaries and sale papers rely on the revenue record or a licensed surveyor.

Tips for homework, exams and building jobs

  • Write the answer in square units (cm², m², ft²). A bare number, or cm where cm² is meant, is an easy mark to lose in Matric, O-level and other school papers.
  • Use the value of π the question asks for. Many school books use 22/7 or 3.14; this page uses the full value, so a circle of radius 7 cm shows 49π ≈ 153.94 cm², while 22/7 gives exactly 154 cm².
  • Halve the diameter before using πr². If a question gives the distance across a circle, that is the diameter.
  • For triangles, trapeziums and parallelograms, use the perpendicular height, never the slanted side.
  • Put every length in the same unit before multiplying, or use the mixed-unit option. A strip 2 m by 50 cm is 2 × 0.5 = 1 m², not 100.
  • Square the conversion factor, not just the number: 3 m² is 3 × 10.7639 = 32.29 ft², not 3 × 3.28 = 9.84 ft².
  • For flooring and tiles, buy a little more than the floor area for cuts and breakage; the Tile Calculator adds a waste allowance for you.
  • For a whole room or house with several shapes, the Square Footage Calculator adds the pieces and works out a cost.

How it works

Choose the shape, pick the unit you measured in and type the lengths. The area appears as you type, in that unit squared, with the formula filled in with your numbers and a picture of the shape with each length marked. For round shapes the answer is also shown as a multiple of π when that is a neat number (900π). If you measured some lengths in feet and others in yards, open Units and give each length its own unit: the lengths are converted and the area is worked out once in each unit you used. The table under the result lists the same area in acres, hectares, square feet, square meters and other units, including the marla and kanal used for land and plots in Pakistan.

The formula

  • Rectangle: A = l × w (a square is a rectangle with l = w, so A = s²)
  • Triangle from three sides (Heron): s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2, A = √(s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)); any two sides together must be longer than the third
  • Trapezoid (trapezium): A = (b₁ + b₂) × h ÷ 2, where h is the distance between the parallel bases
  • Circle: A = πr²
  • Sector: A = (θ ÷ 360°) × πr² with θ in degrees, or A = θr² ÷ 2 with θ in radians
  • Ellipse: A = πab, with a and b the semi-axes (half the longest and shortest widths)
  • Parallelogram: A = b × h, with h measured at a right angle to the base
  • Units: 1 ft = 0.3048 m exactly, 1 acre = 43,560 ft², 1 hectare = 10,000 m², 1 kanal = 20 marla

A worked example

A plot 30 ft wide and 75 ft long has an area of 30 × 75 = 2,250 ft². At 225 ft² to the marla that is 2,250 ÷ 225 = 10 marla, or half a kanal; in square meters it is 2,250 × 0.09290304 = 209.03 m².

Questions

How do I find the area of a room or a plot of land?

For a rectangular room or plot, choose Rectangle and type the length and the width. For an L-shaped room, split it into two rectangles, work out each one and add them. The table under the result gives the same area in square meters, acres, marla and kanal.

How many square feet are in a marla and a kanal?

It depends on the place: a marla is 225 square feet in many cities, 250 in some housing schemes and 272.25 in old revenue records. A kanal is always 20 marla, so 4,500, 5,000 or 5,445 square feet. The table shows all three sizes; check your property papers for the one used.

How do I calculate the area of land with four different sides?

Measure one diagonal as well as the four sides. The diagonal splits the plot into two triangles; enter each triangle's three sides on the Triangle tab and add the two areas. Multiplying the averages of opposite sides is only a rough shortcut and overstates the area unless the plot is a rectangle.

What is the area formula of a square, a rectangle and a triangle?

A square is side × side (s²), a rectangle is length × width, and a triangle is half of base × height. When you know a triangle's three sides instead, use Heron's formula, which the Triangle tab does for you.

How do I calculate the area of a circle from the diameter?

Halve the diameter to get the radius, then square it and multiply by π. A circle 10 cm across has r = 5 cm and an area of 25π ≈ 78.54 cm². You can also use πd² ÷ 4, which gives the same answer.

How do I calculate area in square feet from inches or meters?

Pick inches or meters as the unit and enter the lengths; the result row and the unit table give square feet. By hand, divide square inches by 144, or multiply square meters by 10.7639.

What is the formula for the area of a trapezium?

Add the two parallel sides, multiply by the height between them and halve the result: A = (b₁ + b₂) × h ÷ 2. Parallel sides of 8 m and 12 m that are 5 m apart give 50 m². Use the Trapezoid tab, which is the American name for the same shape.

Can I mix feet and inches, or yards and feet?

Yes. Open Units, tick the box and choose a unit for each length. The lengths are converted (for example 20 ft = 6.6666666666667 yd) and the area is given in each unit you used, with the conversion shown in the steps.

What is area in math, and how is it different from perimeter?

Area is the amount of flat surface inside a shape, counted in square units such as m². Perimeter is the length of the edge all the way around, in plain units such as m. A 10 m × 10 m square has an area of 100 m² and a perimeter of 40 m.

What is the area formula of a cylinder?

A cylinder is a solid, so it has a surface area rather than a flat area: two circles plus the curved side, 2πr² + 2πrh. A can with r = 4 cm and h = 10 cm has 32π + 80π = 112π ≈ 351.86 cm². The Surface Area Calculator does this for cylinders, cones, spheres and boxes.

How do I calculate an area in Google Maps or Google Earth?

In Google Earth, open the Measure tool, click each corner of the plot and close the shape; the area and perimeter appear on a computer. Treat it as an estimate, since it ignores slopes, and use the revenue record or a surveyor for anything legal.

How accurate are the answers?

The area is worked out from your exact numbers and shown to 14 significant digits; the table of other units is rounded to 6 significant digits. Unit factors are the exact legal definitions, such as 1 foot = 0.3048 meters.
