How it works

Type a date and a time, pick the place where that time is and the place you want it in. Places are real cities, so their daylight saving time is applied on the exact date you pick (New York is 5 hours behind UTC in winter and 4 in summer), or plain UTC offsets that never change. The result shows the time and date there, both time zones with their UTC offsets, the time difference, the same moment in UTC and in 16 big cities. A time skipped or repeated by a clock change is explained in a note.

The formula

  • UTC time = local time − the UTC offset of the first place on that date
  • Time there = UTC time + the UTC offset of the second place at that moment
  • Time difference = offset of the second place − offset of the first place
  • Offsets and clock changes come from the IANA time zone database, as built into your browser
  • A skipped time (clocks jump forward) is read with the offset from before the jump; a repeated time (clocks go back) is taken the first time

A worked example

3:30 PM on Nov 21, 2026 in Karachi (UTC+05:00) is 10:30 AM UTC. New York is on Eastern Standard Time (UTC-05:00) that day, so it is 5:30 AM there, 10 hours behind. On Jul 15, 2026 New York keeps daylight time (UTC-04:00), and the same 3:30 PM in Karachi is 6:30 AM in New York.

Sources

  • IANA time zone database
  • ECMAScript Internationalization API (time zones in browsers)
  • Temporal: times skipped or repeated by clock changes

Questions

Is daylight saving time included?

Yes, for cities. Each city follows its own clock changes on the date you pick, so London is UTC+00:00 in January and UTC+01:00 in July, and Sydney is UTC+11:00 in January. Pick a fixed UTC offset instead if you want a number that never changes.

What if the time does not exist or happens twice?

On the night clocks jump forward, a time such as 2:30 AM in New York never happens; it is read as 3:30 AM daylight time. On the night clocks go back, 1:30 AM happens twice; the first one is used and the note tells you what the second one would give.

Why does the date change?

Places far apart can be on different days. When it is 9:00 AM on Saturday in Karachi, it is still 11:00 PM on Friday in New York. The result always shows the date in the second place and says whether it is the next day or the day before.

Which time format can I type?

Either 12-hour time such as 3:30 PM or 3.30pm, or 24-hour time such as 15:30, with or without seconds. You can also type noon or midnight. Under More options you can choose how the answers are shown.
