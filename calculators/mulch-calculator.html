How it works

Type the length and width of each bed (up to three are open, nine more are under Beds 4 to 12), or switch to Total area if you already know it, and set the depth. The amount appears as you type in cubic yards or cubic meters, with cubic feet and liters, and the number of bags. Each box has its own unit menu, so a bed measured in feet and another in meters add up correctly. Under Price and bags you can set the bag size you buy and a price per volume or per bag to see the total cost.

The formula

  • Area = sum of length × width of every bed (or the total area you type)
  • Volume = area × depth
  • Bags = volume ÷ bag size, rounded up
  • Cost = volume in the price unit × price, or bags × price per bag
  • 1 yd³ = 27 ft³ = 0.764554857984 m³; 1 ft³ = 28.316846592 L; 1 acre = 43,560 ft²

A worked example

One acre covered 3 inches deep: 43,560 ft² × 0.25 ft = 10,890 ft³, which is 403.33 yd³ or 308.37 m³. In 2 ft³ bags that is 5,445 bags; at $4 a bag the mulch costs $21,780.

Questions

How deep should mulch be?

About 2 to 4 inches (5 to 10 cm) for most beds. Three inches (7.5 cm) keeps most weeds down and holds moisture; deeper layers can starve roots of air. Keep mulch a few inches away from tree trunks and plant stems.

How many bags of mulch make a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so it takes 13.5 bags of 2 cubic feet or 9 bags of 3 cubic feet. In metric, a cubic meter is 1,000 liters, so 20 bags of 50 L.

My beds are odd shapes. What should I do?

Split each bed into rough rectangles and enter each one as its own row. A round bed can be entered as a square of the same width and then reduced by about a fifth, or work out its area with the Square Footage Calculator and use Total area.

Is bulk mulch cheaper than bags?

Usually, once you need more than two or three cubic yards. Enter both prices (per cubic yard for bulk, per bag for bags) to compare the totals, and remember that bulk delivery often has a fee.
