How it works

For each day you worked, type when you started and finished and how long your unpaid break was; leave days off empty (Saturday and Sunday are under their own heading). Times can be typed as 8:30 AM, 8:30, 830 or 17:30, and an end time without AM or PM is read as the first such time after the start, so 9:00 to 5:30 means 5:30 PM. If the end is earlier than the start, the shift ends the next day. Each day's hours are the end minus the start minus the break; they can be rounded to the nearest 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes or the hour. With pay turned on, pick how overtime works: by default hours over 40 in the week are paid at 1.5 times the base rate, the US federal rule, or pick a daily limit, no overtime, or a cap on paid hours.

The formula

  • Day hours = end time − start time − break (plus 24 hours when the end is earlier than the start)
  • Rounded day hours = nearest multiple of the rounding step (7 h 40 min → 7 h 45 min to the quarter hour)
  • Weekly overtime: regular = smaller of total hours and the weekly limit; overtime = total − regular; pay = regular × rate + overtime × rate × overtime multiplier
  • Daily overtime: the same split for each day with the daily limit, each day's pay rounded to the cent, then added up
  • Capped pay without overtime: paid hours = smaller of the hours and the limit (per day or per week) × rate

A worked example

Five days of 8 h, 8 h 15 min, 7 h 40 min, 9 h and 11 h 30 min make 44 h 25 min. At $35 an hour with overtime after 40 hours a week: 40 h × $35 = $1,400.00 base pay, and 4 h 25 min (4.4167 h) × $35 × 1.5 = $231.88 overtime, so the week pays $1,631.88.

Questions

How is overtime worked out?

With the default rule, every hour over 40 in the week is paid at 1.5 times the base rate. That is the US federal rule for most hourly workers. Some places count overtime by the day instead (for example after 8 hours a day); pick the daily limit for that. You can change the limits and the overtime multiplier, for example to 2 for double time.

How do I enter a night shift?

Type the times as they appear on the clock, such as 10:00 PM to 6:30 AM. Because the end is earlier than the start, the shift is counted as ending the next morning, and the table marks it as next day.

How do I type the break?

As hours and minutes, such as 0:30 or 1:00, or as plain minutes, such as 30 or 45. Forms like 45m or 1h 15m work too. Leave it empty or type 0 when there was no unpaid break.

Should I round the hours?

Only if your employer does. US rules allow rounding to the nearest 5, 10 or 15 minutes as long as it evens out over time. The rounding here is to the nearest step, so 7 h 40 min becomes 7 h 45 min and 7 h 37 min becomes 7 h 30 min at 15 minutes.

Can I keep or print my time card?

The page address holds everything you typed, so you can bookmark it or send the link. The table has a download button for a spreadsheet file, and you can add a report title and notes under Day names and report before printing the page.
