The density in other units

How it works

Pick what you want to find, then enter the other two values, each with its own unit. Density tells you how much mass fits into a given volume, so the three are tied together by one rule: density equals mass divided by volume. The calculator first converts your numbers into matching units (for example pounds into kilograms, or gallons into cubic centimeters), then divides or multiplies, and shows each step. To find a volume or a mass you can also pick a common material, such as water, steel or gold, and its typical density is filled in for you.

The formula

  • Density: ρ = m ÷ V
  • Volume: V = m ÷ ρ
  • Mass: m = ρ × V
  • Exact unit factors: 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, 1 in = 2.54 cm, 1 US gallon = 231 in³ = 3.785411784 L, 1 UK gallon = 4.54609 L, 1 carat = 0.2 g
  • Specific gravity = density ÷ density of water at 4 °C (999.97 kg/m³)

A worked example

A metal block has a mass of 8,900 kg and a volume of 1 m³: ρ = 8,900 kg ÷ 1 m³ = 8,900 kg/m³, close to copper or nickel. Going the other way, 2 US gallons of a liquid with a density of 7.85 g/cm³: 2 gal = 7,570.823568 cm³, so m = 7.85 × 7,570.823568 = 59,430.97 g, about 59.43 kg.

Questions

What is density?

Density is the mass packed into each unit of volume. A liter of water has a mass of about 1 kg, while a liter of steel has a mass of about 7.85 kg, so steel is about 7.85 times as dense. Dense materials feel heavy for their size.

Can I mix units, like pounds and liters?

Yes. Each value has its own unit menu. The calculator converts everything into matching units before it divides or multiplies, and the steps show each conversion, so you can check it by hand.

What is specific gravity?

It is the density compared with water at 4 °C. A specific gravity of 2.7 (aluminium) means the material is 2.7 times as dense as water. Anything below 1 floats in fresh water.

Why does the density of a material change?

Temperature and pressure change it a little: most things expand when warm, so they become slightly less dense. Mixtures such as steel, wood or concrete also vary with their exact make-up. The material list gives typical values at room temperature.
