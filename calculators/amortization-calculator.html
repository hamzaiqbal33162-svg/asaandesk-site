How it works

Enter the loan amount, the term and the yearly interest rate. The calculator finds the one monthly payment that repays the loan by the end of the term, then follows it month by month: each month's interest is the balance times the monthly rate, and the rest of the payment lowers the balance. Early on most of the payment is interest; near the end most of it is principal. Under Extra payments you can add an amount every month, once a year or once, or a list of one-time amounts with their months. Extras go straight to the balance, so the payment stays the same, the loan ends sooner and you pay less interest.

The formula

  • Monthly rate r = yearly rate ÷ 100 ÷ 12; payments n = years × 12 + months
  • Monthly payment M = L × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n); at 0% interest M = L ÷ n
  • Each month: interest = balance × r; principal = M − interest + any extra; new balance = balance − principal
  • The last payment only clears what is left; interest saved = interest without extras − interest with extras
  • Paid off in = first payment month + number of payments

A worked example

A 200,000 loan over 15 years at 6%: r = 0.005 and n = 180, so M = 200,000 × 0.005 ÷ (1 − 1.005^−180) = 1,687.71. In the first month the interest is 200,000 × 0.005 = 1,000.00, so 687.71 repays the loan and the balance becomes 199,312.29. Adding 100 every month, 1,000 every June and 5,000 once ends the loan after 149 payments instead of 180 and saves 21,688.62 of interest.

Questions

Why is so much of each early payment interest?

Interest is worked out on the balance you still owe. At the start the balance is largest, so the interest part is too. Each payment lowers the balance a little, the interest part shrinks, and more of the same payment goes to principal.

Do extra payments lower my monthly payment?

No. The scheduled payment stays the same and the extras go straight to the balance, so the loan ends sooner. The results show the new payoff month and the interest you save.

Is an extra payment early worth more than one later?

Yes. Money paid early stops interest building up on it for all the remaining months, so the same extra amount saves more the sooner you pay it.

Can I download the schedule?

Yes. Open the monthly schedule and use the CSV download to keep it in a spreadsheet. The yearly schedule sums each loan year from your first payment month.
