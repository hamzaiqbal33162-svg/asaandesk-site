What a time zone is and what this time zone calculator does

A time zone is an area that keeps the same clock time. Each one is defined by its offset from Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), the world's reference clock: Pakistan Standard Time is UTC+05:00, India Standard Time UTC+05:30, and Eastern Standard Time in New York UTC-05:00. Offsets on the map run from UTC-12:00 to UTC+14:00, and a few are not whole hours, such as India's half hour and Nepal's 45 minutes.

This time zone calculator converts a date and time from one place to another. Pick real cities and it applies each city's own rules, including daylight saving time (summer time) on the exact date you choose, using the IANA time zone database built into your browser. It shows the time and date there, both zones and their offsets, the difference in hours, the same moment in UTC, and a table of 16 major cities, which makes it a handy meeting planner.

UTC and Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) are often used as if they were the same thing; for everyday clock times they agree. London uses GMT (UTC+00:00) in winter and British Summer Time (UTC+01:00) in summer, so London is not always on UTC.

How to use the time zone calculator

  • Date: the day of the time you want to convert. The date matters, because daylight saving rules change the offset during the year.
  • Time: type it in 12-hour form (3:30 PM) or 24-hour form (15:30); seconds are optional, and noon or midnight also work.
  • From (where this time is): choose a city, UTC, or a fixed offset such as UTC+05:00. Pakistan has Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad on the list, all on the same time.
  • To (show the time here): the city or offset you want the time in.
  • Under More options, Show times as switches between 12-hour and 24-hour answers.

Cities follow their clock changes; a fixed UTC offset never changes. Use a city whenever you mean a real place.

Worked examples: meetings, family calls and flights

Meeting with New York. A call at 8:00 PM on Dec 3, 2026 in Karachi is 10:00 AM in New York, which is on Eastern Standard Time: New York is 10 hours behind. The same 8:00 PM on Jul 9, 2026 is 11:00 AM in New York, because New York is on daylight time and only 9 hours behind. Pakistan does not change its clocks, so the gap changes when the other country does.

Family abroad. 8:00 PM in Islamabad is 6:00 PM in Riyadh (2 hours behind) and 7:00 PM in Dubai. 8:00 PM in Lahore is 8:30 PM in Delhi and 8:45 PM in Kathmandu.

London in winter and summer. 8:00 PM in Karachi is 3:00 PM in London in December (GMT) but 4:00 PM in July (British Summer Time).

Flights. A flight leaving Lahore at 2:30 AM on Thursday, Dec 10, 2026 leaves at 9:30 PM on Wednesday, Dec 9 in London time. If the flight takes 9 hours, it lands at about 6:30 AM on Dec 10 in London. Convert the departure to the arrival city's time first, then add the flight time.

Across the date line. 9:00 AM on Friday, Jan 15, 2027 in Sydney is 3:00 AM the same Friday in Karachi, and 10:00 PM on Thursday in UTC: Sydney is on summer time then and 6 hours ahead of Pakistan.

Daylight saving time and the hours that are skipped or repeated

Many countries in Europe and North America move their clocks forward one hour in spring and back in autumn. On those nights an hour disappears or happens twice, and the calculator explains what it did.

On Mar 8, 2026 New York's clocks jumped from 2:00 AM to 3:00 AM, so 2:30 AM did not exist there; the calculator reads it as 3:30 AM daylight time, which was 12:30 PM in Karachi. On Nov 1, 2026 New York's clocks went back, so 1:30 AM happened twice; the first one (UTC-04:00) gives 10:30 AM in Karachi and the second one (UTC-05:00) 11:30 AM, and the note shows both.

Pakistan uses one time zone, Pakistan Standard Time (UTC+05:00, Asia/Karachi in the database), and has not used daylight saving time since 2009. Southern-hemisphere places such as Sydney change their clocks at the opposite time of year, so their summer time falls in December and January.

How to calculate time zones by hand, with longitude or in Excel

To find the time difference, subtract the two UTC offsets: New York (−5) and Karachi (+5) differ by 10 hours in winter. To convert, take away the first place's offset to get UTC, then add the second place's offset. Check whether either place is on summer time that day.

In geography lessons the rule of thumb is 15° of longitude per hour, because the Earth turns 360° in a day of 24 hours. A place 75° east of Greenwich is 75 ÷ 15 = 5 hours ahead of UTC by sun time, which matches Pakistan's UTC+05:00. Real zones follow national borders, not exact lines of longitude, so this gives only a first estimate.

In Excel, times are fractions of a day (12:00 PM is 0.5), so an offset of h hours is h/24. With a Karachi date and time in A1, =A1-10/24 gives New York in winter and =A1-9/24 in summer, or use =A1-TIME(10,0,0). Excel does not know daylight saving dates, so you must choose the right offset yourself, which is exactly what this calculator does for you.

A short history of time zones

Before railways and the telegraph, towns kept their own local solar time. In October 1884, the International Meridian Conference in Washington, D.C., attended by 26 countries, chose the meridian through Greenwich as longitude zero and endorsed a universal day counted from midnight there. It did not set up time zones; countries adopted standard zones on their own over the following decades.

For that reason the calculator warns that zone rules before 1970 come from historical records and may be incomplete, and that before about 1900 many places still kept local solar time.

Tips for planning across time zones

  • Always give a meeting time with its zone or city: 10:00 AM New York time, not just 10:00 AM.
  • Check the date, not just the hour; a late-evening call from Pakistan can fall on the next day in Tokyo or Sydney.
  • Around March, October and November, check again: in 2026 the US changed its clocks on Mar 8 and Nov 1 but the UK on Mar 29 and Oct 25, so the gap between Pakistan and each of them changes on a different weekend.
  • Use a city rather than a fixed offset when you mean a real place, so summer time is applied.
  • For a flight, convert the departure time to the arrival city first, then add the flight duration with the Time Calculator.

How it works

Type a date and a time, pick the place where that time is and the place you want it in. Places are real cities, so their daylight saving time is applied on the exact date you pick (New York is 5 hours behind UTC in winter and 4 in summer), or plain UTC offsets that never change. The result shows the time and date there, both time zones with their UTC offsets, the time difference, the same moment in UTC and in 16 big cities. A time skipped or repeated by a clock change is explained in a note.

The formula

  • UTC time = local time − the UTC offset of the first place on that date
  • Time there = UTC time + the UTC offset of the second place at that moment
  • Time difference = offset of the second place − offset of the first place
  • Offsets and clock changes come from the IANA time zone database, as built into your browser
  • A skipped time (clocks jump forward) is read with the offset from before the jump; a repeated time (clocks go back) is taken the first time

A worked example

8:00 PM on Dec 3, 2026 in Karachi (UTC+05:00) is 3:00 PM UTC. New York is on Eastern Standard Time (UTC-05:00) that day, so it is 10:00 AM there, 10 hours behind. On Jul 9, 2026 New York keeps daylight time (UTC-04:00), and the same 8:00 PM in Karachi is 11:00 AM in New York, 9 hours behind.

Questions

How do I calculate the time zone difference?

Subtract the UTC offset of one place from the other on the date you need. Karachi is UTC+05:00 and New York UTC-05:00 in winter, a difference of 10 hours; in summer New York is UTC-04:00 and the gap is 9 hours.

What is the time zone of Pakistan?

Pakistan Standard Time (PKT), UTC+05:00, for the whole country. Pakistan has not used daylight saving time since 2009, so the offset is the same all year.

What is the time zone of India?

India Standard Time, UTC+05:30, half an hour ahead of Pakistan. 8:00 PM in Lahore is 8:30 PM in Delhi.

What is UTC?

Coordinated Universal Time, the world's reference time from which every time zone is measured. For everyday purposes it matches GMT; it never changes for daylight saving.

Is daylight saving time included?

Yes, for cities. Each city follows its own clock changes on the date you pick, so London is UTC+00:00 in January and UTC+01:00 in July, and Sydney is UTC+11:00 in January. Pick a fixed UTC offset instead if you want a number that never changes.

What if the time does not exist or happens twice?

On the night clocks jump forward, a time such as 2:30 AM in New York never happens; it is read as 3:30 AM daylight time. On the night clocks go back, 1:30 AM happens twice; the first one is used and the note tells you what the second one would give.

Why does the date change?

Places far apart can be on different days. When it is 8:00 PM on Thursday in Karachi, it is already midnight going into Friday in Tokyo and 2:00 AM on Friday in Sydney. The result always shows the date in the second place and says whether it is the next day or the day before.

How do I calculate time zones using longitude?

Divide the longitude by 15 to get the hours from Greenwich: east is ahead, west is behind. 75° E gives UTC+5, the same as Pakistan, but real zones follow borders, so check the actual zone.

How do I convert time zones in Excel?

Add or subtract the difference in hours divided by 24, for example =A1-10/24 for Karachi to New York in winter. Excel does not know daylight saving dates, so change the number when either place changes its clocks.

Which time format can I type?

Either 12-hour time such as 3:30 PM or 3.30pm, or 24-hour time such as 15:30, with or without seconds. You can also type noon or midnight. Under More options you can choose how the answers are shown.
