How it works

  1. The pots drop onto the bench, the watering can flies in and the 0% stamp lands on packet 404
  2. Hold the pointer over the bench to pour, or press a pot to water it; it sprouts, grows and blooms
  3. Each bloom hangs a tag that links to its page; get all four in bloom for a petal shower
  4. Type in Plant a seed and press Plant to grow your result in pot 404
  5. Water pot 404 before planting and a worm in a hat pops up to complain
  6. It plays a short demo by itself until you click or type

About

A full not-found page for a plant shop, set on a potting bench below the Margalla hills with a seed packet for Page Not Found staked in an empty pot. Hold to pour from the watering can and the other pots sprout, leaf out and bloom, each flower carrying a tag that links to a page. Plant a search and the old packet is pulled out, a seed drops in and the page you need grows in its place.
