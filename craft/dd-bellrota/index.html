How it works

  1. Pick a team at the top (or all teams) and open a page from the sidebar; on a phone use the menu button.
  2. On Lobby board, watch keys swing in and change colour as people clock in or take breaks; click a key to clock someone in or out, start a break or mark a no-show.
  3. On Rota, drag shifts between people and days (Alt copies, M moves with the keyboard), add shifts, then copy last week, fill open shifts or publish.
  4. On Attendance, filter on time, late, left early or no-show and click a row to fix the clock times.
  5. On Leave, approve or decline requests; balances update and you are told which shifts now need cover.
  6. On Payroll, adjust lines, approve and pay the run, open any payslip or download the bank file.

About

An eight-page app for hotels, restaurants, care homes and other teams that work in shifts, in plum, ivory and champagne. The lobby board hangs a key for everyone on today's rota; keys swing and turn gold as people clock in, amber when late and dashed red when missing. Plan the week by dragging shifts, fix clock times, approve leave and run payroll with a shared service charge and payslips.
