How it works

  1. On Living room, tap Lamp, Fan, Cooling, TV or Music and drag the Blinds and Lamp sliders: the drawing behind the glass changes with you
  2. Hover the dock at the bottom: icons grow like a magnifier; the active page slides a pill under them
  3. In Lights, dim each lamp, pick candle, warm, neutral or daylight, or switch every light at once
  4. Drag the thermostat dial in Climate, set the ceiling fan speed and watch it spin faster in the picture
  5. In Media, play, skip, shuffle, scrub the song and set the volume; switch the TV input and channels
  6. Lock the front door with the big lock, open the door camera, make a one-time gate code, and refresh the guest Wi-Fi
  7. Change the backdrop blur, hide the cat or reset the demo in Settings

About

A complete smart-home control app for one family flat, built around its living room. The room is drawn behind the frosted glass, softly blurred, and it answers back: the arc lamp glows warm or cool, the blinds roll down, the ceiling fan spins, the TV flickers and the air conditioner blows. A floating dock switches between eight pages. Good for a smart-home or home-automation product, with a milky light theme and a smoked dark theme.
