How it works

  1. Open any of the nine pages from the sidebar
  2. Switch the cash-flow chart between 7D, 30D and 12M and watch it morph
  3. Add, edit or delete transactions; balances, budgets and charts follow
  4. Click a budget ring to change its limit, and add money to goals
  5. Hover a card to tilt it, freeze it, show its number or create a virtual card
  6. Pay bills or toggle autopay, read insights, press Ctrl+K to jump, and reset demo data in Settings

About

A complete personal finance starter for banking apps, budgeting apps and money trackers, drawn in black with neon green and pink glows, plus a light theme. Nine working pages cover the dashboard, accounts, transactions, budgets, cards, goals, bills, insights and settings. Charts glow and morph, budget rings sweep in, and neon cards tilt in 3D as you move over them.

Use it in your site

Press Copy code or Download: it is one HTML file with its CSS and JavaScript inside and no outside files. Open it in a browser, or copy its parts into your page. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to the address to force a theme.
