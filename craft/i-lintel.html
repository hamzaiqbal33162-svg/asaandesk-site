How it works

  1. Open the Homes menu for buy, rent, new builds, commercial and towns, or press Ctrl+K to search homes, towns and articles
  2. On the home page pick a tab and filters: the mini map lights up the matching homes, then Show homes opens the listings
  3. Point at a card and its price pin rises on the map; click a pin for a preview, drag to pan, zoom with + and −, and tick Search as I move the map
  4. Open a home to browse the gallery full screen, hover each room on the floor plan, drag the mortgage sliders and book a viewing
  5. On Sell, answer three short steps and watch the guide price range move as you go
  6. Save homes with the heart, then add notes, compare up to three and manage alerts and viewings in your shortlist
  7. Switch the theme in the header; Reset demo data is in the footer

About

A seven-page website for a small estate and letting agency in a made-up river valley, set in warm limestone, charcoal and olive. Homes to buy and rent, new builds and commercial space sit on a hand-drawn map whose price pins light up with the cards. Each property page has a full-screen gallery, a floor plan you explore room by room and a mortgage slider, with pages for a live valuation, the agents, a journal and your shortlist.

Use it in your site

Press Download: you get a ZIP with one HTML file (its CSS and JavaScript inside) and an img folder with its 14 photos. Keep them side by side, open the HTML file in a browser, or copy its parts into your page and swap in your own photos. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to the address to force a theme.
