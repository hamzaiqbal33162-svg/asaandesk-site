How it works

  1. Pick a service, then press Next and watch the train drive to the next station
  2. Choose one of the next five days and a free arrival time (the full slot is crossed out)
  3. Type your name and an 11-digit mobile number starting with 03; mistakes shake and explain themselves
  4. Check the summary and total, press Book now for the ticket, and Back at any step to drive back
  5. Press Book another clean to rewind the train to the start

About

A home cleaning booking form in four steps: service, time, address and a final check. The progress bar is a drawn metro line: a two-car train speeds up, leans through the curves and brakes at each station while the line fills in behind it and passed stations get a tick. Booking sends the train to the end of the line, throws confetti and prints a ticket.
