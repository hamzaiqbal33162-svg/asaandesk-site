How it works

  1. Open a page from the navy side menu: Overview, Flows, Flow canvas, Templates, Runs, Connections, Shipments or Settings
  2. On the Flow canvas press Test run, switch the sample between a hold and a release notice, and drag the timeline to replay it
  3. Drag step cards to move them, use the + on a wire to add a step, click a card to edit or remove it, then publish
  4. Filter and search flows, star them, switch them on or off, duplicate, move or delete them, or start one from a template
  5. Retry or close failed runs, open a run for its step timings, and export runs as CSV
  6. Renew an expiring connection, connect a new app, and clear a customs hold from a shipment
  7. Press Ctrl+K to jump anywhere; change members, run policy and theme, or reset demo data, in Settings

About

An eight-page automation app for the operations desk of a sea and air freight forwarder. Flows read customs emails, chase missing documents, watch vessels and tell people what changed, and the overview shows the run tide, the run in flight, failed runs and cut-off countdowns. On the flow canvas you drag step cards around while the wires follow, and a test run sends glowing data packets along the wires step by step. A replay timeline lets you scrub back and forth while the side panel shows what each packet carried.

Use it in your site

Press Download: you get a ZIP with one HTML file (its CSS and JavaScript inside) and an img folder with its 5 photos. Keep them side by side, open the HTML file in a browser, or copy its parts into your page and swap in your own photos. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to the address to force a theme.
