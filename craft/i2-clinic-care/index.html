How it works

  1. Scroll the home page to clean the tooth step by step, then drag the line on the smile slider
  2. Open Book a visit: pick a treatment, a dentist and a day, tap a free time and confirm with your name and mobile number
  3. Add treatments to your plan on Services, change your insurance and watch what you pay count up
  4. Build an estimate on Prices, spread it over 3, 6 or 12 months and save it
  5. Filter the smile gallery, reviews and FAQ; write a review or ask a question
  6. Move or cancel a visit, print a receipt and add family members on My visits
  7. Press Ctrl+K to search treatments, dentists and answers; reset demo data from the footer

About

A ten-page website for a family dental clinic in Lahore, in clean white with aqua and soft coral. On the home page a drawn tooth loses its stains and starts to sparkle as you scroll through the five steps of a visit. Booking times flip like cards when you change day or dentist and keep changing live as other patients book and cancel; services with a treatment plan, a before and after smile gallery, insurance estimates, reviews, FAQ, directions and My visits all work.

Use it in your site

Press Download: you get a ZIP with one HTML file (its CSS and JavaScript inside) and an img folder with its 8 photos. Keep them side by side, open the HTML file in a browser, or copy its parts into your page and swap in your own photos. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to the address to force a theme.
