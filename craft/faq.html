CHILL & CRAFT · FAQ

Questions and answers

How to use the components and templates, what you may do with them, and what they work with.

What is Chill & Craft?

A library of animated UI components (buttons, code fields, sliders, upload boxes, tab bars, cards, checkout buttons), full page templates (sign-in, landing, shop, 404, greetings and small games) and complete admin dashboards (CRM, shop, finance, SaaS and Pakistani business apps) made by AsaanDesk.

Is it free?

Yes, everything is free for now. Each item shows its regular price for later; anything you copy or download now stays yours to use.

Can I use them in client or commercial projects?

Yes. Use them in personal and commercial websites and apps, change them as you like. Please do not resell or share the files as your own template pack.

How do I use one?

Open an item, press Copy code (or Download) and you get one HTML file with its CSS and JavaScript inside. Open it in a browser to see it, then move the HTML, the <style> and the <script> parts into your page. Studio items with photos download as a ZIP: keep the img folder next to the HTML file, or swap in your own photos.

Do they need React, Tailwind or a library?

No. Every item is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no outside files, fonts or images, so it works anywhere: plain sites, WordPress, React or Vue.

How do I change the colours?

Each file keeps its colours in CSS variables near the top of the <style> block. Change them there. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to the address to force a theme.

Do they work on phones?

Yes. Every item is tested at phone and desktop widths, works with touch and keyboard, and calms its motion when the device asks for reduced motion.

Which browsers are supported?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and phone.

Will there be more?

Yes. New items appear on the New page first.
