How it works

  1. Open a page from the sidebar: Today, Counter, Credit, Stock, Cash-up, Expenses, Reports, Staff or Settings
  2. Watch the till on Today: each cash sale drops notes in and takes change out
  3. On the Counter, scan or type a code or name (try 2x eggs), pick how they paid and complete the bill
  4. On Credit, pick a customer, add credit or a payment and watch the bar move, then send a reminder
  5. Put low items on the supplier order sheet, send it, and mark the order received to restock
  6. Count the notes at the Cash-up, check the difference and close the day
  7. Press F2 for a new bill or Ctrl+K to jump anywhere; reset the demo data in Settings

About

A nine-page desk for Barkat General Store, a made-up corner shop in Lahore. Notes land in a live till (cash drawer) as bills ring up, and the credit page shows each customer's balance as a bar that grows with credit and shrinks with payments, next to a WhatsApp-style reminder preview. Counter, stock and supplier orders, cash-up, expenses, reports, staff and settings all work and keep their data in the browser.

Use it in your site
