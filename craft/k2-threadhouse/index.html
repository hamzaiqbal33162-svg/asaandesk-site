Threadhouse

How it works

  1. Open a page from the top bar: New in, Shop, Collections, Fit studio, Track order, your wishlist or the bag
  2. Tap a colourway swatch, or the dots under a piece, to dye its photo; hover a product photo to see the weave
  3. On a product page choose unstitched, a standard size or stitching to your measurements and watch the price and dates change
  4. In the fit studio type your measurements: the figure changes shape and the line for each field is drawn where the tape goes
  5. Filter the shop by category, price, colour, stitching and fabric, then check out in PKR with cash on delivery or the demo card form
  6. Follow an order on the tracking page while the workroom updates it live
  7. Press Ctrl+K to search everything; reset demo data in Account

About

A multi-page shop for a Lahore boutique selling lawn 3-piece suits, embroidered shirts, sarees and dupattas, in ivory, rose and deep teal. Colourway swatches show the real cloth and dye the product photo with a wipe, like a bolt of fabric unrolling, and a hover lens shows the weave up close. The fit studio has a drawn figure that takes your shape as you type and draws each measuring line, and every piece can be bought unstitched or stitched to those measurements, then followed through the workroom on the tracking page.

Use it in your site

Press Download: you get a ZIP with one HTML file (its CSS and JavaScript inside) and an img folder with its 14 photos. Keep them side by side, open the HTML file in a browser, or copy its parts into your page and swap in your own photos. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to the address to force a theme.
