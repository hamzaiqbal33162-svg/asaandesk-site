How it works

  1. Open a page from the glass sidebar: Home, Rooms, Devices, Climate, Energy, Security, Scenes or Settings
  2. Tap a room on the floor plan, switch floors, then tap a pin to turn that device on or off
  3. Run a scene such as Wind down or Film club and watch the lights, pins and tiles change
  4. Drag round the heating dial, or use the arrow keys on it, to set each floor's temperature
  5. Watch the energy flow, switch the energy chart between 24 hours, 7 and 30 days, and export a CSV
  6. Open a camera, filter the clips, lock every door and set the alarm on the Security page
  7. Press Ctrl+K to search and jump, and reset the demo data in Settings

About

An eight-page smart home app for one family house, drawn as walnut and olive glass with brass light at dusk, or linen and sage glass by day, over a blurred photo of the house. Tap a room on the floor plan and its lights, blinds, speakers and locks pop up as pins you can switch, while lit rooms glow. A live energy flow sends dots from the roof, the battery and the grid into the house, and the cameras, clips, locks, alarm, heating dial, scenes and rules all work.
