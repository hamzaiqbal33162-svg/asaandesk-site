How it works

  1. Open a page from the top bar: Browse, Staff picks, Pre-orders, Reading club, Gift cards, the wishlist, your account or the bag
  2. Hover a spine on the home shelf, or switch Browse to Shelf view, and the book slides off into the hand; on a phone, tap a spine
  3. Filter by genre, language, translation, format and price, open a book, pick an edition and press Look inside to turn the sample pages
  4. Reserve pre-orders, save a seat and log your page in the reading club, and design a gift card with a live preview
  5. In the bag, switch on gift wrap and watch the paper fold round the book; choose the paper, the ribbon and the tag
  6. Check out with cash on delivery, bank transfer or the demo card, then follow the parcel on the track page
  7. Press Ctrl+K to search everything; reset demo data in Account settings

About

A multi-page shop for an independent bookshop in Islamabad, in paper, ink blue and warm red. Books stand on a drawn shelf: hover a spine and the book slides off, turns from spine to cover in mid-air and lands in a drawn hand. Every book opens to a look inside sample with page turns, and the bag wraps the first book in the paper and ribbon you choose, fold by fold. There are staff picks, pre-orders with countdowns, a reading club with progress and a vote, gift cards, checkout in PKR and order tracking.

Use it in your site

Press Download: you get a ZIP with one HTML file (its CSS and JavaScript inside) and an img folder with its 10 photos. Keep them side by side, open the HTML file in a browser, or copy its parts into your page and swap in your own photos. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to the address to force a theme.
