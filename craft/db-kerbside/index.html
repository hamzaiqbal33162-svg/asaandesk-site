How it works

  1. Use the All channels, Online or Stores switch in the top bar to rescope the overview numbers, charts and flow.
  2. Hover a ribbon in the flow chart to see how much a channel sold in a category; switch 7D, 30D or 12M to redraw it.
  3. Open an online order to move it from picking to shipped, or to ready to collect at a chosen store.
  4. Drag returns across the board from requested to refunded, or use each card's Move menu.
  5. Click any number on Inventory to count or adjust stock, and transfer stock between the warehouse and stores.
  6. Plan promotions on the seven-week timeline, pick a store on the drawn map, and preview or download reports as CSV.
  7. Collapse the sidebar to icons, switch the theme, or reset the demo data in Settings.

About

A dense, thirteen-page back office for a clothing brand with a web shop, an app and four stores. A channel filter in the top bar rescopes the overview, sales flow from each channel into each category as animated ribbons, and new orders and store receipts arrive live. Good for a retail brand, a home goods shop or anyone running online and in-store sales together.
